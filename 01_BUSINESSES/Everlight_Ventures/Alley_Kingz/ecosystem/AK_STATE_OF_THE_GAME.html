01 / What it isThe pitch, in one paragraph

A live-action street game about upright, jacked gangster dogs who run a city block by block. The Warriors and the Ninja Turtles by way of 80s Homie figures, driving Twisted Metal war rigs. It is a tower battler with an MMO wrapped around it: a walkable hub, raidable player bases, crews, a card economy, and a 106-book comic saga underneath. It is not a kids game.

04 / The DocksA third of your roster has nowhere to stand

The largest gap in the game

The fiction assigns all 106 dogs to eight districts. The game ships six playable zones. THE DOCKS holds 34 dogs, the single biggest district in the saga, and players cannot go there. THE UNDERCITY holds 4 more. A boss is already written for it: THE DOCK SOVEREIGN. The names appear in buildmode.js but not as roamable turf.

The decision this forces

The deck is 11 cards, and the canon says you assign them to buildings "according to their TRAITS + FACTIONS." So a mono-crew deck needs 11 cards in that crew. Five of the eight districts cannot field one. Either rebalance the roster toward ~13 dogs per district, or accept that crews operate across turf and faction is culture rather than a deck filter. Pick one deliberately. Right now it is neither.

06 / Is it finished?The honest answer

What is genuinely done

The writing, the card data, the combat feel (screen shake, hitstop, hit flash, floating damage all real and good), the PVP loop (real players raid real bases through a server-authoritative edge function), the economy math, and 2D art at depth. This is not a shell. It is a real game with a real spine.

What is broken, ranked by what it costs you

1. Cosmetics grant with no charge, and an unauthenticated shop is live on the old project. You cannot sell skins today. 2. 34 dogs have no home. 3. Five chest tiers share one animation, so a 20x reward feels identical to the smallest. 4. 105 of 106 cards share 7 generic FX clips, which is why the roster reads generic. 5. The rig layer is a stub. 6. Zero 3D.

So: is it finished? No. But the unfinished parts are not the hard parts. You have the novel, the engine, the balance and the art. What is missing is the last mile of every system: the chest that never learned it had tiers, the shop that never learned to charge, the district that never got built, the rigs that never got names.

The pattern is the same everywhere: someone scaffolded the archetype, shipped it, and never came back to fill it in.

07 / Where the work goesIn order

  1. Fix the ledger. Charge for cosmetics, bind identity to the JWT, kill the zombie endpoint. Three small edits. Without them every other item on this list earns zero.
  2. Wire the juice you already own. Tap-gated card reveals with real art, rarity-gated audio, per-tier chests. No Unity, no credits, no art. Pure wiring.
  3. Build The Docks and The Undercity. 38 dogs come home and a written boss becomes fightable.
  4. Author the 8 crews and the 20 rigs. Writing, not spending.
  5. Generate 106 full-body refs free on FLUX, then mesh through Tripo.
  6. Port to Unity. ~2,530 draw sites across 26 modules. engine.js survives at 87%.
The one-line answer to "where are we at"

You are one finished novel, one working engine, and one broken cash register away from a real game. The ideas are done. The wiring is not.
