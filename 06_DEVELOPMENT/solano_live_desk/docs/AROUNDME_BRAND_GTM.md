# AroundMe: Brand, Go-To-Market + IP Protection

An Everlight Ventures product. Strategy captured 2026-07-12.

## Everlight baseline (the permanent root, always assume it)
Everlight Ventures solves multiple logistics problems with the least resources,
minimalist, gives back, follows trends/cycles and monetizes them efficiently,
grounded in morality, and is aggressive + risk-taking yet safe + protective at the
same time. AroundMe must mentally align with this even before it visibly says
"Everlight." Brand personality = aggressive intelligence, protective purpose. Lead
with protective; back it with aggressive data.

## Positioning
Not a navigation app; a civilian situational-awareness layer that happens to
navigate. The hooks nobody else has, lead with these:
- "Warns you about the shoulder-driver doing 90 before he reaches you."
- "Tells your street you're a delivery driver, not a prowler."
- "Routes you out the clear back road when everyone else is stuck in one line."

## Virality / go-to-market (free-first)
1. Shareable near-miss loop. One-tap "share what just happened" that mints a clean
   map clip ("AroundMe warned me 90 seconds before this"). Every big moment becomes
   content. This is the growth engine. Mirrors the AK viral-clip model.
2. Own ONE vertical in ONE metro first. Gig drivers (DoorDash/Uber/Instacart).
   Get into r/doordash, r/couriersofreddit, and local driver Facebook groups. 100
   loud drivers beat 10,000 silent installs. Drivers evangelize tools that keep
   them safe and paid.
3. Market the differentiators (reckless-driver alert, delivery self-marker,
   dispersed-egress routing), not the map. The map is table stakes; the hooks are
   the story.
4. Referral baked into safety: "add a guardian / share your live trip" recruits the
   person on the other end every time it is used.
5. Cadence: short vertical video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) of a real alert firing beats
   any paid ad. "Watch AroundMe catch this before Waze."

## Protect the IP + the name (do this BEFORE going public)
1. Trademark, not patent, protects the name/logo. File a USPTO intent-to-use
   application (form 1-B) for "AroundMe" in Class 9 (software) + Class 42 (SaaS).
   ~$250-350 per class. Locks your priority date from filing day, even pre-launch.
   This is the single most important pre-launch move.
2. Run a FREE USPTO TESS knockout search first ("AroundMe" is a common phrase, so
   expect possible conflicts, which is another reason a distinctive logo + tagline
   matter).
3. File UNDER Everlight Ventures, not a personal name, so the mark ladders to the
   parent brand.
4. The IDEA is not protectable; the execution + brand are. Copyright on the code is
   automatic the moment it is written (keep the repo private). Protect the name
   (trademark), the brand assets, and any genuinely novel method.
5. Provisional patent (~$65-150) ONLY for the one truly novel algorithm, the
   coordinated evacuation dispersal / Dispersed Egress routing. Gives 12 months of
   "patent pending" priority. Do NOT try to patent the whole app.
6. Sequence, and it matters: trademark filed + domains/handles locked + repo
   private + a "AroundMe and the AroundMe logo are trademarks of Everlight
   Ventures" site footer, THEN go public. Press day is copycat day; the fence goes
   up first. Keep the novel methods (dispersal routing, the self-marking trust
   model) out of detailed public posts until the provisional is filed. Market the
   benefit, not the method.
