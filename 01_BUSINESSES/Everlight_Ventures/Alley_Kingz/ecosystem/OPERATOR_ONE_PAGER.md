# WAVE 7 -- OPERATOR ONE-PAGER (Rich, 2-minute read)

## WHAT THE BALANCE AUDIT FOUND
Verdict: the original 48 cards are clean and need ZERO changes. The problems
are all in the 58 Heavy/Street variants:
- Street commons are the strongest cards per energy in the game. Worst:
  Nitro + Spike (cost 2, flying, 35 cards can never hit them), Knuckles +
  Flatline (highest DPS in the game at cost 5), Switchblade/Carrier/Hotwire.
- Tombstone, Anvil, Slab break the 2850 HP ceiling and out-tank $BCARDD,
  your flagship Mythic, at the same cost. That kills the chase-card premium.
- Heavy structures (Emplacement, Flakwall) are the weakest slots in the game.
Fix is ONE tuning pass: trim Street damage on cheap cards, enforce the HP cap
on everyone, small relief for Heavy structures. Roughly 12 cards touched.

## THE 3 BIGGEST DESIGN DECISIONS (and why)
1. LOOT IS 100% AUTO-MAGNET, BANKED AT THE GATE. Kills drop real shrunken art
   tokens; your dogs scoop what they walk near; the district gate banks it
   (die before the gate = lose half the commons, never the rare stuff). Why:
   one-thumb game -- tapping loot would fight card placement, and research
   says the pickup moment, not the payout, is the dopamine hit.
2. MYTHICS WIN ON UTILITY, NOT RAW STATS. Rarity stays as is; we enforce one
   stat ceiling on every card so nothing invades $BCARDD's lane. Why: cheaper
   than rebuilding the whole curve, and it protects the king.
3. CLASSES SHIP AS A DATA LAYER, NOT A REWRITE. All 106 cards get a class,
   structures split into 5 real archetypes (ramp beam, turret, lockdown,
   nest, pylon), and ONE set of match counters feeds loot, quests, nemesis,
   and rap sheets at once. Built once, used everywhere -- everything blends.
