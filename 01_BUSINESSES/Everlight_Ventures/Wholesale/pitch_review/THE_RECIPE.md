## THE FORMULA (Piper first-touch -- the only cold email)
Order matters. Each beat has a source.

1. **Warm, human open** -- Piper's voice, names them + the property. NO number yet. (character)
2. **Accusation Audit** (Voss, SALES_LENS #1): name their suspicion before they feel it --
   "you're probably thinking this is the kind of letter that shows up right before someone
   tries to underpay you -- I'd think that too." Earns trust by demonstrating, not claiming.
3. **Compassionate WHY** (psych + operator): graceful, no tax-shaming, no internal dates.
   "I work with folks around {area} carrying a place that's become more weight than it's worth."
4. **PAS - Problem -> Agitate** (MARKETING_LENS): name the problem, then make the cost of
   inaction REAL with carrying-cost math -- "between taxes + insurance that's ~${carry}/mo, and
   a traditional listing sits ~90 days, that's ~${carry*3} in hold costs before a dime of
   commission or repairs." Ethical FOMO from real math, not fake deadlines.
5. **SPIN Implication question** (SALES_LENS #2): let THEM compute it -- "what's it costing you
   a year, all in?" Sellers who do their own math are 3x more committed.
6. **ANCHOR REPLACEMENT then THE NUMBER** (the hinge, all lenses + operator "show the number"):
   - Traditional path: list ~${arv} retail, minus ~6% commission, minus repairs to make it
     listable, minus 90-day carry, minus the back taxes out of proceeds = realistic net ~${trad_net}
     -- and only IF it sells.
   - Our path: **${offer_low}-${offer_high} cash**, as-is, 7-day close, back taxes handled, $0 out
     of pocket. Now the cash number reads as the rational, lower-stress choice -- not a lowball.
7. **Area economic data** (operator + Zillow research ZHVI, real per-ZIP): one true sentence of
   local context ("homes around {zip} are moving at {zhvi}, but as-is/distressed trade lower").
8. **Tenure as RESPECT** (psych, when years_owned high): "22 years is a long time to steward a
   place -- you've earned a straight answer, not a runaround."
9. **Social proof** (MARKETING_LENS #2): one anonymous local line -- "closed one a couple
   streets over last month, owner had cash in 6 days."
10. **Pride-of-return / future-state** (operator FOMO+pride): paint the clean exit + what the
    cash unlocks for THEM (out from under, fresh start, on your terms).
11. **Low-friction single CTA** + **consult-counsel line in EVERY email** (SALES_LENS ethics):
    "reply and I'll send the real number + a plain-English summary; talk to your family or an
    attorney first, always."
12. **Reframed AI footer** (operator's positive intent, not the reply-suppressing version):
    confident "real company, real people, AI helps us reach folks fairly" -- not "a machine is
    after your house."

## The ethical line (SALES_LENS -- binding)
Legitimate = remove friction (shame, overwhelm, distrust, process confusion) + show real math +
honor the 4-round cap + encourage counsel/family. Predatory = fabricated urgency, manufactured
scarcity, discouraging counsel, exploiting decline, withholding material facts. "Seamless for us"
= remove friction, NEVER break resistance through pressure/repetition.
