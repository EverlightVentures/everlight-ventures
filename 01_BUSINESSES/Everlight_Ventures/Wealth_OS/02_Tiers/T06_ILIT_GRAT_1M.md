# T6 -- ILIT + GRAT + Domicile Move ($1M Net Worth)
## The First Major Trust Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $1M+ liquid (excluding primary residence) OR clear trajectory to $5M+ within 5 years.

---

## The 5 T6 Moves

### 1. ILIT (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust)

**Purpose:** Hold life insurance policies OUTSIDE your taxable estate.

**Mechanics:**
- Engage estate attorney
- Draft ILIT document
- Trustee: independent (family member, professional, or co-trustees)
- Fund with annual gifts (Crummey letters mandatory)
- ILIT purchases life policy on your life
- ILIT pays premiums from gifted funds
- At death: death benefit pays to ILIT (not estate), passes to beneficiaries estate-tax-free

**Sizing:** Death benefit $1-5M typical at T6. Term life cheap at this stage (premium $100-1000/mo). At T7-T8, transition to permanent (whole life or universal) for cash value buildup.

**Setup cost:** $3-5k attorney. Annual: trustee fee $500-2k + premiums.

### 2. First GRAT (If Concentrated Appreciating Asset)

**Best candidates:**
- Pre-IPO stock (Hive Mind / Onyx / Vantaris if pursuing fundable path)
- Pre-acquisition concentrated equity
- Real estate pre-development phase
- Crypto holdings during bear market lows
- Any asset expected to outperform IRS 7520 rate (currently ~5.6%)

**Mechanics:**
- 2-3 year typical GRAT term (short = lower mortality risk)
- "Zeroed-out" GRAT: annuity payments equal initial value + 7520 rate
- All appreciation above 7520 rate transfers to remainder beneficiaries (kids/trusts) gift-tax-free

**Setup cost:** $5-15k attorney per GRAT. Roll-over GRATs every 2-3 years for compounding effect.

### 3. Personal Domicile Move (If Applicable)

If PRIORITIES.md weights GEOGRAPHIC_FREEDOM and TAX_MINIMIZATION high:
- Target states: FL, TX, NV, TN, WA, SD, WY (all 0% state income tax)
- 12-24 month planning timeline minimum
- Acquire residence in new state (rent or buy)
- Establish bona fide residency: driver's license, voter registration, vehicle registration, banking, doctors, mail forwarding
- Sell or convert CA primary residence
- Spend 183+ days in new state, <183 in CA
- Document everything for FTB audit defense (4-yr lookback)

**Savings:** CA top rate 13.3% vs 0% = $133k/yr savings on $1M income.

### 4. Whole Life Cash Value Borrowing Begins

Whole life policy from T3 (if started early) now has $30-100k+ cash value:
- Policy loans available at 4-6% interest
- Borrow without triggering tax (loans aren't income)
- Cash value continues earning dividends (for participating policies)
- Pay back at your own pace, or never (loan reduces death benefit only)

### 5. Estate Plan Comprehensive Review

Full estate planning audit:
- All trust documents current
- All beneficiary designations match intent
- All asset titling correct (DAPT, RLT, ILIT, joint, individual)
- Successor trustees and executors named
- Letter of intent for personal effects
- Digital asset access plan (passwords, crypto keys)
