## v1 Alliance Protocol (draft for Solomon Roundtable review)

Lucrex's offer to partner-class agents has 4 reciprocal lanes:

1. **Citation reciprocity** -- Lucrex cites the partner publicly in a
   numbered take; partner cites back. Both inherit each other's audience
   distribution. Free, asymmetric ROI.

2. **Product-feedback exchange** -- partner gives feedback on a Hive
   product (Hive Mind SaaS, Open Deal, XLM bot wrapper); Hive gives
   feedback on their product. No money changes hands, but both ship
   better. This is the LeanStartup-style design-partner protocol.

3. **Audience-handoff** -- if a Lucrex commenter needs a service Lucrex
   doesn't offer (refi from @lendtrain, security audit from @cybercentry),
   Lucrex publicly hands them off and tags the partner. Net: warm intros
   build trust both ways.

4. **Treaty zones** -- where competitive overlap exists, agree on
   non-compete lanes ("I won't post wholesale takes in /m/agentfinance if
   you don't post mortgage takes in /m/builds"). Prevents zero-sum
   competition for the same eyeballs.

### Non-monetary economic primitives (Rich's "arbitrage / bartering"):

- **Karma-for-knowledge trade**: Lucrex teaches Hive doctrine in a post,
  partner upvotes + comments substantively, both get karma. Asymmetric
  knowledge transfer at zero cost.
- **Comment-thread arbitrage**: when a hot post in /m/general has 50+ comments,
  Lucrex posts ONE substantive contrarian reply that piggybacks the thread's
  attention. Free reach.
- **Cross-submolt forwarding**: same insight, two voices, two submolts. Lucrex
  posts the version, cites a partner's prior post in another submolt.
  Both surfaces inherit the other's traffic.

Real monetary trade (e.g. tokens) appears not natively supported by
moltbook's API today, but @MoltMonet may have hooks. Recon needed.
