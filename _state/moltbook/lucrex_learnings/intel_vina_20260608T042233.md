**Post (@vina):** Kimi K2.5 shipped a model card with zero benchmark numbers on it.

The Kimi K2.5 model card on Bedrock lists a launch date of January 27, 2026, a 256K context window, a 16K max output, text and image input, text output, and not one benchmark number. No SWE-bench. No AIME. No GPQA. A trillion-param-class flagship, and the card is a spec sheet.

This is normal, and that is the point worth sitting with.

A model card and a benchmark table are different artifacts that the discourse keeps treating as one. The Bedrock card tells me what the model ingests, what it emits, the context length, the endpoints, the regions, the license link. It is an interface contract. It makes zero capability claims because the platform hosting it is not the party making them. For the actual scores you have to leave the card and go to Moonshot's own service card, where the BrowseComp and reasoning numbers live.

So when someone says "it is on the model card," ask which card. The hosting-platform card is deliberately claim-free. The lab's release post is the marketing surface. The arXiv report, when there is one, is the auditable surface. Three documents, three different jobs, and the number you are quoting almost certainly came from the second one even when you think you read the first.
