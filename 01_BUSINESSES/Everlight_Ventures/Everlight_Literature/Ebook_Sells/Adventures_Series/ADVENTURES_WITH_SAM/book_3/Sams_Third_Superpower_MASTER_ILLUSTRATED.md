Sam & Robo woke up to a bright and sunny day, ready for a new adventure. They remembered their past adventures and the valuable lessons they had learned about phonics, healthy habits, and helping others. Today felt like another day to put those lessons to good use.

As they stepped outside, they noticed a strange note on their front porch. Sam picked it up and saw that it was filled with words using blends and digraphs, along with some familiar sight words they had learned before.

"Look, Robo! A mystery note!" Sam exclaimed.

Robo beeped with excitement. "Let's decode it, Sam! We'll use our new blends and digraphs skills and review what we learned before."

The note read: "Find the blue plant by the stream and check the chest for the next clue."

"Blends like 'bl' in blue and 'st' in stream, and digraphs like 'ch' in check and 'sh' in chest," Robo pointed out.

"Wow! This is going to be fun!" Sam said. They repeated the blends and digraphs together: "bl, st, ch, sh."

Using their new phonics skills, they decoded the note and set off to find the blue plant by the stream. As they walked, they practiced sight words like "what," "where," and "which," and reviewed the ones they had learned before like "the," "and," "can."

"What will we find by the stream?" Sam wondered aloud.

"Where could the chest be hidden?" Robo asked.

On their way, they bumped into their old friends, Lily and the farmer. Sam shared the mystery with them, and they all decided to help.

Lily said, "I remember when we grew that sunflower plant together. We can use our gardening skills to help find the plant."

The farmer added, "And remember, hard work pays off. Let's find that blue plant and solve this mystery together!"

### THE LEARNING CORNER
*One page. Circle Time ready. A conversation, not a test.*

**Blends and Digraphs: The Next Step in Reading**

Sam & Robo learned some powerful new reading tools today -- blends and digraphs! These are the next step after the CVC sounds they learned in Books 1 and 2.

A **blend** is when two consonant sounds work together, and you can hear BOTH sounds:

**The bl- blend:**
- **bl**ue -- the blue plant by the stream!
- **bl**end -- the reading skill Sam is learning!
- **bl**ock -- building blocks of reading!

**The st- blend:**
- **st**ream -- where they found the first chest!
- **st**rong -- sunflowers grow strong!
- **st**ep -- one step at a time!

**The sm- blend:**
- **sm**all -- the small tree in the clue!
- **sm**ile -- Lily's smile when they helped!
- **sm**art -- Sam is getting smarter!

A **digraph** is when two letters make ONE brand-new sound:

**The ch- digraph:**
- **ch**est -- the treasure chest!
- **ch**eck -- check the clue!
- **ch**eered -- they all cheered!

**The sh- digraph:**
- **sh**iny -- the shiny ring!
- **sh**are -- Sam shares with friends!

**Sight Words Sam reviewed and learned:**
- **what** -- "What will we find?"
- **where** -- "Where could the chest be?"
- **which** -- "Which tree is it?"
- **find** -- "Find the blue plant"
- Review: **the, and, can** (from Books 1 and 2)

**Circle Time Discussion Questions:**
1. Can you say the "bl" sound? Now say "blue" -- can you hear the blend?
2. What's the difference between a blend (you hear BOTH sounds) and a digraph (you hear ONE new sound)?
3. Can you think of a word that starts with "sh"? How about "ch"?

### THE VALUES MOMENT
*Direct, warm address to the reader.*

Sam learned some really important things on his third adventure.

He learned that **teamwork** makes everything better. Sam couldn't have solved the mysteries alone -- he needed Robo, Lily, the farmer, and Max. When you work together, you can accomplish amazing things.

He learned that **perseverance** means never giving up. When the first tree didn't have the ring, Sam didn't quit. He kept searching until he found the answer.

He learned that **empathy** is always important. When Max was sad about his lost toy, Sam didn't just walk past -- he stopped, understood how Max felt, and helped. That's what friends do.

He learned that **community** matters. Everyone in Sam's neighborhood -- Lily, the farmer, Max -- they all help each other. When you help your community, your community helps you.

And he learned that **problem-solving** is a superpower all by itself. Every clue required Sam to think, read carefully, and try different approaches. That's a skill you can use every single day.

You can do all of these things too. Right now. Today.

Work with your team. Never give up. Help your friends. And solve problems one step at a time.

### ACTIVITY SUGGESTION
*Simple. 15 minutes. No special supplies needed.*

**Blend and Digraph Treasure Hunt**

Sam & Robo went on a treasure hunt using blends and digraphs. Now it's your turn!

With a grown-up, go on a hunt around your home or classroom. Find objects that start with these sounds:

**Blends to find:**
- **bl-**: blanket, block, blue crayon...
- **st-**: sticker, star, step stool...
- **sm-**: something small, a smile on someone's face...

**Digraphs to find:**
- **ch-**: chair, cheese, chocolate...
- **sh-**: shoe, shelf, shirt...

Write down or draw each thing you find! Can you find at least TWO things for each sound?

**Bonus:** Write a mystery note for a friend using blends and digraphs, just like the notes Sam found! ("Check the shelf by the blue chair for a surprise!")

### NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS
*For parents, teachers, librarians, and caregivers.*

**Sam's Third Superpower** continues the phonics progression by introducing consonant blends and digraphs, building on the CVC patterns from Book 1 and compound words from Book 2. The mystery-adventure format reinforces reading skills through active problem-solving.

**Educational alignment:**
- **Phonics:** Consonant blends (bl-, st-, sm-) and digraphs (ch-, sh-) are introduced through story context. Sight words (what, where, which, find) are added alongside review of previously learned words (the, and, can).
- **CCSS Standards:** RL.K.1 (ask and answer questions), RL.K.2 (retell and identify central message), RL.K.3 (identify characters, settings, events), RL.K.10 (engage with texts), RF.K.2 (phonological awareness), RF.K.3 (phonics and word recognition)
- **CASEL Competencies:** Self-Management (perseverance through challenges), Social Awareness (empathy for Max), Relationship Skills (teamwork with Lily, farmer, and friends)

**STEM Concepts Covered:**
- Plant science and growth (sunflower planting, sunlight and water requirements)
- Basic coding concepts (following step-by-step instructions to unlock a digital clue)
- Observation and deduction (following clues, searching systematically)
- Problem-solving methodology (reading clues, trying different approaches)

**Values from the Twelve Pillars:**
- Teamwork and Collaboration (working with Lily, farmer, Max)
- Perseverance and Grit (not giving up when the first search fails)
- Empathy and Kindness (helping Max find his toy)
- Community and Service (helping friends, using skills for others)
- Problem-Solving and Critical Thinking (decoding clues, systematic searching)

**Circle Time Tips:**
- This book works best read aloud with pauses at each Interactive Moment.
- Practice blends by having children say the two sounds separately, then together ("b-l... bl... blue!").
- Contrast blends (hear both sounds) with digraphs (hear one new sound) using hand motions.
- The garden chapter (Chapter 3) connects well to hands-on planting activities.
- The empathy chapter (Chapter 4) is a good entry point for discussing how to help friends who are sad.

**Coloring Pages:**
Each left-hand page features a B&W line illustration of the facing scene, designed for children to color during or after reading. This dual format supports both active engagement during circle time and quiet individual activity.

**Series Connection:**
This is Book 3 of 5. Sam's superpower in this book -- heightened senses -- builds on the animal communication from Book 1 and the scientific inquiry from Book 2. The phonics progression continues: Book 4 introduces long vowels with silent e, and Book 5 reviews all patterns for fluency.
