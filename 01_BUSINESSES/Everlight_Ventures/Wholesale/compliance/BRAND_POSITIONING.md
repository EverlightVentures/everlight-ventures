# Brand Positioning: The Everlight Promise (CarMax of Wholesale)

**What we are:** The friendly, reputable, fair-deal wholesaler. When someone hears "Everlight," they think CarMax for distressed homes. No haggling games, no high-pressure, no tricks. A fast, honest, written offer, and they walk away any time.

**What we are NOT:** Aggressive, pushy, late-night-call, pressure-close wholesalers. That is the stereotype we compete against. Our edge is trust at scale.

## Brand Principles

### 1. Transparent Pricing (show the math)
Every offer to a seller includes a plain-language breakdown:
- Estimated market value (range, sourced from public comps)
- Our purchase price
- Assignment fee (stated, not hidden)
- Estimated closing costs covered by us

No "up to" ranges, no bait numbers, no surprises.

### 2. No-Haggle Default
The first offer we send is our best offer. If the seller counters, we may nibble once (a la the Straight Line method), but we never open with a lowball expecting them to counter. CarMax built a $30B business on this.

### 3. Walk-Away Clause
Every contract has a 7-day Quality Assurance Review Period clause. Seller can walk away in the first 7 days with no penalty, no earnest money forfeit, no lawyer. This is in the contract, not hidden in fine print.

### 4. Response-Time Commitment
- Email: respond within 4 business hours on weekdays
- Inbound SMS: respond within 1 hour during call-legal hours
- Inbound call: Harrison answers or calls back same-day
- Contract-to-close: 14-21 days promised, tracked, reported

Every response delay against this commitment is logged and flagged in the weekly review. Missed SLAs on three deals = stand-down on new outreach until root-caused.

### 5. Post-Deal Feedback Loop
Every closed deal ends with a seller-satisfaction survey (3 questions, 2 minutes):
- Did Everlight treat you fairly?
- Would you refer a friend to us?
- What could we have done better?

Scores aggregated on `#compliance` dashboard. Published (anonymized) in the company quarterly report. Sellers become our best marketing.

### 6. Public Physical Office
- Real Everlight Ventures LLC mailing address in every email footer (CA domicile office)
- Phone number answered by a human during business hours
- No PO box, no virtual suite, no "contact us through our website only"

### 7. Identity In Every Touchpoint
- Rich's name (or Piper / Harrison's on behalf) on every email, not "The Everlight Team"
- Role title stated (Acquisitions / Closing / Compliance)
- Photo on the email signature (trust-builder -- humans respond to faces)

### 8. Professional-Grade Collateral
- Branded HTML email templates with the gold-crown design system
- PDF deal one-pagers (not crappy Word docs)
- Plain-English contract language, with a one-page summary cover
- Voicemail greeting that names Everlight Ventures and states call-back SLA

## What We Explicitly Do NOT Do

- **No cold SMS** (reputationally risky + legally gated). Sellers text us when they want to.
- **No robocalls / AI voice** to cold leads. Every outbound call is a human.
- **No yard signs at the property** (illegal in many states + trashy).
- **No misleading subject lines** ("Urgent regarding your property" = NO).
- **No pretending to represent the lender** or bank (foreclosure-rescue statute violation).
- **No taking title before escrow closes** (equity-skimming risk).
- **No buying from sellers under duress without cooling-off** (CA/FL foreclosure consultant statutes).

## Compliance Is A Feature, Not A Cost

Every item in `STATE_COMPLIANCE_MATRIX.md` and `CHANNEL_STRATEGY.md` is a BRAND asset. The disclosure language in every contract is the receipt a future buyer shows their friends: "these guys were straight with me."

CarMax built the "7-day return" into the product. We build the "7-day QA Review Period" into the contract. Same principle, same brand halo.

## Measurable Brand Health

Quarterly scorecard (Chart Dawson owns):
- **Trust NPS** from post-deal survey. Target: 70+.
- **Complaint rate** to BBB / AG / CFPB. Target: 0 per quarter.
- **Referral-sourced deals** as % of pipeline. Target: 30% by year 2.
- **Response-time SLA adherence**. Target: 95% on-time.
- **Repeat-buyer rate** (cash buyers who do 2+ deals with us). Target: 50% by year 2.

## Agent Voice Alignment

Every outbound message must sound like the CarMax salesperson, not the late-night wholesaler guru:
- Warm, plain, direct
- No urgency manufactured out of thin air
- No "I have buyers lined up" unless we actually do
- No promises we can't keep
- Named human sender, not a blasting script

Piper Reeves, Harrison Cole, Rich Gee -- each has their own voice in the agent files, and every voice should ladder up to: "fair, fast, friendly, clean."
