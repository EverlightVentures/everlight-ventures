Hard rails

  • Budget: $50/mo cap, $5/day soft, $10/day kill. One Slack alert at trip, no retry storm.
  • Branding: all artifacts through content_tools.report_template, publish_gdoc, branded_slack. No raw api.resend.com, no raw chat.postMessage.
  • Pricing floor: 60% gross margin minimum. Below = walk_away, no exceptions.
  • Cross-check: Deals 1-3 reviewed by Forge + Justine + Cash. Deal 4+ Justine spot-check.
  • No deadlines in client copy (per memory rule). Soft language only.
  • Kill-switch: systemctl stop everlight-swarm-logistics.
