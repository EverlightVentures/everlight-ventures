# ALLEY KINGZ -- THE GAME <-> SHOP MIRROR (standing doctrine)
**Date:** 2026-06-07 | **Operator law (Rich):** "The market is the sister of the brother of the game.
Whatever the game has, the market has the mirror and complement." Every game element has TWO paths:
**EARN it** (play / ladder / trophy / PvE grind) and **GET it in the shop** (pay-to-play accelerate). They
mirror + complement; the shop NEVER replaces the earn-path (no pay-to-win), it shortcuts the grind.

## THE STANDING RULE (applies to EVERY future game feature)
> When we add anything to the GAME side, we define its SHOP mirror in the same breath -- the earn-path
> (how the ladder gives it) AND the buy-path (how the shop sells the shortcut), compared + contrasted, legal-
> lane tagged. No game feature ships its design without its market twin outlined here.

## THE COMPARE/CONTRAST FRAME (Rich's "ladder requirement vs how you get it in the shop")
For every item, two columns must always be answerable:
1. **Ladder requirement:** what playing it costs in TIME (matches/trophies/copies/wins).
2. **Shop shortcut:** what it costs in MONEY to skip that time -- priced as a *time-save*, never a power-unlock
   a free player can't eventually reach. The soft wall (named Mini-Boss Gate, ~15-20% above the strongest
   free deck) is the exact moment the shop shortcut feels worth it -- that is the conversion seam.

## THE TWO LEGAL LANES (never blended -- see MONETIZATION_LEGAL_LANES.md)
- **Lane A = the game shop:** sell draws/cards/passes, all prizes in-game value only, never cashable. (COD/PUBG model.)
- **Lane B = the B-CARDD BET blackjack:** sweepstakes, cashable, free-entry/AMOE. A SEPARATE product.
- **The one rule:** never sell a paid draw whose prize is cashable for real money.

## NO-PAY-TO-WIN FLOOR (the trust spine that keeps the ladder fair)
Card/tower curve `1 + 0.10*(L-1)` (HP/DMG only) -> a maxed Common never beats a base Mythic. Ranked Standard
normalizes all cards to a fixed level (wallet buys ZERO win-rate). The shop sells TIME and FLEX, never POWER.
