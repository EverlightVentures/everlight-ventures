Legal, fix before art

"GTR" is a Nissan trademark and it is sitting in the card data right now. Every chassis needs an original name that evokes the real car without borrowing it. Renaming after the meshes are built means re-rendering them. Rename first.

The same disease, art side

105 of 106 cards share 7 generic class clips. Only $BCARDD has bespoke FX. The roster reads generic because it is generic. The rig layer and the FX layer failed the same way: an archetype scaffold nobody came back to fill in.

02 / FactionsSeven crews, one city

The flattest thing in the roster, and nobody named it: faction and combat role are the same field. Boneguard Crew is bruiser. Zoomie Syndicate is sprinter. All 26 Boneguard dogs play identically. The factions are not cultures. They are stat blocks wearing names.

The structural fix

Decouple them. Faction is turf, look and lore. Role is combat function. Every crew fields all four roles, so an all-Boneguard deck runs a bruiser, a sprinter, a tech and a turret and still reads as Boneguard. One data change, zero credits, and it multiplies deck identity across the whole roster.

The game already ships six zones with no gangs in them: the Lot, Downtown, Neon Heights, the Yards, Factory Row, the Strip. That is The Warriors' architecture sitting unused. One city, one crew per turf, everybody wants the same streets.

Retired

  • Zoomie Syndicate. "Zoomies" is a puppy word. It undercut every scene it appeared in.
  • Leashbreak Tactix. The spelling was doing the work the writing should have. The idea survives, harder, as The Strays.
  • K9 Circuitry. Tech is a job, not a bloodline. It survives as the tech-ops role inside every crew.
  • Boneguard Crew keeps the name, drops "Crew", and becomes an actual faction instead of a synonym for bruiser.
The eighth faction, not playable

THE COLLAR. The ones who put the collars on. They do not hold turf because they hold paper on all of it. Every crew in the city hates them, and every crew in the city has taken their money. That contradiction is the engine of the story mode, and it is the only thing all seven agree on.

03 / ThesisRigs are gear, not decoration

A rig is a mount, a weapon platform, and an identity, the same slot a dragon occupies in an MMO. It is owned separately from the dog, swapped freely, and it changes what the dog does, not just how it looks. That one decision turns a 106-card roster into a combinatorial one.

Owned, not attached

The Garage

Rigs live in their own collection. You own a rig, not "Stonejaw's car." Any dog you own drives any rig you own.

Signature bonus

Lore pays out

Every dog has one canon rig. Driving it grants the signature bonus. Lore becomes a stat, so the writing carries mechanical weight.

Off-class is legal

Build diversity

A bruiser in a sport chassis is a real build with real tradeoffs. Not a penalty. A choice.

Rig sets the role

Frontline / protected

Chassis class decides where the dog stands in the lane. The rig is how you re-role a card you already own.

06 / SkinsThe cheap half is the money half

This is the most important economic fact in the document, and it is counterintuitive.

Skins cost zero credits

A skin is a material and texture swap on a mesh you already own. Twenty rigs times six skins is 120 sellable SKUs for zero additional Tripo credits. Candy paint, chrome, matte black, rust, neon underglow, gold. The mesh is bought once. The paint is free forever. Only accessories, meaning spoilers, plows, roof guns, rims and spikes, need geometry of their own.

07 / AttributesWhat a rig card carries

Mirroring the dog schema exactly, so the importer, the ScriptableObjects and the Unity socket taxonomy all take it without re-authoring.

Identity

rigNumber · name · make · family

Its own number space (R001 up), not a field hanging off a dog.

Attributes

armor · speed · handling · payload

Four stats that modify the dog's own hp, move_speed, range and damage.

Loadout

weapon · ability · cooldown

The rig brings its own active. Same shape as the dog's ability block.

Rarity

Common up to Mythic

The same five rungs as the dogs, so drop tables and the chest ladder need no new logic.

Lore

origin · flavor · comic

A 5-beat origin, same as the 106 dog books. Cars the movie, by way of The Warriors.

Sockets

hood · roof · rims · rear · underglow

Five attach points, authored the same way as the dog's six cosmetic slots.

08 / OrderWhat has to happen, in sequence

  1. Rename the chassis. Kill "GTR" before a single mesh is rendered.
  2. Author the 20 makes: names, silhouettes, attributes, weapon, ability. Free. This is writing, not spending.
  3. Write the rig roster onto the 106 cards, replacing 44 empty and 58 templated entries with real signature rigs.
  4. Lock the synergy matrix in data, so the 16 builds are tunable numbers and not hardcoded cases.
  5. Generate 20 full-body chassis refs with the same free FLUX pipeline that solved the dogs.
  6. Mesh them through Tripo at premium tier. Roughly 2,780 credits, all in.
  7. Author the skin catalog as material definitions. Zero credits, pure revenue.
  8. Wire the Garage: own, equip, signature bonus. Server-side. See below.
The blocker nobody wants to hear

Steps 1 through 7 are art and writing. Step 8 decides whether any of it earns a dollar. The cosmetic shop currently grants items with no balance check and no deduction, and the modular slot system, the exact one this rig catalog would plug into, has no server ownership at all. Build the rig economy on top of that and you ship a free catalog. Fix the ledger in the same month as the art, not after it.
