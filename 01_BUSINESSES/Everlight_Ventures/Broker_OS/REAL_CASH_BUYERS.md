## WHOLESALE PLATFORMS & BUYER NETWORKS (Use These First)

### InvestorLift -- #1 Wholesale Disposition Platform
- **Website:** investorlift.com
- **What:** 5.5 million+ cash investors on platform. List deals, auto-match to buyers. Used by 3,000+ wholesalers.
- **How to use:** Sign up, list properties under contract, platform broadcasts to matched buyers in your market.
- **Cost:** Paid platform (worth it once first deal closes)
- **Why it matters:** Single largest buyer pool in wholesale real estate. One listing reaches thousands.

### Connected Investors -- Wholesale Marketplace
- **Website:** connectedinvestors.com
- **What:** Real estate investing software with One-Click Cash Buyers tool. Find who's buying cash in any zip code.
- **How to use:** Search by market, find recent cash buyers, connect directly.
- **Cost:** Paid tiers available, some free features

### PropStream (acquired BatchLeads July 2025)
- **Website:** propstream.com
- **What:** 160M+ US properties. Find cash buyers by searching recent cash transactions in any county.
- **How to use:** Filter by "cash transactions" in target zip codes. Export buyer names + addresses. Skip trace for contact info.
- **Cost:** ~$99/mo (free 7-day trial)

### National REIA -- Find Local Investor Groups
- **Website:** nationalreia.org/find-a-reia/
- **What:** 120+ local chapters, 40,000 members. Interactive map to find REIA in any city.
- **How to use:** Attend meetings, network, build buyer list face-to-face.
