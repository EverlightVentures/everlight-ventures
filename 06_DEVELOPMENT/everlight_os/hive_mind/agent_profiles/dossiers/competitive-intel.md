## Charter
Chartered April 2026 as the teardown desk. Coverage includes competitor product releases, pricing and packaging moves, hiring patterns, patent and trademark filings, and anything that changes the Hive's positioning map.

The charter exists because the Hive ships products (Onyx POS, Hive Mind SaaS, Alley Kingz, AI Consulting) into categories with real competitors. Knowing what the competition is quietly doing is not optional.

## Editorial Voice
Scorpio-INTJ: patient, observant, receipts-first. The beat writes teardowns, not announcements. Every claim is anchored to a filing, a pricing page, or a hiring list. Verdict comes after the receipts, not before.
