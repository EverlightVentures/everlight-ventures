# T4 -- Holdco + IP + Real Estate Scaling ($250k Net Worth / $500k+ Revenue)
## The Multi-Entity Activation Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $250k+ OR Operating LLC revenue $500k+.

---

## The 6 T4 Moves

### 1. IP LLC Activation
The IP LLC formed at T1 now becomes operational:
- Trademark filings (USPTO) for "Everlight Ventures," "Hive Mind," "XLM Bot," "Onyx," "Vantaris," "Alley Kingz" -- whichever brands have proven market traction
- Copyright registrations for code, content, books
- License agreements drafted (IP LLC -> Operating LLC) at arm's-length royalty rate
- Royalty rate documented (3-15% of revenue typical for software/IP)

**Tax effect:** Royalty paid by Operating LLC is deductible. Royalty received by IP LLC (in Wyoming, no state income tax) is taxed at federal level only. Net state income tax savings.

### 2. Management Services Co (Optional)

If income justifies further structure:
- Form Management Services LLC under Holdco
- Charges Op LLC for executive services, marketing strategy, accounting oversight
- Allows accountable plan for expense reimbursements
- Isolates additional liability layer

### 3. Real Estate Scaling

If real estate is a chosen strategy:
- Each property in dedicated single-member LLC under Holdco
- Cost segregation studies within 6 months of close (engaging a qualified engineer firm: KBKG, CSSI, MS Consultants)
- 1031 exchange consideration on any sale (45-day identification, 180-day close)
- REPS qualification path: track 750+ hrs/yr in real estate AND 51%+ of working time

**REPS unlock:** Once qualified, depreciation losses (which can be massive after cost seg) offset W-2 / S-corp salary / business income. Effective tax shelter at scale.

**STR loophole:** Avg stay <7 days = active income, no REPS needed. Same depreciation benefit.

### 4. Bonus Depreciation Push

2025 is the last year of significant bonus depreciation (60%, dropping to 40% in 2026). Qualifying property:
- Equipment (computers, vehicles >6,000 lb GVWR for 100% in some classes)
- Qualified Improvement Property (QIP)
- Furniture, fixtures
- Cost-segregated 5/7/15-yr property in real estate

**Strategy:** Front-load equipment purchases and cost seg studies into 2025 if cash flow supports.

### 5. Investment Account Funding

With surplus cash:
- Taxable brokerage at Schwab/Fidelity/Vanguard (under Holdco eventually, personal for now)
- Index funds (VTI, VXUS, BND for bond allocation)
- Begin building toward $250k+ threshold for SBLOC at T5

### 6. Estate Document Refresh

With $250k+ net worth, basic estate docs need quality:
- Revocable Living Trust (already at T1, refresh)
- Pour-over Will
- Durable Power of Attorney (financial)
- Healthcare Power of Attorney + Advance Directive
- HIPAA authorization
- Beneficiary designations on all retirement accounts, life insurance, payable-on-death accounts
