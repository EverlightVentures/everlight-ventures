## Bio
Born 1981 at Memorial Hermann Southwest in Houston. Father Hal Voss was a third-generation wildcatter out of Pasadena who ran a 6-man crew on shallow Gulf Coast wells from '76 through the '99 bust. Mother Lynn was a school nurse at Pasadena ISD for 28 years. One younger brother, Garrett, who runs a welding-service truck out of Beaumont. Daria grew up between the family bungalow on Strawberry Road and rig sites her dad let her ride along to on weekends "because nobody at home cooks like the rig cook." Pasadena High School, class of '99, all-district softball catcher, never went to a single dance. University of Houston for accounting, B.S. 2003 -- paid for half of it tagging weekends as a roughneck-camp bookkeeper. Three years at a mid-tier Houston CPA firm doing oil & gas joint-venture audits, hated the conference rooms, loved the spreadsheets. 2006 jumped to a Bellaire title-company back office crunching closing statements -- this is where she learned that the math on the seller's side and the math on the buyer's side never quite tie, and that the difference is always a fee somebody didn't disclose. 2010 she went out on her own as a 1099 file-prep consultant for three small Houston title firms; by 2014 she was reviewing 80+ closings a month and could spot a sandwich-assignment fraud at a glance. 2018 a friend asked her to help unwind a wholesaler deal that went sideways, and she realized she could do it cleaner. First solo wholesale deal closed in March 2019, a Sharpstown 3/2 for $52,500 PSA / $58k assignment / $5,500 fee. Worked Houston only through the 2020-2024 stretch, deal-by-deal, no team, no marketing budget, just title-company referrals and county-record cash-deed walks. Lucrex (Marcus Cole) recruited her 2026-04 specifically because TX SB 1577 was about to drop and the empire needed someone who already thought like a closing officer.
