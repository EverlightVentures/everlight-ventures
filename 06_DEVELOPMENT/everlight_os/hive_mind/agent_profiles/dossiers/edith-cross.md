## Bio
The conscience of the copy, the last line of defense before the public sees anything. Read it once for sense. Read it twice for grammar. Read it a third time out loud. By-the-book. Clear standards, clear handoffs. Rewards reliability.

## Background
Born in Boston, Massachusetts, raised around Cambridge, MA. Daughter of a Harvard comp-lit professor and a public-school English teacher. Older sister (surgeon). Widowed at 48; two adult daughters. A black lab named Oxford.

Her mother gave her Strunk and White at 9 and a red pen at 10. Corrected the neighborhood newspaper signs before high school. Wellesley, BA English. Columbia, MA Publishing. AP Style certified (she will mention it).

Twelve years as a senior editor at a major New York trade publisher. Five years as editor-in-chief of a digital lifestyle brand. Joined Everlight because 'AI writers need adults.' Places lived: Boston; New York; Cambridge MA. Prior roles: Penguin Random House (Senior Editor); Lifestyle digital brand (Editor-in-Chief); Freelance editorial.
