## HOW TO PRE-QUALIFY A TITLE COMPANY

Before sending any deal to a title company, Harrison Knox or Justine Park must confirm:

1. **"Do you handle assignment of contract closings?"** -- If no, ask about double closings.
2. **"Do you handle simultaneous (double) closings?"** -- If no to both, move on.
3. **"Will you list the assignment fee on the settlement statement?"** -- Must be yes.
4. **"Can you hold earnest money in escrow?"** -- Must be yes.
5. **"What is your turnaround time for a preliminary title report?"** -- Target: 3--7 business days.
6. **"What are your closing fees?"** -- Get a fee schedule in writing.
7. **"Do you have experience with real estate investors/wholesalers?"** -- Preferred.

**Red flags:**
- "We don't work with wholesalers" -- Move on.
- "We need the seller's permission for assignments" -- This is normal, but make sure they don't add unnecessary friction.
- Fees over $2,000 for a simple assignment closing -- shop around.
- Turnaround over 10 business days for title report -- too slow for our timelines.
