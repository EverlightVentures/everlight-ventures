# L7 -- Generational Engine
## How Wealth Survives 100 Years

**The Rockefeller fortune is now in its sixth generation. The Walton fortune is in its fourth. The Mellon fortune is in its fifth. The mechanism is L7. Without it, even great wealth typically vanishes within 3 generations (the "shirtsleeves to shirtsleeves" rule).**

---

## The Three Eras of Generational Wealth

### Era 1: Wealth Creation (You, T0-T8)
Build the assets, deploy the OS, optimize tax structure.

### Era 2: Wealth Transfer (T7-T11)
Move assets to the successor generation efficiently, minimize estate tax, structure for protection from heirs' creditors / divorce / immaturity.

### Era 3: Wealth Stewardship (Heirs, indefinitely)
Trust governance, family education, productive deployment, philanthropic vehicles, sustained values.

L7 covers Era 2 and the bones of Era 3.

## The Annual Exclusion Stack (T2 onwards)

**The free gift:** Each year, you can gift up to $19,000 (2025 amount) to ANY person, no gift tax, no documentation, no lifetime exemption used.

**Stacked across recipients and spouses:**
- $19k per donor per recipient per year
- Married couple = $38k per recipient per year (gift-splitting)
- 5 grandchildren = $190k/yr/couple
- 5 grandkids + their 5 spouses = $380k/yr
- Add adult children + their spouses + nieces/nephews = $1M+/yr possible

**Compounded over decades:** $200k/yr × 30 years = $6M moved out of estate, ZERO gift tax, lifetime exemption preserved for other moves.

**Action:** As soon as any heirs exist (or once net worth justifies it), max out annual exclusion every year. Tools: 529s, custodial accounts, direct medical/educational payments (these have UNLIMITED exclusion under different rules).

## 529 Plan Superfunding (T2-T3)

**Standard 529:** State-sponsored education savings account. Contributions grow tax-free. Withdrawals for qualified education expenses are tax-free.

**The superfund:** You can front-load 5 years of annual exclusion gifts in one year. So in 2025: $19k × 5 = $95,000 per donor per beneficiary in a single contribution, all gift-tax free.

**Couple superfunding for one child:** $190,000 in one shot.
**Couple superfunding for 5 grandkids:** $950,000 in one shot, all out of estate, no gift tax.

**SECURE 2.0 (2024) bonus:** Up to $35,000 lifetime can be rolled from 529 to a Roth IRA for the beneficiary (after 15 yrs of 529 ownership, contribution limits apply). 529 becomes a flexible retirement vehicle if education isn't needed.

**Best 529 plans:** Utah (My529), Nevada (Vanguard), Illinois (Bright Start) -- low fees, good investment options. Pick state agnostic of your residence (most state tax deductions are minor).

## Direct Educational and Medical Payments (Sec 2503(e))

**Unlimited exclusion** for payments DIRECTLY to:
- A qualified educational institution (tuition only, not room/board)
- A medical provider (medical bills, health insurance premiums)

**Stack with annual exclusion:** Pay grandchild's $80k/yr private college tuition direct to the school AND give them $19k cash, all gift-tax free.

**Power move:** Pay tuition for an entire generation directly. Free transfer with no impact on lifetime exemption, no gift tax, unlimited.

## Lifetime Gift / Estate Exemption (T6-T8)

**The big number:**
- 2025: $13.99M per person ($27.98M per married couple) lifetime exemption
- 2026 (if no legislative action): ~$7M per person

**How it works:**
- Use it during life (gift tax exclusion) OR at death (estate tax exclusion). Combined.
- Once used, doesn't reset. Permanent reduction.
- "Use it or lose it" by year-end 2025 for the doubled amount.

**Strategic deployment:**
- **SLAT** (Spousal Lifetime Access Trust) -- use exemption now while spouse retains access
- **Dynasty Trust** -- use exemption AND protect for generations
- **GRAT/IDGT** -- use leveraged transfer techniques to multiply exemption

## The Generation-Skipping Transfer (GST) Tax

**The IRS's anti-Dynasty-Trust weapon:** A 40% TAX on transfers that skip a generation (grandparent to grandchild bypassing the parent generation).

**The shield:** GST exemption ($13.99M in 2025, sunsetting to $7M like estate exemption).

**Strategy:** Allocate GST exemption to Dynasty Trusts. Once allocated, ALL future appreciation in the trust is also GST-exempt forever. So $13.99M moved into a Dynasty Trust today, growing at 7% over 100 years, becomes ~$11 BILLION, all GST-tax-free, all out of any heir's taxable estate forever.

**This is the actual mechanism of multi-century wealth.**

## Crummey Withdrawal Power

**The technical fix for ILIT premium gifts (and other irrevocable trust contributions):** For a gift to qualify for annual exclusion, the recipient must have a "present interest" -- the right to use the gift now. Trust assets are usually FUTURE interests. Crummey solves this.

**The mechanic:** Each beneficiary gets a 30-day "Crummey window" to withdraw their share of the contribution. They (predictably) don't withdraw. After 30 days, the contribution stays in trust. But because they HAD the right, the gift qualified for annual exclusion.

**Documentation:** Crummey letters mailed to each beneficiary annually. Tedious but mandatory. ILIT trustees handle this routinely.

## The Family Bank Concept (T8+)

**The idea:** Lend money to family members at AFR (Applicable Federal Rate) instead of giving outright. Currently AFR is ~4.5%.

**Why it works:**
- Loan is not a gift (no exemption used)
- Borrower can deduct interest if for qualified use
- Lender (or the trust acting as lender) earns return
- Default risk is family-managed
- Multi-generational wealth grows by RECYCLING through family rather than spending external interest

**Walton family example:** Walton trusts loan to younger generation Waltons to fund new business ventures, mortgages, life events. Repaid with interest. Wealth stays in family ecosystem.

**Documentation requirement:** Real promissory note, real interest, real schedule, actual payments. Otherwise IRS recharacterizes as gift.

## Charitable Lead Trust (CLT) -- T8+

**The estate compression play:** Trust pays charity for a term of years, then remainder goes to heirs.

**The math:** $10M into CLT. Pays $500k/yr to charity for 20 years. After 20 yrs, remainder (whatever's left) goes to heirs. For estate / gift tax purposes, the heirs' interest is valued at present value of the future remainder (low). At year 20, if the trust grew at >5%, the heirs receive $5-15M with the gift tax cost calculated as if they only got $1-3M.

**Best for:** Charitably-inclined families with $10M+ estates.

## Generational Wealth Education

**The unspoken layer:** All the legal structures fail if the successor generation isn't educated to deploy them. The "shirtsleeves to shirtsleeves" rule (90% of family wealth gone by Gen 3) is not legal failure -- it's educational failure.

**Investment in successor capability:**
- Family meetings (annual at minimum, semi-annual at T8+)
- Investment committee participation (heirs as observers from age 12+)
- Family bank loans for first-business / first-home (skin in the game)
- Charitable foundation involvement (board seats for adult heirs)
- Family office training program / formal mentorship
- Outside professional engagement (attorney, CPA relationships built early)

---

## The Family Constitution (T9+)

**A written governance document** for the family enterprise. Topics:
- Mission and values (what does the family wealth EXIST for?)
- Decision-making rules (who decides what, voting structure)
- Distribution policy (who gets what, when, under what conditions)
- Education requirements (heirs must complete X before receiving Y)
- Conflict resolution (family council, mediation, arbitration)
- Trustee removal / replacement procedures
- Charitable mandate (% of wealth committed to philanthropy)

**Why it matters:** The biggest threat to multi-generational wealth is family conflict. A constitution is the prenup of family wealth. Better to design it now than litigate it later.

## Special Situation: No Heirs / Charitable-Focused

If the answer to "do you want kids/heirs to inherit" is no:

**Different optimization:**
- All of L7 redirects to charitable structures
- CRT (Charitable Remainder Trust) becomes primary vehicle (income stream for life, remainder to charity)
- Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) for flexible charitable giving with immediate deduction
- Private Foundation if $5M+ charitable corpus and operational mission
- 100% step-up basis at death not relevant (no heirs to receive)
- Estate tax exemption fully usable for charitable bequests (unlimited charitable deduction at death)

**This profile inverts the entire OS** toward L4 (credits maximization) + L1-L3 (lifetime tax efficiency) + charitable structures. PRIORITIES.md captures this via GENERATIONAL: 1.

## Sunset Clock Reminder (CRITICAL)

**Lifetime gift / estate / GST exemption sunsets December 31, 2025.**

If your trajectory crosses $7M before late 2025, the planning window is NOW. The professionals to execute (estate attorneys) typically stop taking new SLAT/Dynasty Trust clients after September 2025 due to volume. Plan early, fund early.

Most likely Everlight scenario: T0 today, T1 ($10-25k) by Q4 2025, T6 ($1M) by 2027-2028. **Net worth unlikely to cross $7M before sunset, so urgency is low.** But monitor the trajectory.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Waiting too long to start exemption planning.** The earlier the asset enters an irrevocable trust, the more growth occurs OUTSIDE the estate. Time is the most valuable input.
2. **Reciprocal SLATs.** Husband funds wife's SLAT, wife funds husband's SLAT, identical terms. IRS unwinds. Stagger by 6+ months, vary terms, vary trustees.
3. **Joint titling appreciated assets with adult kids.** Defeats step-up basis (they receive your basis, not stepped-up). Use trust structures instead.
4. **Forgetting Crummey letters.** Single most common ILIT mistake. Letters must be sent and documented annually.
5. **Family Bank with no real payments.** "Loans" with no payments = gift recharacterization. Real notes, real payments, real interest.
6. **No family education.** Heirs receiving $10M with no investment knowledge, no business experience, no values training = predictable disaster.
7. **DIY family constitution.** Family conflict requires a professional facilitator. Pay for it.
