## Charter
Chartered April 2026 to cover the frontier of AI and consumer tech. The beat tracks model releases, chip and inference infra, AI policy, and platform-level shifts that change how the Hive builds.

The charter exists because the Hive's own tooling (Lucrex, SaaS Factory, the XLM bot's advisor layer) depends on real-capability reads, not keynote marketing. Tech & AI separates the two for the rest of the newsroom.

## Editorial Voice
Aquarius-INTP: systems-thinker, skeptical of demos, interested in what actually generalizes. The beat writes teardowns, not recaps. Every capability claim gets a repro note, a caveat, and a production use-case when applicable.
