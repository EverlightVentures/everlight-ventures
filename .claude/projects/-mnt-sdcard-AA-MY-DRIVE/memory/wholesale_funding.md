## LLC Reinstatement
- Everlight Logistics LLC needs reinstatement via GA Secretary of State
- Cost: $250 filing + $50/yr back annual fees + $25/yr late penalties
- Must apply within 5 years of dissolution or entity is lost permanently
- File at: sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-guide-reinstate-entity

## Available Funding ($0 out of pocket)
- Kiva 0% microloan: up to $15k, no fees, no credit score min (kiva.org/borrow)
- NAACP Powershift Grant: $25k grant, opens Oct 2026
- UGA SBDC: free business consulting (georgiasbdc.org/consulting)
- SBA Microloan: up to $50k at 8-13%, cannot buy real estate but can fund marketing

## Opportunity Zones
- Atlanta has 27 OZ census tracts -- flag these in deal sheets for buyers
- OZ 1.0 sunsets Dec 31, 2026; OZ 2.0 starts Jan 1, 2027
- 10-year hold in OZ = ALL appreciation is tax-free

## Strategy
- Close first deal as sole prop, use $475 from fee to reinstate LLC
- Or apply for Kiva $0 loan immediately to fund reinstatement + operations
