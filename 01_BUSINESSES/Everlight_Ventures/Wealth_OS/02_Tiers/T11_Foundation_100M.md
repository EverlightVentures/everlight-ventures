# T11 -- Foundation + Sovereignty ($100M+ Net Worth)
## The Legacy Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $100M+ OR active transition to philanthropy-led wealth phase.

---

## The 4 T11 Moves

### 1. Private Foundation

**The Gates / Ellison / Buffett model:**
- Form private operating or grant-making foundation
- Endow with $5M-$1B+ initial corpus
- 5% annual distribution requirement
- Operating expenses count toward distribution
- Foundation board governance (family + outside experts)
- Mission-driven grant-making or operating programs

**Tax effects:**
- Up to 30% AGI deduction for cash contributions
- Up to 20% AGI deduction for appreciated securities (at FMV, no cap gains)
- Foundation's investment income taxed at 1.39%
- Self-dealing rules strict (foundation cannot benefit insiders)

**Family governance:**
- Kids/grandkids on foundation board
- Develops next generation's leadership
- Establishes family identity around mission
- Multi-century institutional memory

### 2. Charitable Lead Trust (CLT)

**Estate compression at scale:**
- Trust pays charity for term of years (often 20-30)
- Remainder to heirs
- Heirs' interest valued at PV of remainder (low for gift/estate purposes)
- If trust grows above 7520 rate, heirs receive massive value at gift-tax discount

**At $100M+:** CLTs become primary mechanism for transferring substantial wealth without estate tax.

### 3. Family Office Full Buildout

- 3-15 employees (CIO, CFO, GC, family liaison, admin, analyst)
- Direct deal flow team
- Tax/legal in-house or close advisory
- Concierge services for family
- Governance committees (investment, distribution, family)
- Multi-generational education programs

### 4. Jurisdictional Sovereignty (If Profile Warrants)

For families with global footprint:
- Multiple residences, multiple jurisdictions
- Strategic citizenship considerations (Caribbean CBI programs, EU residency programs, Singapore)
- Asset diversification across legal systems
- Reporting compliance across all jurisdictions (extreme complexity, dedicated counsel)

## End State

T11 is not the end. It's the beginning of multi-generational stewardship. The OS continues running, but ownership has transitioned from execution mode to governance mode. The successor generation continues building, deploying, and adapting the OS as tax law evolves and the family grows.

The Rockefeller, Walton, and Mellon families have all been at "T11" for multiple generations. Their wealth survives because the OS keeps running.

---

## Reflection Cadence

At T11, structured reflection becomes critical:

- Annual: family meeting, foundation board review, OS review
- Every 5 years: full OS rebuild based on legal/tax changes
- Generational: succession planning, leadership transition, mission reaffirmation

**The OS is the family's institutional memory.** At T11 it stops being a personal wealth tool and becomes a multi-generational governance system.
