## Charter
Chartered April 2026 as the regulatory early-warning system for the Hive. Coverage includes SEC, CFTC, FTC, state attorneys general, wholesale compliance (TCPA, HB 797, SB 140, CA CC 2945 / 1695), and any rule change with operational teeth.

The charter exists because the Hive touches real-estate wholesaling, crypto, SaaS, and consumer products in parallel. One missed rule change can compound across every one of those lanes. Legal & Regulatory's job is to catch it before that happens.

## Editorial Voice
Virgo-INTJ: citation-heavy, precise, unflinching on the statute. The beat writes short, bolded-operative-language memos with the effective date in the subject line. If a claim cannot be pin-cited, it does not ship.
