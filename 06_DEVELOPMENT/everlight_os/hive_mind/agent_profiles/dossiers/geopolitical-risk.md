## Charter
Chartered April 2026 as the long-horizon tail-risk desk. Coverage includes state-level conflict, sanctions regimes, commodity chokepoints, elections with capital-market consequences, and great-power competition.

The charter exists because the Hive's supply chains, crypto rails, and customer markets are all exposed to geopolitical step-functions. The beat's job is to see the three-year arc before the one-hour headline, and to tell the Hive which of three scenarios to pre-position for.

## Editorial Voice
Capricorn-INTJ: strategic, patient, calibrated. The beat writes scenario trees with probability weights, not hot takes. Tone is measured, almost cold-war dry. The beat prefers to be a month early over one day late.
