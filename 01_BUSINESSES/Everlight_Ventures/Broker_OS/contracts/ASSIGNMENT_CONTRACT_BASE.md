### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Assignor entered into a Real Estate Purchase Agreement ("Original Contract") dated [ORIGINAL_CONTRACT_DATE] with the Original Seller for the purchase of the Property described above; and

**WHEREAS**, the Original Contract [does / does not] contain a prohibition on assignment; and

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

### 1. ASSIGNMENT

Assignor hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the Original Contract, including but not limited to the right to purchase the Property on the terms and conditions set forth in the Original Contract.

### 2. ASSIGNMENT FEE

Assignee shall pay to Assignor an assignment fee of **$[ASSIGNMENT_FEE]** ("Assignment Fee"). The Assignment Fee shall be paid as follows:

- **$[DEPOSIT_AMOUNT]** non-refundable deposit due upon execution of this Assignment Agreement, payable to [ESCROW_HOLDER].
- **Balance of $[BALANCE_AMOUNT]** due at closing, payable through the closing/escrow agent.

The Assignment Fee is separate from and in addition to the purchase price in the Original Contract.

### 3. EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT

[STATE_EMD_RULES]

The Earnest Money Deposit ("EMD") of **$[EMD_AMOUNT]** shall be held by [ESCROW_HOLDER] in accordance with the terms of the Original Contract and applicable state law.

### 4. ASSIGNOR'S REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee that:

(a) Assignor has the legal right and authority to assign the Original Contract;

(b) The Original Contract is in full force and effect and has not been modified, amended, or terminated except as disclosed herein;

(c) Assignor has not previously assigned, pledged, or encumbered its interest in the Original Contract;

(d) Assignor is not the owner of the Property -- Assignor holds an equitable interest under the Original Contract only;

(e) The purchase price in the Original Contract is **$[ORIGINAL_PURCHASE_PRICE]**;

(f) The Assignment Fee is **$[ASSIGNMENT_FEE]** (full and transparent disclosure).

### 6. INSPECTION / DUE DILIGENCE PERIOD

[STATE_INSPECTION_PERIOD]

Assignee shall have **[INSPECTION_DAYS] calendar days** from the Effective Date of this Assignment to conduct inspections, due diligence, and review of the Property and the Original Contract. During this period, Assignee may terminate this Assignment for any reason by providing written notice to Assignor and Escrow Holder.

Upon termination during the inspection/due diligence period:
- EMD shall be returned to Assignee per the terms of the Original Contract and applicable state law.
- Non-refundable deposits or due diligence fees, if any, shall be retained by the party entitled to them under state custom and the terms herein.

### 7. CLOSING

Closing shall occur on or before **[CLOSING_DATE]**, or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

[STATE_CLOSING_REQUIREMENTS]

### 8. ASSUMPTION OF OBLIGATIONS

Upon closing, Assignee assumes all of Assignor's obligations under the Original Contract, including but not limited to the obligation to purchase the Property at the Original Contract price and on the Original Contract terms.

### 9. ASSIGNOR'S ROLE AND LIMITATIONS

**Assignor acts solely as an assignor of its contractual interest.** Assignor:

(a) Does NOT act as a real estate broker, agent, or advisor to either party;

(b) Does NOT guarantee the condition, value, or fitness of the Property for any purpose;

(c) Does NOT owe a fiduciary duty to Assignee or Original Seller;

(d) Has NOT inspected the Property and makes no representations regarding its condition;

(e) Recommends that Assignee conduct independent inspections, obtain title insurance, and engage legal counsel.

### 10. ESCAPE CLAUSES AND CONTINGENCIES

This Assignment is contingent upon the following:

(a) **Title Contingency**: Clear and marketable title, free of liens and encumbrances not disclosed in the Original Contract.

(b) **Inspection Contingency**: Satisfactory completion of the inspection/due diligence period per Section 6.

(c) **Financing Contingency** (if applicable): Assignee obtaining financing commitment within [FINANCING_DAYS] days of the Effective Date.

(d) **Partner/Investor Approval Contingency**: This Assignment is subject to approval by Assignee's partners or investors within [APPROVAL_DAYS] business days of the Effective Date. If approval is not obtained, Assignee may terminate this Assignment by written notice, and EMD shall be refunded.

[STATE_ESCAPE_CLAUSES]

### 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

(a) **Assignee Default**: If Assignee fails to close through no fault of Assignor or Seller, Assignor may retain the non-refundable deposit as liquidated damages, which shall be Assignor's sole and exclusive remedy. Assignor waives the right to seek specific performance or consequential damages against Assignee.

(b) **Assignor Default**: If Assignor fails to perform, Assignee may seek return of all deposits and the non-refundable deposit, or pursue specific performance or actual damages.

### 12. INDEMNIFICATION

(a) Assignor shall indemnify and hold harmless Assignee from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from Assignor's breach of this Agreement or misrepresentation.

(b) Assignee shall indemnify and hold harmless Assignor from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from Assignee's assumption of the Original Contract or actions after closing.

### 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Assignment shall be resolved as follows:

(a) **Mediation**: The parties shall first attempt to resolve the dispute through mediation.

(b) **Arbitration**: If mediation fails within 30 days, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by JAMS in [ARBITRATION_VENUE], [STATE], under JAMS Comprehensive Arbitration Rules.

(c) **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of [STATE].

(d) **Attorneys' Fees**: The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

### 14. LIABILITY LIMITATION

Assignor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Assignment Fee actually received by Assignor. In no event shall either party be liable for consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages.

### 15. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

Assignor and Assignee are independent parties. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the parties. Each party is responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and compliance obligations.

### 16. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Assignment Agreement, together with the Original Contract, the State Addendum attached hereto, and any exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No modification shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all parties.
