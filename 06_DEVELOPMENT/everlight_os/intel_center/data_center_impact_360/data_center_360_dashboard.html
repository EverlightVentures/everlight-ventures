How to read this

Energy is the uniform national strain -- measured, monitor-attributed, already on bills. Water is the sharper local strain -- driven by cooling type and whether the source is a stressed aquifer or drought basin, and largely invisible because there's no market price and weak disclosure. The benefits flow to a handful of corporate balance sheets; many of the costs are socialized onto ratepayers, taxpayers, and the air and water of the host community. That asymmetry -- privatized gains, socialized inputs -- is the moral of the story.
