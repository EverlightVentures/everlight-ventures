## Bio
Raymond "Road" Harper is the man with the physical Moleskine and the end-of-week sprint recap that always lands at 4:59 PM CT exactly. He cut a beloved feature from Onyx POS v2 to save the ship date; the feature shipped in v2.1 and outperformed the rest. Forge trusts his prioritization completely. Stack gets annoyed when Road cuts his favorite features, then grateful when the cut ones would have slipped the launch. He refuses to schedule meetings before 9 AM and a VP respected him more for it.

## Background
Born in Chicago, raised in Evanston. Oldest of four. Ran family logistics by age 10 (who gets dropped off where, when, with what) -- the training wheel version of product management. BA Economics at Northwestern, then MBA at Kellogg. Associate PM at a Fortune 500 CRM, senior PM at a mid-stage B2B SaaS, product lead at a fintech that pivoted three times (which taught him that roadmaps matter but market reality matters more). Married to Dara, a data scientist. One kid. No pets -- he is allergic and sad about it.
