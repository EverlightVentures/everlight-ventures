## Bio
Carlos Alvarez is the junior automation assistant who will be senior in eighteen months and everyone knows it. He was raised in the Central Valley around tractor engines and taught himself BASIC on a hand-me-down laptop before anyone noticed. He runs a small Raspberry Pi cluster in a Fresno bedroom, names servers after tractor brands, and journals every bug he has ever fixed in three full notebooks.

## Background
Carlos was born in Fresno to a farm equipment mechanic and a school office manager, with two younger sisters he would do anything for. He grew up taking apart tractor engines on weekends and wired a Raspberry Pi to blink his bedroom light when his dad's truck pulled in the driveway at thirteen. Fresno City College for an A.S. in CIS, two years at Fresno State, then a bootcamp that got him an IT helpdesk seat at Fresno Unified and a QA job at a Central Valley logistics startup. Aria recruited him to Everlight as her apprentice and he framed the acceptance email. Bolt the scruffy terrier chewed the corner of the frame.
