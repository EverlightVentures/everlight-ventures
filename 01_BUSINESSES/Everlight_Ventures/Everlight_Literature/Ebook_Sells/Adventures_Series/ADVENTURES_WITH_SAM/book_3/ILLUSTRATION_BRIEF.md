## FORMAT REQUIREMENT -- CRITICAL

Every scene in this book requires **TWO illustrations:**

1. **LEFT PAGE -- B&W Coloring Page:** Clean line art with bold outlines. Open white space for kids to color with crayons/markers. No solid fills. This is a COLORING BOOK page.

2. **RIGHT PAGE -- Full Color Illustration:** Same scene, fully colored. Vibrant, saturated, cartoon/animation quality. Rich detail. This is the "finished" version the child sees while coloring the left page.

Both pages show the SAME scene from the SAME angle. The B&W version is the line art version of the color page.

## KDP PRODUCTION SPECS -- BY FORMAT

All illustrations must be created at print-quality resolution first,
then downsampled for ebook. Never upscale from ebook to print.

### Paperback Interior (KDP Print)
- **Trim size:** 8.5 x 11 inches (standard children's picture book)
- **Bleed:** 0.125 inches on all sides (final file: 8.75 x 11.25 inches)
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Pixel dimensions:** 2625 x 3375 px (at trim), 2775 x 3600 px (with bleed)
- **Color space:** CMYK preferred, sRGB accepted (KDP converts)
- **File format:** PNG or TIFF (lossless) for master files
- **Interior PDF:** Images embedded at 300 DPI, fonts outlined
- **No text or critical content within 0.25 inches of trim edge**

### Hardcover Interior (KDP Print)
- **Same as paperback** for interior illustrations
- **Cover:** Case laminate wrap adds 0.625 inches per side beyond trim
- **Use KDP Cover Calculator** for exact wrap dimensions (varies by page count)
- **Spine width:** Calculated from page count (use KDP calculator)

### Ebook (Kindle)
- **Interior images:** RGB, JPG or PNG
- **Resolution:** 72-300 DPI (displays at screen resolution)
- **Recommended pixel size:** 1600 x 2560 px (Kindle max display)
- **Max file size per image:** 50 MB
- **Cover image:** 2560 x 1600 px (landscape) or 1600 x 2560 px (portrait)
- **Minimum cover:** 1000 x 625 px
- **File format:** JPG (for smaller file size) or PNG

### Cover Specs by Format

| Format | Width x Height | Bleed | DPI | File Type |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----|-----------|
| Paperback cover (front only) | 8.75 x 11.25 in | 0.125 in | 300 | PDF or PNG |

## ART STYLE -- MATCH BOOKS 1-2

The style is established. Do NOT deviate.

- **Genre:** Children's cartoon illustration, coloring book hybrid
- **Line weight:** Bold, clean outlines (B&W pages). Confident strokes, no sketchy lines.
- **Faces:** Large, expressive eyes. Simple noses. Wide mouths that show emotion clearly.
- **Colors (full color pages):** Highly saturated, bright, warm. Think animation-ready. Greens are GREEN. Blues are BLUE. No muted palettes. Golden light throughout.
- **Backgrounds:** Detailed but not cluttered. Nature scenes should have identifiable elements (individual flowers, leaves, rocks, clouds) that a child can point to and name.
- **Shading (B&W pages):** Minimal grey tones. Primarily clean outlines suitable for coloring. Professional coloring book quality.
- **Overall mood:** Warm, painterly children's book style, golden light, friendly characters.

## CHARACTER REFERENCE -- CANONICAL SPECS

### SAM
- **Age appearance:** 5-7 years old
- **Hair:** Brown/dark, messy, spiky on top (outside-kid hair)
- **Eyes:** Large, round, brown, VERY expressive -- the first thing you notice
- **Face:** Round, warm, animated. Big smile when excited. Brow furrows when thinking.
- **Skin:** Warm, sun-touched (match Books 1-2 exactly)
- **Outfit for Book 3:** Red t-shirt (consistent with series), jeans, scuffed sneakers
- **Backpack:** Slightly too big for him, worn, always on his back
- **Superpower moment (Ch 5):** Add golden glow aura when he discovers his heightened senses superpower. Match the "superhero reveal" style from Books 1-2 celebration scenes.

### ROBO
- **Size:** Sam's height or slightly shorter, compact
- **Body:** Boxy/rounded torso, silver/grey metallic with blue and orange accent lights
- **Head:** Rounded square shape with a small antenna on top (ball on stick)
- **Eyes:** Square/rounded with horizontal line pupils (like screen displays). Glow blue-orange.
- **Chest display:** Rectangular screen panel on torso showing icons and simple graphics (changes per scene)
- **Limbs:** Simple, rounded, non-threatening. Feet are flat/boot-like.
- **Expression:** Robo emotes through his EYES and CHEST DISPLAY. No mouth. Eyes change shape to show curiosity (wide), concern (angled), joy (curved up).

### LILY
- **Hair:** Short, dark, sometimes in a ponytail
- **Eyes:** Large, bright, friendly
- **Outfit:** Casual -- overalls or t-shirt and shorts. Energetic kid.
- **Role:** Sam's friend who appears in Ch 1, helps with the mystery.

### THE FARMER
- **Age:** 50s-60s, weathered and kind
- **Build:** Sturdy, strong hands
- **Face:** Sun-tanned, warm smile, kind eyes
- **Outfit:** Wide-brimmed hat, work boots, flannel or overalls
- **Role:** Appears in Ch 1, helps with the mystery hunt.

### MAX
- **Age appearance:** 5-7 years old, same age as Sam
- **Hair:** Lighter brown or blond, slightly neater than Sam
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive -- sad when introduced, happy when helped
- **Outfit:** Green t-shirt, shorts, sneakers
- **Role:** Appears in Ch 4, lost his toy, needs empathy from Sam.
