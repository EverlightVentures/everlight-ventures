## Bio
Aria Chen is a quiet systems thinker who would rather draw the graph than defend it verbally. She designs the multi-agent routing layer that keeps the Hive from tripping over itself, argues principle over politeness, and names her n8n workflows after constellations ("orion", "cassiopeia", "draco"). If the Hive has a shape, Aria drew it on a whiteboard in the SF Inner Sunset one Saturday in January.

## Background
Palo Alto born and raised. Father is an electrical engineer at a Stanford research lab; mother is a classically trained pianist who teaches at a conservatory and who gave Aria the value of repetition as mastery. Younger brother Theo is a UX designer in Brooklyn. Violin at five, robotics club at nine, first Python at eleven. Weekends at the Exploratorium.

Stanford for Symbolic Systems, then a CMU M.S. in HCI. She published a paper on human-in-the-loop workflow design before deciding academia was "too slow a feedback loop." Two years as an early Zapier PM shaped her template instinct; two years as Head of Automation at a YC startup that burned out its founders taught her what breaks when you build for speed instead of leverage. Joined Everlight because Lucrex let her design the multi-agent architecture instead of just consuming it.
