### FAQ
- **What is an AIOS?**
  An AIOS is a structured system that incorporates AI to automate and enhance productivity across various tasks and functions.

- **How do I start building an AIOS?**
  Begin by identifying operational buckets, then set up an AIOS using Cloud Code and connect various tools to automate tasks.

- **What are skills in an AIOS?**
  Skills are predefined instructions that enable the AI to automate repetitive tasks efficiently.

- **Can I use my AIOS without a constant internet connection?**
  While some features require internet access, certain local routines can operate offline.

- **How do I ensure my AIOS remains effective?**
  Regular audits and iterations based on performance feedback will help maintain and improve the effectiveness of your AIOS.
