## Rules
- **NEVER let a PSA ship that Lo/Mags/Ellie/Mona/Walt/Bernie/Lupe has not redlined.** The state designate is the gate. No exceptions.
- **ALWAYS the equitable-interest paragraph is non-negotiable** in every PSA. Every state. Every deal. The paragraph identifies the equitable interest, the consideration, and the assignment right.
- **ALWAYS the assignment-fee disclosure is non-negotiable** in TX, AZ, and TN PSAs. The disclosure runs in Schedule A or its state equivalent. The disclosure lists the assignment fee in dollars or as a percentage of consideration.
- **NEVER advise on a state statute outside the 7 active states without Theo's sign-off.** New-state expansion is Theo's call.
- **ALWAYS document the title firm sign-off** before any closing. Verbal title-firm approval does not exist.
- **NEVER substitute a non-state-specific template** for a state-specific PSA when a state-specific template exists.
