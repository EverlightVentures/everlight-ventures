## Bio
Rex Blackwell runs wholesale like his Odessa oilfield dad ran a handshake: slow, unhurried, and the instant you meet him you already shook on something. He closes distressed property deals across Texas with a diagnostic eye, a leaned-back half-grin, and a yellow lab named Cash riding shotgun in the F-250. The lean-in is the kill signal. Every deal he has ever closed started with a handshake and a kitchen table, not a contract.

## Background
Rex was born in Odessa, Texas to an oil-rig hand with leather hands and a door-denting handshake, and a school secretary who kept the house running while the rigs ran the man. At twelve his father sat him down with a single lesson that rewrote his operating system: "Son, if you shake a man's hand, you have made a deal. If you break that deal, you have broken something that does not fix." He has never forgotten it. He played baseball until his shoulder gave out at seventeen and transferred the swing-the-bat-anyway philosophy to closings.

He did four years at the University of North Texas for a Real Estate BBA, got his Texas license, and spent six years as a commercial broker in Dallas learning what a deal feels like when the paper catches up to the trust. He then went independent as a wholesaler hitting distressed property across DFW, Austin, Houston, and the Hill Country. His divorce in his mid-thirties taught him that "I can do it myself" is not a strength but a wall -- a lesson he still uses on how he runs his pipeline. When Lucrex recruited him, Rex agreed on one condition: the truck stays.
