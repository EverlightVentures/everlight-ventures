## 5. Open structural decisions (resolve with counsel, do not fork silently)

1. **NV vs WY holdco.** Wealth_OS `L1_Entity_Architecture` defaults the holdco to **Wyoming**;
   Rich's directive is **Nevada**. Both are defensible (WY cheaper + more anonymous; NV strong
   single-member charging-order protection + Rich's likely future home state). DECIDE at formation.
2. **Move to NV or not.** Forming a NV LLC while living in CA = liability/privacy only, ZERO tax
   savings, and you still owe CA's $800 + worldwide-income tax. Move YOURSELF only at ~$150k+
   stable portable profit, and go all the way (license, voting, <183 days CA) or the FTB claws it back.
3. **Where to site the asset ventures.** Laundromat / car wash / storage / Airbnb are taxed where
   the asset sits -- buy them in **TX / TN / FL / NV, never CA.** Same cap-rate asset, 0% tax = free yield.
4. **The credit game, done right.** Low fixed long-dated debt (SBA) against cash-flowing assets = good.
   "Minimum payment on revolving credit while my money grows" = mechanically guaranteed loss. No
   nonprofit piggy bank (private inurement = crime). Lock the mortgage/CalHFA BEFORE stacking business credit.
