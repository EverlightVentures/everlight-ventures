## 3. Why the POOL always covers the payout (the ~90x cushion)
The B-Card fires ~once per **370,000 hands** (1/1,854,799 cards, ~5 cards/hand). With a ~5% house edge, the
house collects around each hit:
`0.05 x avg_SC_bet x 370,000 = ~18,500 x avg_SC_bet` in SC losses from the surrounding play.
The payout is at most `200 x avg_SC_bet` (and hard-capped at 888). So:
`cushion = 18,500x / 200x = ~92x` - the pool collects ~90x more than the biggest payout it funds.
**No single player funds their own jackpot - the POOL does, ~90x over. That is why you never need a purchase gate.**
