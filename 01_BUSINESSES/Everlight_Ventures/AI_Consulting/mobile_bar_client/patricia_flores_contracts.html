1. Scope of Services

1.1 Services. Bartender agrees to provide professional mobile bartending services for the Client’s event as described in the attached Event Booking Form (Exhibit A). Services include:

  • Professional cocktail preparation and beverage service
  • Bar setup and breakdown
  • Drink menu consultation and customization (if applicable)
  • Ice, garnish, and standard bar tool provision (unless otherwise noted)
  • Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) in compliance with California ABC regulations

1.2 Event Types. Services may be provided for private events, corporate functions, weddings, birthday parties, holiday gatherings, and other lawful social occasions.

1.3 Service Area. Yolo County (including Woodland and Davis), Solano County, and San Francisco County, California. Events outside this area may be accommodated with a travel surcharge.

1.4 Exclusions. Bartender does not provide: DJ/entertainment, event planning, catering, venue rental, security, or guest transportation.

2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1 Bartender is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Client.

2.2 Bartender is not entitled to any employee benefits from Client.

2.3 Bartender is solely responsible for all taxes arising from compensation received under this Agreement. Client will issue Form 1099-NEC if payments exceed $600/year.

2.4 The Parties acknowledge California AB5 and the ABC test (Labor Code Section 2775). Bartender represents that: (A) free from Client’s control; (B) performs services outside Client’s usual business; (C) independently established in the same trade.

3. Pricing and Payment

3.1 Hourly Rate: $  per hour, per bartender.

3.2 Minimum Booking: Four (4) hours. Additional hours billed in 30-minute increments.

3.3 Packages: Flat-rate packages per Event Booking Form supersede hourly rate.

3.4 Deposit: Non-refundable deposit of $  or  % due upon signing to secure the date.

3.5 Balance Due: No later than   business days before event. Overtime/extras due within 7 days post-event.

3.6 Accepted Payment: Cash, Zelle, Venmo, CashApp, credit/debit card, check.

3.7 Late Payment: 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or maximum permitted by CA Civil Code Section 1671.

3.8 Overtime: 1.5x standard rate in 30-minute increments. Client must confirm authorization.

4. Liquor Provision

4.1 Client-Provided Alcohol (Patricia's Standard Model). Client supplies all beverages. Bartender advises on quantities upon request.

4.2 Bartender-Provided Alcohol (Optional Add-On). Bartender purchases as Client’s agent. Client reimburses at cost plus  % procurement fee. Receipts provided. Does not constitute alcohol sale. No ABC license required for private events. If event structure changes to include alcohol sales, Client must obtain ABC permit.

4.3 No ABC License Required (Service-Only Model) per CA Business and Professions Code Sections 23300–23399.

4.4 Special Event Permits. Client’s sole responsibility to obtain ABC Daily or Special Event Permits if required.

5. Responsible Beverage Service and ABC Compliance

5.1 RBS Certification per CA Business and Professions Code Section 25681.

5.2 Refusal of Service. Bartender reserves absolute right to refuse service to anyone who:

(a) appears under 21 without valid ID;

(b) presents fraudulent ID;

(c) appears visibly intoxicated per BPC Section 25602;

(d) exhibits aggressive or threatening behavior.

5.3 No Service to Minors. Non-negotiable per BPC Section 25658.

5.4 Client Cooperation. Client shall not pressure Bartender to serve refused persons. Violation constitutes material breach.

5.5 Last Call. 30 minutes before scheduled end of service.

5.6 No Service After 2:00 AM per BPC Section 25631.

6. Liability and Indemnification

6.1 Bartender Insurance. $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate general liability, or $1,000,000 per-event liquor liability. Certificate of Insurance provided upon request.

6.2 Client Indemnification of Bartender. Client indemnifies Bartender against claims arising from:

(a) guest conduct or negligence;

(b) Client’s breach of this Agreement;

(c) failure to obtain required permits;

(d) injury or death not caused by Bartender’s gross negligence;

(e) guest alcohol consumption before or after the event.

6.3 Bartender Indemnification of Client. Bartender indemnifies Client against claims arising from Bartender’s gross negligence or willful misconduct only.

6.4 Limitation of Liability. Bartender’s maximum liability shall not exceed the total fees paid for that event. No indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages.

6.5 Guest Behavior. Client assumes full responsibility for guest behavior, safety, and transportation.

6.6 Venue Liability. Bartender is not responsible for venue damage except damage resulting from Bartender’s direct negligence.

7. Cancellation and Rescheduling

7.1 Cancellation by Client:

(a) 30+ days before event: Full refund LESS deposit.

(b) 7–29 days before event: Forfeit deposit. 50% refund of balance paid.

(c) 48 hours to 6 days before event: No refund.

(d) Less than 48 hours / no-show: 100% of total contracted amount due.

7.2 Rescheduling. One free reschedule with 14+ days’ notice within 90 days. Additional reschedules: $  fee.

7.3 Cancellation by Bartender. Best efforts to provide replacement bartender. Full refund if no suitable replacement is available. Liability limited to refund of amounts paid.

8. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations caused by events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, government restrictions, pandemics, civil unrest, or other acts of God. If a force majeure event continues for 30 or more days, either Party may terminate this Agreement with a full refund of all amounts paid.

9. Setup, Breakdown, and Event Logistics

9.1 Arrival. Bartender will arrive 60 minutes before service start time.

9.2 Bar Equipment Provided by Bartender: bar tools, drinkware, napkins, garnish tools, and portable bar or table covering.

9.3 Client Provides:

(a) bar area with 6-foot table;

(b) electrical outlets if needed;

(c) adequate lighting;

(d) weather protection for outdoor events;

(e) water and ice access;

(f) all beverages, unless Bartender-Provided Alcohol option is selected.

9.4 Breakdown. Bartender will complete breakdown within 60 minutes of service end. Bartender is not responsible for general event cleanup.

10. Gratuity

10.1 Gratuity is not included in the service fee.

10.2 A tip jar is permitted unless Client opts out on the Event Booking Form.

10.3 A pre-arranged gratuity of  % may be added to the invoice.

11. Damage and Breakage

11.1 Client is responsible for damage to Bartender’s equipment caused by guests.

11.2 Bartender will exercise reasonable care with Client’s property.

11.3 Neither Party is liable for damage caused by force majeure events.

12. Dispute Resolution

12.1 The Parties agree to attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days before pursuing other remedies.

12.2 If negotiation fails, the Parties shall submit to mediation in the county where the event was held, or Yolo County if no event occurred.

12.3 If mediation fails, disputes of $12,500 or less shall be resolved in small claims court; disputes exceeding $12,500 shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by JAMS in Sacramento, California.

12.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

12.5 The prevailing Party in any dispute shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs per CA Civil Code Section 1717.

13. General Provisions

13.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with Exhibit A (Event Booking Form) and Exhibit B (Liability Waiver), constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.

13.2 Amendments. This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by both Parties.

13.3 Severability. If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

13.4 Waiver. Failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement.

13.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party’s prior written consent.

13.6 Notices. All notices shall be delivered by email, certified mail, or personal delivery to the addresses listed in this Agreement.

13.7 Headings. Section headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

13.8 Electronic Signatures. Electronic signatures are valid and enforceable per the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (CA Civil Code 1633.1 et seq.) and the federal E-SIGN Act (15 USC 7001 et seq.).

14. Term and Termination

14.1 This Agreement is effective upon signing and continues through completion of all booked events.

14.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate with 30 days’ written notice.

14.3 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately upon material breach, provided the breaching Party is given a 10-day cure period.

14.4 Survival. Sections 2 (Independent Contractor), 6 (Liability), 12 (Dispute Resolution), and 13 (General Provisions) survive termination.

This Liability Waiver (“Waiver”) is attached to and incorporated into the Mobile Bartending Service Agreement identified above.

Recitals

WHEREAS Bartender will provide professional mobile bartending services at Client’s event as described in the Service Agreement and Event Booking Form;

WHEREAS the Event involves the preparation, service, and consumption of alcoholic beverages by adult guests;

WHEREAS Client acknowledges that the service and consumption of alcoholic beverages carry inherent risks including, but not limited to, intoxication, impaired judgment, physical injury, property damage, and potential legal liability;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and in the Service Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Acknowledgment of Responsible Beverage Service

1.1 Bartender holds a valid Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) certification as required by California Business and Professions Code Section 25681.

1.2 Bartender will employ responsible service practices, including but not limited to:

(a) requesting valid government-issued photo identification from any guest who appears to be under 30 years of age;

(b) refusing service to any person under the age of 21, per BPC Section 25658;

(c) refusing service to any person who appears visibly intoxicated, per BPC Section 25602;

(d) monitoring guest alcohol consumption throughout the event;

(e) ceasing service to any individual as deemed appropriate by Bartender’s professional judgment.

1.3 Client shall support and not interfere with any service decisions made by Bartender in accordance with the practices described above.

2. Client Responsibility for Guest Conduct and Safety

2.1 Host Responsibility. Client, as host of the event, assumes primary responsibility for the conduct and safety of all guests, invitees, and attendees.

2.2 Guest Behavior. Bartender is not a security provider and shall not be expected to control, restrain, or manage guests. If Bartender determines that the environment has become unsafe due to guest behavior, Bartender reserves the right to cease service and leave the event immediately without refund.

2.3 Impaired Driving Prevention. Client shall take reasonable steps to prevent intoxicated guests from operating motor vehicles, including but not limited to:

(a) arranging designated drivers;

(b) providing rideshare information (Uber, Lyft, taxi);

(c) arranging overnight accommodations when feasible;

(d) monitoring guest departures.

2.4 Social Host Liability. Client acknowledges the doctrine of social host liability under California Civil Code Section 1714 and accepts responsibility as the host of the event.

3. Assumption of Risk

3.1 Client acknowledges and understands the following risks inherent in events involving alcoholic beverages:

(a) guest intoxication and impaired judgment;

(b) physical injuries resulting from slips, falls, or accidents;

(c) property damage;

(d) allergic reactions to ingredients in beverages;

(e) slip-and-fall hazards related to spilled beverages or wet surfaces;

(f) altercations or disorderly conduct among guests.

3.2 Client voluntarily assumes all risks associated with the event, including the risks described above, except to the extent that such risks arise directly and solely from Bartender’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

4. Hold Harmless and Indemnification

4.1 Hold Harmless. Client agrees to hold harmless and release Bartender, and Bartender’s agents, employees, subcontractors, heirs, and assigns (collectively, “Released Parties”) from any and all claims, demands, actions, causes of action, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of or related to:

(a) the consumption of alcoholic beverages by any guest at the event;

(b) injuries sustained by intoxicated guests or by third parties as a result of an intoxicated guest’s actions;

(c) DUI, traffic accidents, or other incidents involving a guest who consumed alcohol at the event;

(d) the conduct of guests or third parties at the event;

(e) damage to the event venue or third-party property;

(f) Client’s breach of this Waiver or the Service Agreement.

4.2 California Civil Code Section 1542 Waiver. Client expressly waives the provisions of California Civil Code Section 1542, which states:

“A general release does not extend to claims that the creditor or releasing party does not know or suspect to exist in his or her favor at the time of executing the release and that, if known by him or her, would have materially affected his or her settlement with the debtor or released party.”

4.3 Indemnification. Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Released Parties from and against any and all third-party claims, actions, proceedings, damages, judgments, and costs (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of or related to any matter covered by this Waiver.

5. Bartender’s Retained Liability

5.1 Notwithstanding any other provision of this Waiver, Bartender is not released from liability for:

(a) gross negligence or willful misconduct by Bartender;

(b) knowingly serving alcohol to a visibly intoxicated person in violation of BPC Section 25602(a);

(c) knowingly serving alcohol to a minor in violation of BPC Section 25658.

5.2 In all cases, Bartender’s liability is limited as set forth in Section 6.4 of the Service Agreement.

6. Allergies and Dietary Restrictions

6.1 Client acknowledges that cocktails and beverages may contain common allergens, including but not limited to: nuts, dairy, eggs, gluten, soy, and citrus.

6.2 Client is responsible for informing Bartender in writing of any known guest allergies or dietary restrictions prior to the event.

6.3 Client agrees to hold Bartender harmless for allergic reactions or adverse effects from beverages, unless Bartender was specifically informed of the allergy and failed to take reasonable precautions.

7. Photography and Media

7.1 Client grants Bartender permission to take photographs and/or video during the event for use in Bartender’s portfolio, social media, and marketing materials, unless Client opts out below.

OPT OUT: Client does not grant permission for photography or media use.

8. Severability

If any provision of this Waiver is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the validity, legality, and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not be affected or impaired.

9. Governing Law

This Waiver shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, consistent with the dispute resolution provisions of Section 12 of the Service Agreement.

10. Voluntary Execution

Client represents and warrants that:

(a) Client has carefully read this Waiver in its entirety and fully understands its contents;

(b) Client has had the opportunity to consult with independent legal counsel prior to signing;

(c) Client signs this Waiver voluntarily and without coercion;

(d) this Waiver is binding upon Client and Client’s heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns;

(e) Client is at least 18 years of age and legally competent to enter into this Waiver.
