# Liquidity Crisis Engine
## Never Sell at the Wrong Time

**The cardinal sin of wealth management: forced sales during market crashes. Pre-positioned liquidity prevents this. This engine maintains 5 separate liquidity sources so you ALWAYS have access to cash without triggering tax or selling assets at depressed prices.**

---

## The 5-Layer Liquidity Stack

### Layer 1: Operating Cash (Immediate, $5-50k)
- Business checking account, accessible same-day
- Personal checking, accessible same-day
- Target: 30-60 days of operating expenses
- Yield: low (high-yield savings ~4-5% APY today)

### Layer 2: Cash Reserve (Same-week, $50-500k+)
- High-yield savings (Marcus, Ally, Live Oak)
- Treasury Direct (T-bills 4-6%, fully government-backed)
- Money market mutual funds (Vanguard VMFXX, Fidelity SPRXX, Schwab SWVXX)
- Target: 6-12 months of full lifestyle expenses
- Yield: 4-5% currently

### Layer 3: SBLOC -- Securities-Backed Line of Credit (Same-day, up to 50% of taxable brokerage)
- Activated at T5 (typically with $250k+ taxable brokerage)
- Same-day draw against pledged investment account
- Rate: SOFR + 0.5-2% (currently ~5.5-7%)
- Use only for emergencies or opportunistic deals
- Conservative LTV: 25-35% maximum

### Layer 4: HELOC (Same-week, primary residence equity)
- Activated at T3-T5 if home equity supports
- Variable rate, currently 7-9%
- Up to 80-90% combined LTV
- Documentation up-front while not needed (banks reluctant in crisis)

### Layer 5: Cash-Value Life Insurance Loan (Same-week, T6+)
- Whole life policy from T3+ has accessible cash value at T6+
- Borrow at 4-6% interest (often less than HELOC/SBLOC)
- Cash value continues earning dividends
- No repayment schedule required
- Most stable liquidity source -- not market-correlated

---

## Pre-Positioning Doctrine

**Banks lend most willingly when you DON'T NEED IT.** Pre-position lines of credit during good times:

- HELOC: open at T3-T5 with no balance. Available if needed.
- SBLOC: open at T5 with $0 balance. Available if needed.
- Whole life policy: open at T3 with small premium. Cash value available years later.
- Business line of credit: open with bank early in business relationship.

**Do NOT wait until crisis to apply.** A 2008-style market crash = banks tighten credit. Existing lines stay open; new applications get rejected.

## Crisis Scenarios + Response

### Scenario 1: Personal Liquidity Need ($10-50k for emergency, opportunity, etc.)
**Response:**
1. Check Layer 1-2 first. If sufficient, use it.
2. If not, draw from SBLOC at conservative LTV.
3. Pay back from next quarter's income.

### Scenario 2: Business Cash Flow Gap ($50-500k for AR aging, expansion bridge, etc.)
**Response:**
1. Check Layer 2 (cash reserve).
2. Draw business line of credit if pre-positioned.
3. Bridge with SBLOC if necessary.
4. Last resort: factor receivables (expensive, ~30% APR).

### Scenario 3: Major Market Crash (S&P -40%, Real Estate -25%)
**Response:**
1. DO NOT sell investments at depressed prices.
2. Cut discretionary spending immediately.
3. Live off Layer 1-2 cash reserves (12-month runway).
4. Selectively use Layer 3-5 if needed.
5. Watch for opportunistic asset acquisitions during the crash (this is when wealth is BUILT).

### Scenario 4: Forced Liquidity Event (Lawsuit, divorce, IRS levy)
**Response:**
1. Engage attorney IMMEDIATELY (asset protection, family law, or tax controversy).
2. Do NOT move assets in panic (fraudulent transfer concerns).
3. Use pre-existing structures (DAPT, Holdco) -- they protect what's already in them.
4. Layer 4-5 borrowing OK as long as not in fraudulent transfer.

### Scenario 5: Major Health / Family Crisis ($100k-$1M+ unexpected need)
**Response:**
1. Layer 5 (whole life policy loans) -- best for sustained need.
2. HSA tax-free reimbursement of past medical expenses (if receipts saved).
3. Roth IRA contributions (not earnings) accessible without tax/penalty.
4. SBLOC for bridge.
5. NEVER tap retirement accounts before exhausting other layers.

---

## What NOT To Do In a Liquidity Crisis

1. **Sell investments in a market crash.** Realize losses at the bottom = locked-in damage.
2. **Tap retirement accounts.** 10% penalty + ordinary income tax = lose 30-50% of every dollar withdrawn.
3. **Take payday-style loans.** APRs 100%+ destroy wealth.
4. **Sell real estate in a downturn.** Fire-sale pricing.
5. **Skip professional consultation.** $500/hr for an attorney pales next to wealth lost in crisis decisions.
6. **Move assets in panic.** Fraudulent transfer rules unwinds it later, costs more.

## Stress Test Checklist (Run Annually)

```
[ ] If S&P drops 50% tomorrow, can I cover 12 months of life expenses without selling?
[ ] If business revenue drops 80%, can I cover business expenses for 6 months?
[ ] If interest rates rise 300 bps, can I service my variable-rate debt?
[ ] If I can't access bank loans for 2 years, do I have alternative liquidity?
[ ] If forced to sell ONE asset class, which would I sell first? Last?
[ ] What's my margin call threshold on SBLOC? Have I left enough cushion?
[ ] When did I last review my emergency contact list (attorneys, advisors, banks)?
```

If any answer is "no," fix it BEFORE the crisis arrives.
