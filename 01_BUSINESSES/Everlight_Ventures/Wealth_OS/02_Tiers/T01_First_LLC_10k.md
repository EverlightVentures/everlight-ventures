# T1 -- First Entity Stack ($10-25k Net Worth)
## The Activation Tier

**Trigger:** First wholesale commission close, estimated $10-15k. Or any $10k+ income event from any source.

**Posture:** Move FAST. Most professionals bill hourly, but 90% of T1 setup costs are one-time fees that pay back within 12 months via tax savings. Free-path-only doctrine eases here -- this is the tier where strategic spend begins.

---

## The 7 T1 Moves

### 1. Engage CPA + Business Attorney (Within 30 Days of Trigger)

From your T0 professional shortlist. Confirm:
- Multi-entity / S-corp expertise
- Familiarity with R&D credit
- Comfortable with Wyoming / Delaware filings
- Reasonable monthly retainer ($300-800 for CPA, project-based for attorney)

**Initial spend:** $2-5k attorney for entity setup, $300-500/mo CPA retainer.

### 2. Form Holdco LLC in Wyoming

**Name:** Everlight Holdings LLC (or similar)
**State:** Wyoming
**Annual fee:** $50
**Members:** You (or eventually a trust) as 100% owner
**Manager:** You
**Registered agent:** Wyoming Registered Agent service ($50-150/yr)
**Anonymity:** Wyoming does not require member disclosure on public filings

**Purpose:** Sits at the top of the entity structure. Owns Operating LLC, IP LLC, future Real Estate LLCs.

### 3. Form Operating LLC

**Name:** Everlight Operating LLC (or similar -- can be doing-business-as)
**State:** California (or Wyoming with CA foreign qualification, depending on activity volume in CA)
**Members:** Holdco LLC (100%) -- this is a single-member disregarded entity for tax
**Manager:** You

**Purpose:** Where the actual business activity happens (wholesale, services, content sales).

**Tax treatment:** Disregarded by default. **At T3 ($100k+):** elect S-Corp via Form 2553 for self-employment tax savings.

### 4. Form IP LLC in Wyoming

**Name:** Everlight IP LLC (or similar)
**State:** Wyoming
**Members:** Holdco LLC (100%)

**Purpose:** Holds trademarks, copyrights, software code, brand IP. License agreements with Operating LLC charge royalties (deductible to Op, ordinary income to IP, low-tax-state).

**Note:** Don't fully activate IP licensing until T4 ($250k). At T1 just exists as a placeholder entity.

### 5. Decide on QSBS C-Corp Track

For each exit-track business (Hive Mind SaaS, Onyx, Vantaris):

**If pursuing QSBS Sec 1202 path:**
- Form as **Delaware C-corporation**
- Issue founder stock at incorporation (this STARTS the 5-yr QSBS clock)
- Document gross assets at issuance (must be <$50M)
- Maintain proper corporate records (board minutes, written consents, annual filings)
- Annual fees: ~$300 DE franchise tax + agent fees

**Cost:** $1-3k attorney per C-corp formation. Worth millions if any exit.

**Common error to avoid:** Forming as LLC and converting later. The QSBS 5-yr clock RESETS at conversion. Lose 60%+ of the benefit.

### 6. Banking + Bookkeeping Setup

**Banking:**
- Business checking for Operating LLC (Mercury, Relay, or local bank)
- Business savings for tax reserve
- Separate business credit card (Chase Ink Business Cash, AmEx Blue Business Cash) for clean expense separation

**Bookkeeping:**
- Wave (free) or QuickBooks Online Simple Start ($30/mo)
- Set up chart of accounts with CPA
- Connect bank feeds for auto-import
- Categorize transactions weekly

**Critical:** NEVER pay personal expenses from business account. NEVER deposit personal income to business account. Single biggest "veil-piercing" failure point.

### 7. R&D Credit Retroactive Filing

**Engage R&D credit specialist** (alliantgroup, KBKG, McGuire Sponsel, Source Advisors). Contingency 15-25%.

**Process:**
- Provide T0 R&D documentation spreadsheet
- Specialist conducts study, drafts technical memorandum
- File Form 6765 with current return AND amended returns for 2024-2026
- Credit applied against current tax OR (for startups <5 yrs old, <$5M revenue) against payroll tax up to $500k/yr

**Estimated value:** $7-40k initial claim, plus annual ongoing claims of similar magnitude.

## The Augusta Rule (Activate Now)

With Operating LLC formed:
- Hold 14 days/yr of business meetings at home
- Document: agenda, attendees, business purpose, rent comp study
- Invoice from you (personal) to Operating LLC for daily rent
- Operating LLC writes check to you, deducts as business expense
- You report rent on Form 1040 but exclude under Section 280A(g)
- Net result: $5,000-30,000 of tax-free shifted income/yr

**Required documents (kept in `04_Dispatch_Log/Augusta/`):**
- Daily rent comp study (3 local hotel meeting room rates)
- Annual calendar of meeting dates with agendas
- Invoices from you to Operating LLC
- Cancelled checks / bank transfer records

## What You Are NOT Doing at T1

- **Not S-corp election yet** (need $80k+ profit for the salary/distribution math to work -- defer to T3)
- **Not opening DAPT** (no assets to fund, no creditor exposure -- T5 move)
- **Not opening any trust beyond Revocable Living Trust** (estate too small to justify -- T6+)
- **Not deploying captive insurance** (need $500k+ revenue and real risks -- T5+)
- **Not changing personal domicile** (premature, do at T6 with major liquidity event -- but track CA days now)
- **Not borrowing against anything** (no assets to pledge yet)
