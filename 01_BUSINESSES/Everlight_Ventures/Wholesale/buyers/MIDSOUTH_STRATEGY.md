## The Gap All 3 Missed (Marcus catch)

**TN Code 62-13-104 surety bond exposure.** Justine flagged it earlier today: $50,000 surety bond required for "engaged in the business of" assigning real estate purchase contracts in TN. **One deal = arguably one-off. Five+ deals = "engaged in the business of."**

If we ship MSHB 10+ deals over 90 days as the relationship matures, **Marquise is operating without the bond** that statute requires. Exposure = each unbonded assignment is technically a violation; civil + administrative penalties under TN DCI authority. Marquise as sole prop = personal liability stack.

**Mitigation options:**
- **A. Cap at "one-off" volume** -- ship 2-3 MSHB deals max in 90 days, defer scale-up until bond posted.
- **B. Post the $50k surety bond** before deal #4. Most TN surety bonds for this rule cost ~$500-1,500/yr in premium (depending on credit) plus $5k+ collateral if credit-impaired. From first-deal commission, this becomes feasible.
- **C. Operate as agent-of-buyer (Chris) instead of as principal-assignor** -- different statutory posture; no bond required as buyer's agent. Requires written agency agreement with MSHB.

**Penny would have weighed in here on cost-benefit but her dispatch failed.** Recommended path pending Penny review: **Option B** -- post the bond after first commission funds. Until then, cap at 2-3 deals total to MSHB.
