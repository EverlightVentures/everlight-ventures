- [00:04:00] **Connecting Delays to Spatial Properties**
  - The speaker proposes a method to define delays as Euclidean distances between neurons, leading to a novel delay gradient update rule.
  - Key properties of the learnable delay matrix include:
    - Zero diagonals, indicating immediate updates for auto-synaptic connections.
    - Symmetry, where the delay from neuron I to J equals the delay from J to I.
    - Triangular inequality, ensuring the shortest path is the direct connection between neurons.
