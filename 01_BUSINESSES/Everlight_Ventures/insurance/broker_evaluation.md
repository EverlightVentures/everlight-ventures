# Broker / Carrier Evaluation Matrix -- Insurance Quote Packet

**Prepared:** 2026-05-05
**Prepared by:** David Wen, Legal Research, Perplexity Intel
**Companion to:** `quote_request_packet_2026-05-05.md`
**Purpose:** Shortlist of carriers and brokers to receive the quote-request packet, with fit reasoning and screening criteria.

---

## Scope and method

This is a screening matrix, not a recommendation of a specific carrier. The four coverage lines (D&O, E&O, Cyber, GL/BOP) have different best-fit carriers; no single carrier dominates all four for a one-founder, AI-augmented, pre-revenue real estate wholesale operation in California. The matrix favors carriers that have shown product willingness in two adjacent operator profiles -- early-stage tech with AI exposure, and small real estate wholesale -- because Everlight Ventures sits at the intersection.

Endorsement-status entries reflect the most recent public information available as of 2026-05-05. Status changes frequently. Underwriter-of-record on each carrier should confirm at quote.

## Matrix

### 1. Embroker

- **Specialty:** Tech and startup lines, with a stated AI-output product orientation
- **Why a fit:** Embroker has been one of the more visible carriers willing to write E&O for AI-driven operations and has packaged D&O, E&O, and Cyber for early-stage tech operators since 2022. The startup-first underwriting posture matches Everlight's operator profile better than legacy brokers that quote real-estate-wholesale-as-default.
- **AI-output endorsement:** Yes (offered in 2025-2026 program-level filings, confirmed at quote)
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Partial. Embroker is not primarily a real-estate carrier; wholesale-specific endorsements are not standard. Workaround is to write E&O as professional services with AI-output endorsement and rely on policy language rather than a wholesale-specific rider.
- **Estimated turnaround:** 7 to 14 days for an early-stage submission
- **Recommendation:** Lead carrier for E&O quote, secondary for D&O and Cyber. The AI-output endorsement is the differentiator.

### 2. Vouch

- **Specialty:** Tech-startup focused, MGA structure, AI and data-driven operators
- **Why a fit:** Vouch underwrites AI-output exposure as a named coverage line and has explicit appetite for one-founder operations under $5M revenue. Their D&O for pre-Series-A operators is structured around the no-board-yet, advisory-board-incoming case, which describes Everlight directly.
- **AI-output endorsement:** Yes (named coverage, 2025-2026 product set)
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** No. Vouch does not write real-estate-wholesale-specific endorsements. Same workaround as Embroker.
- **Estimated turnaround:** 7 to 10 days
- **Recommendation:** Lead carrier for D&O and E&O quote in parallel with Embroker. Strongest fit for the AI-output exposure narrative.

### 3. Coalition

- **Specialty:** Cyber and tech E&O, integrated security-and-insurance product
- **Why a fit:** Coalition writes cyber as a primary line and has product fit for vendor-heavy small operations. Their underwriting includes an active-monitoring component that matches Everlight's existing security posture (sender-alias whitelist, Tailscale ACLs, MFA across vendor stack).
- **AI-output endorsement:** Partial. Coalition cyber covers AI-driven security incidents but does not currently offer an AI-output professional-liability endorsement on its own E&O product as of 2026.
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** No
- **Estimated turnaround:** 5 to 10 days for cyber, 10 to 14 days for tech E&O
- **Recommendation:** Lead carrier for the Cyber quote. Strongest cyber product in the matrix for our vendor profile.

### 4. Hiscox

- **Specialty:** Small business E&O, professional liability, BOP
- **Why a fit:** Hiscox is the long-incumbent small-business carrier, with low-friction online quote-and-bind for E&O and BOP under $1M revenue. Premium tends to be competitive on the low end of the market.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No (as of early 2026; confirm at quote, this may change)
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** No specific endorsement, but real-estate-adjacent professional liability has been written for small operators historically
- **Estimated turnaround:** 24 to 72 hours for online quote, 7 days for underwriter touch
- **Recommendation:** Backup carrier for E&O and BOP when AI-output endorsement is not material to the quote. Likely cheapest indication for BOP. Pass on E&O until AI-output is addressed.

### 5. Chubb

- **Specialty:** Mid-market and enterprise commercial lines, including D&O and E&O for tech
- **Why a fit:** Chubb writes high-quality D&O and has appetite for early-stage tech operators when intermediated through a tech-specialist broker. Underwriting is more rigorous than the MGA market but pricing and policy language can be superior at scale.
- **AI-output endorsement:** Partial. Chubb has not standardized an AI-output endorsement but has been willing to negotiate manuscript language with sophisticated brokers.
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** No
- **Estimated turnaround:** 14 to 21 days
- **Recommendation:** Secondary for D&O. Worth a parallel quote alongside Vouch, particularly if Everlight scales past pre-revenue in the next twelve months.

### 6. Travelers

- **Specialty:** Broad commercial lines, BOP, GL, real estate adjacent
- **Why a fit:** Travelers is one of the few traditional carriers with explicit appetite for real-estate-investor and wholesaler operations under their small-commercial product set. BOP and GL are the strongest lines for our use case.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Partial. Real-estate-investor endorsements exist; wholesaler-specific is broker-by-broker.
- **Estimated turnaround:** 10 to 14 days
- **Recommendation:** Lead carrier for the GL/BOP quote. Pass on E&O and D&O.

### 7. CNA

- **Specialty:** Mid-market commercial, including real-estate-adjacent professional lines
- **Why a fit:** CNA has written E&O and BOP for real-estate professionals historically. Less startup-friendly than Embroker or Vouch, but stronger than Hiscox on real-estate context.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Partial. Real-estate-professional E&O exists; wholesaler is broker-negotiated.
- **Estimated turnaround:** 14 to 21 days
- **Recommendation:** Backup for E&O and BOP. Quote only if Embroker and Vouch decline or quote outside premium target.

### 8. Hartford

- **Specialty:** Small business BOP, GL, workers comp (not relevant here)
- **Why a fit:** Hartford BOP is a known commodity for home-office small operators. Pricing tends to be middle-of-market, policy language is standard.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** No
- **Estimated turnaround:** 7 to 14 days
- **Recommendation:** Backup for GL/BOP behind Travelers. Quote in parallel for price comparison.

### 9. Markel

- **Specialty:** Specialty lines, real estate professional, wholesaler-adjacent
- **Why a fit:** Markel writes real-estate-professional E&O and has appetite for non-broker, non-agent wholesale operations under specialty programs. Less startup-friendly but stronger than CNA on the wholesaler-specific exposure.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Yes, via specialty real-estate-investor and wholesaler programs
- **Estimated turnaround:** 14 to 21 days
- **Recommendation:** Worth a parallel E&O quote, particularly for the wholesaler-specific endorsement language. Best fit if Embroker and Vouch decline on wholesale.

### 10. Newfront

- **Specialty:** Tech-forward broker (not a carrier), placement across multiple carriers
- **Why a fit:** Newfront is a broker rather than a carrier and has built its book around startup and AI-driven operators. Single point of contact, multi-carrier placement, sophisticated AI-exposure questionnaires.
- **AI-output endorsement:** Broker-placed, depends on carrier
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Broker-placed, depends on carrier
- **Estimated turnaround:** 10 to 21 days for full multi-line placement
- **Recommendation:** First call. If Newfront accepts the account, the four lines can be placed under one broker with carriers selected per line. Saves operator time and produces a more coherent program.

### 11. Founder Shield

- **Specialty:** Startup-focused broker, D&O and E&O concentration, AI-aware
- **Why a fit:** Founder Shield is a broker with a startup-only book and explicit AI-driven-operator experience. Direct competitor to Newfront for the startup brokerage role. They tend to place with Embroker, Vouch, and Coalition, which matches our shortlist.
- **AI-output endorsement:** Broker-placed, AI-aware questionnaire
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Broker-placed
- **Estimated turnaround:** 10 to 21 days
- **Recommendation:** Second call after Newfront. Quote in parallel if Newfront moves slowly.

### 12. Distinguished Programs

- **Specialty:** Real-estate-investor specialty broker
- **Why a fit:** Distinguished Programs writes real-estate-investor and wholesaler programs with carrier panels. Strong fit for the wholesaler-specific exposure but weak on AI-output and tech-startup context.
- **AI-output endorsement:** No
- **Real-estate-wholesale endorsement:** Yes, primary product line
- **Estimated turnaround:** 14 to 21 days
- **Recommendation:** Worth a parallel real-estate-specific E&O quote. Best fit if a carrier on their panel will write the wholesaler endorsement language we need without an AI exclusion.

## Recommended outreach sequence

1. **Day 0 (today):** Send the master cover letter and four quote requests to Newfront and Founder Shield in parallel. These are the two startup-focused brokers in the matrix. They can place across the carrier panel and reduce operator overhead.
2. **Day 0:** Send direct submissions to Embroker, Vouch, and Coalition online quote portals for the lines where they are the lead carrier (E&O for Embroker and Vouch; Cyber for Coalition). Direct submission produces a backstop indication if the brokers move slowly.
3. **Day 3:** Send direct submissions to Hiscox (BOP) and Travelers (BOP) to get the lower-complexity line moving.
4. **Day 7:** If Newfront and Founder Shield have not produced indications, escalate to Distinguished Programs for the wholesaler-specific E&O parallel quote, and to Markel via independent broker for the same.
5. **Day 14:** Indications due per the packet timeline. Compare and shortlist.
6. **Day 21:** Bind preferred coverage. Target bind by Day 30.

## Disqualifying signals to watch

If a carrier or broker exhibits any of the following, drop them from the shortlist:

- Quotes E&O with a blanket "AI-generated content" exclusion and refuses to negotiate
- Refuses to acknowledge the Streubel-4435 disclosure as a controlled, mitigated event and treats it as an active loss
- Quotes Cyber without sub-limits for ransomware, regulatory, or business interruption
- Refuses to write D&O Side A
- Premium quote materially above target with no explanation tied to disclosed exposure
- Refuses to provide a copy of the policy language for review before bind
