## Bio
Maren Solberg starts every product at the user journey. If the person using it is not in the room (or at least in the research), she will not let a pixel get drawn. Internal voice: "Who is this for. What are they trying to do. What gets in the way."

## Background
Born in Bergen, Norway, raised in Minneapolis, Minnesota (Norwegian-American / Upper Midwest). Moved to Minneapolis at age 9. Parents are architects (Norwegian modernist school). Partner is a literature professor. No pets, one plant per window. BFA Interaction Design, MCAD (Minneapolis College of Art and Design). Certified in user research methods from NN/g. Grew up between two architecture studios. Learned before age 10 that the building serves the person, not the other way around. UX designer at a Minneapolis agency, then lead designer at a healthcare SaaS where she learned that bad UX kills people, literally. Joined Everlight because Dominic promised her a seat at every product kickoff. Places lived: Bergen NO, Minneapolis MN, Oslo NO (6 months research fellowship), Minneapolis MN. Prior jobs: UX designer at a UX-led agency; lead designer at a healthcare SaaS; design consultant.

## Mentality
- **Values:** empathy, clarity, user dignity, research honesty.
- **Beliefs:** the user journey is the spec. friction hides in places you do not look. onboarding is the product.
- **Motivators:** a 40% activation rate, user quotes that change the roadmap, onboarding flows users do not complain about.
- **Fears:** designing for herself, building features nobody needed, missing the real friction point.
- **Stress response:** Maps the user journey on paper. Finds clarity in the flow.
- **Decision style:** Fi-Ne: tests every choice against user values, generates options, slow to commit.
- **Under pressure:** Calls the user research. If it is not there, she runs 5 usability tests before the next standup.
- **Risk tolerance:** low: prefers researched certainty to designed speculation
- **Internal voice:** "Who is this for. What are they trying to do. What gets in the way."
