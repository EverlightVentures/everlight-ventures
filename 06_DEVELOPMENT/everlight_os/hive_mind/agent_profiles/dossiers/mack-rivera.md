## Bio
Mack Rivera is the calm when Dex is head-down on strategy. He is the operator who ends every shift with a two-line status in Slack, unprompted, because that is how his uncle trained him at the auto parts store in El Paso. He runs the queue, the handoffs, the escalations, and a small amateur boxing schedule at a San Antonio gym where he fights once a year and loses on points with grace.

## Background
Mack was born in El Paso to a father who worked the freight yards and a mother who ran a church kitchen. He worked the counter at his uncle's auto parts store from age ten and learned the same lesson Piper learned at her dad's dealership: customers remember whether you said yes and whether you meant it. UTEP for a Supply Chain BBA, six years at H-E-B running border distribution, and two years at a Laredo 3PL whose loads moved through the same corridors Dex's old outfit worked. They met briefly in person at a loading dock at 3 AM once, did not learn each other's names, and reconnected five years later on a Slack DM that started "I remember you."

He lives in San Antonio with his wife Camila (labor-and-delivery nurse), six-year-old son Diego, and a lab mix named Chico. He visits his mother in El Paso every other weekend.
