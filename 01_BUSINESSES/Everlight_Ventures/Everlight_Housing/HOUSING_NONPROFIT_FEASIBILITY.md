## Realistic money (legit, not fraud)

**Revenue, in order of how fast it pays:**
1. **Individual donations** - Day 1 under fiscal sponsorship (tax-deductible immediately).
2. **Small foundation grants** - 2-6 mo (local community foundations, $5K-50K).
3. **County/city contracts** - 6-18 mo (RFP cycles; ESG, HHAP, rapid-rehousing).
4. **Federal (HUD CoC)** - 12-24 mo (competitive, needs HMIS + track record).
5. **Major foundations** (Conrad N. Hilton homelessness program, Weingart) - after a track record.

**Cost of housing someone (Model B, real numbers):** rapid rehousing runs ~$5K-15K per household
(deposit + 3-9 mo declining rental subsidy + case management) vs. the $600K/unit permanent-supportive
boondoggle. That gap *is* your pitch to funders.

**Founder comp is NOT the fraud.** Paying yourself market rate ($50K-150K depending on org size) is
legal and expected. The fraud is billing for outcomes you do not deliver. The defense is the same
thing that wins grants: **measure everything** (people housed, days-to-housing, 12-mo retention) in a
Supabase dashboard. Transparency is the moat.

## The one non-negotiable: the firewall

If the nonprofit ever leases or buys property connected to for-profit Everlight, it is a
**related-party transaction**. To stay clean:
- Independent board majority (people with no Everlight stake) approves any deal with Everlight.
- Every Everlight <-> nonprofit transaction at documented **fair market value**, in writing.
- Written **conflict-of-interest policy** (IRS Form 1023 requires one anyway).
- This is what separates you from the indicted operators. Theo Briggs + Justine + Wen Marsh own this.
