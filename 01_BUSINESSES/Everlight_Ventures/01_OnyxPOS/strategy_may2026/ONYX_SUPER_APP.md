## THE 12 PILLARS (what the app actually does)

### 1. ONYX WALLET (Cash App + Chime)
- **Balance + P2P payments.** Send money to friends instantly.
- **Onyx Card** (virtual debit): Spend anywhere, earn 1% back everywhere, 5% back at Onyx merchants.
- **Early paycheck:** Direct deposit hits 2 days early (same as Chime). For gig workers + hourly employees at Onyx merchants.
- **Round-ups:** Every purchase rounds up to nearest dollar. Spare change goes into a savings pot or buys fractional crypto.
- **Daily hook:** Check balance. See cashback posted. See friends' activity.
- **Revenue:** Interchange fees on card transactions (1.5%), instant transfer fees ($1.50), ATM partnership.

### 2. CASHBACK ENGINE (Upside + Ibotta + Rakuten)
- **Claim offers before you shop.** "15% back at Joe's Coffee today." Tap to claim, tap to pay, cashback posts overnight.
- **Receipt scan fallback:** If merchant isn't NFC-equipped, snap a photo. CV pipeline extracts the purchase, matches to claimed offer, posts cashback.
- **Boosted offers:** Merchants pay to boost their offers to the top. Self-serve ad platform. $5 minimum, pay-per-redemption.
- **Stacking:** Combine cashback + loyalty points + lottery code on one transaction. Triple-dip.
- **Daily hook:** New offers every morning. FOMO on expiring deals.
- **Revenue:** Merchants pay 5-15% cashback (they set it), Onyx takes 2-3% platform fee on top.

### 3. RECEIPT LOTTERY (Taiwan model + scratch-offs)
- **Every purchase = a scratch-off.** Animated scratch card in app after every transaction.
- **Prizes:** $1-$100 store credit, free items, bonus points, rare NFT collectibles.
- **Jackpot:** Monthly neighborhood jackpot funded by all participating merchants. $500-$5,000.
- **Social proof:** Winners auto-shared to local feed (with permission). "Sarah just won $50 at Joe's Coffee!"
- **Funded by:** 0.1-0.5% of each transaction (merchant sets it). Tiny cost, massive engagement.
- **Daily hook:** Scratch your card. Check if you won. See who else won.
- **Revenue:** Already funded by merchant contribution. Drives repeat visits.

### 4. PREDICTION MARKET (DraftKings + Polymarket + bar trivia)
- **Bet with points, not cash.** Legal everywhere. Same dopamine.
- **Sports:** Tonight's game lines. Prop bets. "Will Steph hit 5 threes?"
- **Local:** "Will the taco truck sell out before 2 PM?" "How many coffees will Joe's sell today?"
- **Pop culture:** "Who wins Best Picture?" "Will Drake drop this Friday?"
- **Weather:** "Over/under 85°F tomorrow?" (ties into smart pricing)
- **Merchant-sponsored:** The bar sponsors tonight's game prediction. Winner gets a free round.
- **Live leaderboard** on the bar's TV (via Onyx merchant dashboard).
- **Daily hook:** Set predictions. Watch games. Collect winnings.
- **Revenue:** Merchants sponsor events (promoted predictions). Platform takes 5% vig on point pools.

### 5. DROP CULTURE (SNKRS + StockX + Supreme)
- **Limited releases from local boutiques.** 50 units. Drops Friday at noon. Waitlist open now.
- **Raffle system:** Join waitlist, higher tier = more entries. Obsidian members get early access.
- **Resale marketplace:** Bought a drop? List it for resale to other Onyx users. Price discovery.
- **Collab drops:** Two merchants collaborate. "Joe's Coffee x Street Boutique: Limited mug + tee bundle."
- **Hype notifications:** Countdown timers. "Dropping in 3 hours." Push notifications.
- **Auction drops:** Bid with loyalty points. Highest bidder wins.
- **Daily hook:** Browse upcoming drops. Check waitlist status. Flex your cops.
- **Revenue:** 10% resale fee. Merchants pay $50 to feature a drop.

### 6. SOCIAL FEED + CLOUT (TikTok Shop + BeReal + Strava)
- **Local commerce feed.** Not global influencers -- your actual friends and neighbors.
- **Purchase flexes:** "Just copped the limited drop at Street Boutique 🔥" (auto-generated card)
- **Lottery wins:** "WON $50 at Joe's Coffee!" with scratch-off animation replay
- **Prediction wins:** "Called Lakers -4.5. Collected 1,200 points." with Robinhood-style confetti
- **Streak milestones:** "30-day streak at 4 merchants! Gold tier unlocked."
- **Reviews + photos:** Rate your purchase. Photo of your meal. Becomes content for the merchant.
- **Creator tier:** Top clout users become "Onyx Ambassadors." Free stuff from merchants. Affiliate commissions.
- **Daily hook:** Scroll feed. Flex purchases. React to friends' wins.
- **Revenue:** Sponsored posts from merchants. Ambassador affiliate fees.

### 7. VOICE / AI COMMERCE (ChatGPT + DoorDash + Alexa)
- **Text or voice order from any Onyx merchant.**
- "Hey Onyx, order my usual from Joe's." → AI knows your history. Places order. Pays from wallet.
- "What's good near me for lunch under $15?" → AI recommends based on your taste + current deals.
- **Group orders:** "Create a group order for the office." Friends add items via link.
- **Pre-staging:** AI tells the merchant what you'll probably order before you arrive (predictive commerce).
- **Daily hook:** Conversational commerce. Never browse a menu again.
- **Revenue:** Order processing fee (3%). Premium AI features for subscribers.

### 8. SPORTS + WATCH PARTIES (FanDuel + Vivid Seats + Meetup)
- **Find bars/restaurants showing tonight's game.** Map view.
- **Reserve a spot** at bars running Onyx. Pre-order food.
- **Live prediction sync:** Your predictions show on the bar's screen. Leaderboard updates in real-time.
- **Watch party hosting:** Merchants create events. "Lakers Watch Party at O'Malley's. First 20 get free wings."
- **Ticket marketplace:** Resell tickets to local events (concerts, sports, comedy shows).
- **Fantasy leagues:** Create leagues with friends. Season-long. Weekly prizes from local merchants.
- **Daily hook:** Check scores. See tonight's events. Join watch parties.
- **Revenue:** Event promotion fees. Ticket marketplace commission (10%). Sponsored watch parties.

### 9. FASHION + STYLE (SHEIN + Depop + Poshmark + The RealReal)
- **Local boutique discovery.** Not fast fashion -- actual shops in your neighborhood.
- **Style profiles:** Set your size, style preferences, budget range. Get curated recommendations.
- **Try-before-you-buy:** Reserve items at nearby boutiques. Try on in-store. No commitment.
- **Secondhand marketplace:** List your clothes for resale. Local pickup (no shipping).
- **Outfit of the day:** Post your fit. Tag which Onyx merchants you're wearing. Earn clout.
- **Stylist AI:** "I need an outfit for a first date, budget $150." AI shops your local merchants.
- **Daily hook:** Browse new arrivals. Post fits. Shop local.
- **Revenue:** 12% resale commission. Merchant feature placement fees. Stylist AI premium.

### 10. LOCAL DEALS MAP (GasBuddy + Google Maps + Yelp killer)
- **Real-time deals map.** See every active offer, flash sale, and boosted deal near you.
- **Gas prices** (yes, include gas stations as merchants).
- **Happy hours** auto-listed from bar merchants.
- **Flash deals:** Merchants push time-limited offers. "Next 30 minutes: BOGO at the pizza place."
- **Surge alerts:** "Joe's Coffee is slow right now. 20% off for the next hour." (inverse Uber surge)
- **Walking directions** to nearest deal.
- **Daily hook:** Check the map before going anywhere. Always find a deal.
- **Revenue:** Boosted pins ($3/day for merchants). Premium map placement.

### 11. SAVINGS + INVESTING (Robinhood + Acorns + Coinbase Earn)
- **Round-up investing.** Spare change from every purchase → savings pot or fractional crypto/stocks.
- **Learn-to-earn:** Watch 60-second videos about local businesses. Earn points. (Coinbase Earn model)
- **Savings goals:** "Save $500 for holiday shopping." Auto-save from cashback.
- **Merchant gift card investing:** Buy $50 gift cards for $45 (merchant subsidizes for guaranteed future revenue).
- **Points-to-crypto:** Convert loyalty points to XLM, BTC, ETH at market rates.
- **Daily hook:** Watch balance grow. Learn and earn. Track savings goals.
- **Revenue:** Crypto exchange spread (1.5%). Gift card float. Investment product referral fees.

### 12. STREAKS + GAMIFICATION + TIERS (Duolingo + Strava + airline status)
- **Visit streak:** Consecutive days visiting ANY Onyx merchant. 7-day streak = 2x points.
- **Merchant streaks:** Visit the same shop 5 days straight. Unlock a secret menu item / exclusive deal.
- **Weekly challenges:** "Visit 3 different merchants this week. Earn 500 bonus points."
- **Seasonal events:** "Summer of Onyx: Hit 10 merchants in July. Win a $100 Onyx credit."
- **Tier progression:**
  - Bronze (0pts): Basic cashback, 1 lottery entry
  - Silver (500pts): 1.5x cashback, priority waitlists
  - Gold (2,000pts): 2x cashback, early drop access, custom social cards
  - Platinum (10,000pts): 3x cashback, VIP events, merchant partnerships
  - Obsidian (50,000pts): 5x cashback, invite-only drops, Onyx Ambassador status, revenue share
- **Annual status reset:** Like airline miles. You earn it every year. Keeps people spending.
- **Daily hook:** Don't break the streak. Complete the challenge. Level up.
- **Revenue:** The gamification IS the revenue. It drives repeat visits, higher spend, and organic word-of-mouth.

## THE VIRAL LOOPS (how it grows itself)

### Loop 1: Cashback Referral
You send a friend $20 on Onyx → They get $5 bonus → They claim their first cashback offer
→ They tell a friend → LOOP

### Loop 2: Lottery Social Proof
You win $50 on a scratch-off → Auto-shared to feed → 12 friends see it → 3 download Onyx
→ They scratch their first card → LOOP

### Loop 3: Prediction Bragging
You call the game right → Confetti animation → Share card to IG → Friends ask "what app is that?"
→ They join to bet against you → LOOP

### Loop 4: Drop FOMO
Boutique drops limited item → 200 on waitlist → 50 get it → 50 post to social
→ Non-users see the hype → Download to join next drop → LOOP

### Loop 5: Merchant Network Effect
3 shops on Onyx in your neighborhood → Cross-promos drive foot traffic → 4th shop sees the traffic
→ They sign up → More deals for customers → More customers → LOOP

## TECH STACK

### Mobile App (what gets built)
- **React Native + Expo** (iOS + Android from one codebase)
- **Expo Router** for navigation
- **NativeWind** (Tailwind for RN) for styling
- **Supabase** for auth, database, realtime
- **Stripe Connect** for payments + Onyx Card
- **Apple/Google Wallet** for NFC loyalty passes
- **Claude API** for AI commerce agent
- **OpenCV** (via backend) for receipt scanning
- **MapLibre** for deals map
- **Expo Notifications** for push

### Backend (already built -- the POS API)
- FastAPI on Oracle (72 endpoints, 3,147 lines)
- Supabase (25 tables with RLS)
- Stripe Connect for multi-merchant payments

### The Key Insight
The POS is the MERCHANT BACKEND. The app is the PRODUCT.
Square built their empire on the merchant side.
**We're building the CONSUMER side that Square never built.**
