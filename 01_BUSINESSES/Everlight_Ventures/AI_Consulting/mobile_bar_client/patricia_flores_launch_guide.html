Section 01

Getting Started Checklist

Phase 1

Legal & Licensing — Week 1-2

  • RBS Certification Required by CA law. Online at abcbiz.abc.ca.gov. 2-4 hours, ~$15-30.
  • Business License Yolo County Clerk's office. ~$50-100/year.
  • DBA (Fictitious Business Name) If using a business name. County Clerk, ~$40.
  • EIN (Employer Identification Number) Free from IRS at irs.gov. Needed for business banking.
  • California Seller's Permit Free from CA CDTFA.
  • Consider an LLC CA Secretary of State, ~$70. Franchise tax $800/yr (waived first year).
  • Business Bank Account Keep business and personal finances separate.
Phase 2

Insurance — Week 2-3

  • General Liability $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate.
  • Liquor Liability Often bundled with general liability.
  • Per-Event Policies FLIP (~$100-200/event), Thimble, Next Insurance (~$300-500/year).
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI) Send to venues and clients for every event.
Phase 3

Equipment

Essential — Start Here ($200-400)

  • Bar tool kit Shaker, jigger, strainer, muddler, bar spoon, pour spouts, opener, corkscrew.
  • Cutting board + paring knife
  • Ice bucket + tongs + scoop
  • Disposable cups 9oz, 12oz, 16oz.
  • Cocktail napkins + speed pourers (set of 12)
  • Bar mat (rubber, non-slip)
  • Folding table (6ft) + black tablecloth/bar skirt
  • Large cooler + trash bags + bus bins

Level Up — Month 2-3 ($300-600)

  • Portable bar (foldable/collapsible)
  • LED lights or string lights
  • Menu sign + easel
  • Glassware set 36 each: coupes, rocks, highballs.
  • Glass rack for transport
  • Blender + beverage dispenser

Premium — Month 4+ ($500-1,000)

  • Branded signage Logo, menu boards, napkins.
  • Smoke gun for smoked cocktails
  • Large format ice molds
  • Professional branded bar mat
  • Uniform/apron with branding
Phase 4

Payment & Booking — Week 3-4

  • Payment processing Venmo Business, Zelle, Square (free reader), cash.
  • Invoicing Square Invoices or Wave (both free).
  • Contracts HelloSign or DocuSign for e-signatures.
  • Booking calendar Google Calendar or Calendly.
  • Client tracker Google Sheet: name, date, package, deposit, balance, notes.
Phase 5

Marketing

  • Instagram business account Your #1 channel. Post every event.
  • Google Business Profile Free. Gets you on Google Maps.
  • Marketplace listings Thumbtack, The Bash, GigSalad for early bookings.
  • Business cards Name, phone, IG handle, email.
  • Partner with event planners and venues

Section 02

Your First Booking

From inquiry to five-star review — every step of the client flow.

1

Inquiry Comes In

DM, call, text, or email. Respond within 1 hour if possible.

2

Qualify the Lead

Date, location, guest count, budget, event type. Get the facts before quoting.

3

Send Pricing Guide + Drink Menus

Let them see the packages. Attach sample menus. Make it visual.

4

Send Contracts

Agreement + Booking Form + Liability Waiver. Use e-signatures.

5

Collect 50% Deposit

Lock in the date. No deposit = no hold. This protects your time.

6

Pre-Event Consultation

Design the drink menu together. Send the shopping list. Confirm details.

7

Collect Balance

Full payment due 7 days before the event. No exceptions.

8

Day Of: Execute

Arrive 1 hour early. Set up. Crush it. Break down. Leave the space better than you found it.

9

Follow Up

Send a thank you. Request a Google/Yelp review. Ask for referrals. Plant the next seed.

Section 06

Pro Tips

TIP 01

Always get the deposit before holding a date. No deposit = no hold. Your time is worth protecting.

TIP 02

Take photos at every event. Your Instagram IS your portfolio. Good lighting, clean pours, happy faces.

TIP 03

Build a signature drink that becomes YOUR thing. Clients remember the bartender with the house special.

TIP 04

Carry business cards and leave 5-10 at the bar. Guests become clients. Every event is a lead generator.

TIP 05

Learn 3-5 "showtime" moves. People film it and tag you. Free marketing that scales.

TIP 06

Never serve someone who's visibly intoxicated. Not worth the liability. RBS training covers this.

TIP 07

Over-prepare, under-promise. Running out of ice is amateur hour. Bring 20% more than you think you need.

TIP 08

Track every dollar. Mileage deduction: $0.67/mile for 2026. Receipts, gas, supplies — it all adds up at tax time.
