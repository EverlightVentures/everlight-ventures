# T2 -- Systems & Optimization ($50k Net Worth / $10k+/mo Revenue)
## The Operationalize Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth crosses $50k OR sustained monthly revenue $10k+ for 3+ consecutive months.

**Posture:** T1 built the chassis. T2 builds the operating systems that run on the chassis.

---

## The 6 T2 Moves

### 1. Bookkeeping Upgrade
- Move from Wave to QuickBooks Online Plus ($90/mo) if multi-entity
- Hire a part-time bookkeeper ($200-500/mo) -- saves your time, improves audit defense
- Monthly close discipline: reconcile bank accounts, categorize transactions, post journal entries
- Quarterly financial statements: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow

### 2. Tax Strategy Calendar (Quarterly Cadence)
With CPA, build calendar:
- Q1 (Jan-Mar): Prior year final estimates, tax filing prep, Q1 estimated payment
- Q2 (Apr-Jun): Tax return filed, mid-year strategy review, Q2 estimated payment
- Q3 (Jul-Sep): Mid-year tax planning, S-corp election timing analysis, Q3 estimated payment
- Q4 (Oct-Dec): Year-end deductions push, equipment buys, retirement contribution max, Q4 estimated payment

### 3. Retirement Vehicle Activation

**Solo 401(k)** (if S-corp election made or self-employed):
- Up to $23,000 employee deferral (2024)
- Up to 25% of self-employment income or salary as employer match
- Combined limit: $69,000/yr ($76,500 if 50+)
- Roth Solo 401(k) variant available for tax-free growth
- Provider: Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard (free), or Solo 401k specialists (~$150/yr) for added flexibility

**Backdoor Roth IRA** (if income > Roth direct contribution limit):
- Contribute $7,000 to Traditional IRA (non-deductible)
- Convert to Roth same year
- Pay tax on any growth in interim (typically $0 if same-day)
- Annual move

**HSA** (if HDHP):
- $4,300 individual / $8,550 family (2025)
- Triple tax advantage (deductible, growth, qualified withdrawal)
- Investment account, NOT spent on current medical
- Receipts saved for decade-later tax-free reimbursement

### 4. Annual Exclusion Gifting (If Heirs Exist)

If you have kids/grandkids/nieces/nephews:
- $19,000/yr/recipient/donor (2025)
- Married couples = $38,000/yr/recipient
- Vehicles: 529 plans, custodial accounts (UTMA/UGMA), direct payments to schools/medical providers (UNLIMITED beyond annual exclusion)

**Strategy:** Front-load early. $19k/yr × 18 yrs at 7% return = $700k+ in 529 per child.

### 5. Hire Your Kids (If Applicable)

For kids under 18 doing real work for the business:
- Pay up to $14,600/yr (2024 standard deduction, $0 federal income tax to them)
- No FICA if business is sole prop OR LLC owned 100% by parent(s)
- Business deducts the wage
- Use proceeds for: 529, Roth IRA (kids can fund Roths with earned income!), future expenses

**Required:** Real work product, real time tracking, real W-2.

**Annual value:** $5,400+ tax savings per kid in 24% bracket parent.

### 6. TBE / Marital Asset Protection (If Married)

If married AND state allows Tenants by Entirety:
- Retitle home as TBE
- Keep marital assets in joint TBE accounts
- Effect: creditor of EITHER spouse cannot reach TBE assets unless BOTH are debtors

**TBE strongest:** FL, TN (uncapped TBE for personal property too).

## What You Are NOT Doing at T2

- Not yet S-corp electing (do at T3 when profit clears $80k)
- Not opening DAPT (premature, T5)
- Not opening trusts beyond RLT (estate too small)
- Not deploying captive insurance
- Not changing domicile
