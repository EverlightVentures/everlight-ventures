## GIG 1: Verified Field Work / Retail Audits

I will send a verified field worker to audit, inspect, or verify any US location

**Category:** Business > Market Research

**Pricing:**

| Tier | Price | Deliverable | Delivery |
|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Basic | $49 | 1 location -- timestamped photos + written report | 3 days |
| Standard | $149 | 3 locations -- detailed photo report + comparison notes | 5 days |
| Premium | $399 | 10 locations -- full analytics dashboard + executive summary | 7 days |

**Gig Description:**

Need eyes on the ground but can't be there yourself? We dispatch verified field workers anywhere in the United States to audit, inspect, photograph, and report back -- so you get the facts without the flight.

Our 40+ person operations team handles retail shelf audits, property condition checks, mystery shopping visits, delivery verifications, and local errands with full proof-of-completion. Every task comes back with timestamped photos, GPS confirmation, and a clear written report. No guesswork. No stock photos. Real boots on real ground.

**Here's what clients use us for:**

- Retail brands checking if their product is actually on the shelf, priced right, and displayed correctly
- Property managers verifying condition before or after a tenant move
- Franchise owners running anonymous mystery shops on their own locations
- E-commerce sellers confirming delivery and installation at the final mile
- Investors who need a current photo set of a property or storefront before making a decision

We've built our workflow around speed and accuracy. When you place an order, our dispatch coordinator assigns a vetted local worker, briefs them on your exact requirements, and quality-checks every deliverable before it reaches you. Most single-location jobs are turned around in 48 hours or less.

**What you get:**

- High-resolution, timestamped photos (minimum 10 per location)
- GPS-verified check-in confirming the worker was on-site
- Written condition or audit report tailored to your checklist
- Dashboard access for Premium orders with filterable data across all locations

No subscriptions. No software to learn. Just send us the address, tell us what to look for, and we handle the rest.

**Ready to get ground truth? Message me with your location and requirements, and I'll send a custom quote within 2 hours.**

**FAQ:**

1. **What areas do you cover?**
   All 50 US states. Urban areas typically dispatch within 24 hours. Rural areas may take 48-72 hours depending on worker availability. We'll confirm timing before you order.

2. **How do I know the worker actually went to the location?**
   Every task includes GPS-stamped check-in/check-out, timestamped photos with metadata, and a written report. Premium orders include a live tracking link.

3. **Can I provide a custom checklist?**
   Absolutely. Send us your audit criteria, inspection checklist, or specific photo angles and we'll brief the field worker accordingly. Custom checklists are included at every tier.

4. **Is this confidential? I don't want the location to know I sent someone.**
   Yes. Mystery shopping and discreet audits are core to what we do. Workers arrive as regular customers or visitors. No branding, no clipboards, no tip-offs.

5. **What if the report isn't what I expected?**
   We quality-check every deliverable before sending it to you. If something's missing or unclear, we'll re-dispatch at no extra cost. Your satisfaction is non-negotiable.
