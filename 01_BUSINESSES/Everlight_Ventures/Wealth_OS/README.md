## Why This Exists

Most "tax strategy" advice stops at "form an LLC and track expenses." That advice saves $5k/yr.

The actual UHNW playbook is **7 stacked layers** that compound: entities license IP to other entities, trusts hold the entities, domicile arbitrage cuts tax bands, credits stack on top, asset protection wraps the whole thing, borrow-buy-die provides liquidity without realization, generational engines move wealth forward without estate tax. Properly stacked, this saves millions per year at scale and protects against lawsuits, divorce, audit, and political risk.

This OS is the architecture, the activation map, and the always-on intelligence system. Execution at each tier still requires licensed professionals (CPA, estate attorney, RE attorney, fiduciary). The OS makes you the principal directing them, not the client being sold to.

## Reality Disclaimers (Read Once)

- **This is research and education, not legal/tax/financial advice.** I am an AI, not a fiduciary, CPA, or attorney.
- **Every active strategy here requires licensed professionals to execute.** The OS gives you the framework to direct them, not replace them.
- **Tax law changes constantly.** A strategy current today may not work in 18 months. The Quarterly Intel Engine exists for this reason.
- **Some strategies (PPLI, captive insurance, offshore trusts) are scrutinized by IRS** when done sloppily. The line between aggressive-but-legal and abusive matters. Do not deploy without competent counsel.
- **Lifetime gift/estate exemption sunsets Dec 31, 2025.** From $13.99M to ~$7M. This is the most consequential tax cliff in 20 years and shapes T6-T8 timing decisions.
- **TCJA, R&D capitalization (Sec 174), bonus depreciation, and Opportunity Zones** all have moving deadlines. Track via `03_Engines/Quarterly_Intel.md`.

1. **Track every business expense, mile, and software cost from January 2026 forward.** R&D credit retroactivity is 3 years. The XLM bot, Hive Mind, Vantaris, Onyx, Broker OS work all qualifies as Section 41 R&D. This is potentially $20-100k of credits you have already earned but not claimed.

2. **Decide on QSBS path before you take outside money on any SaaS.** Section 1202 only works on C-corps with the right paperwork from day one. If we incorporate Hive Mind SaaS, Onyx, or Vantaris as Delaware C-corps NOW, then on a 5+ year hold with <$50M gross assets at issuance, the first $10M of gain on each is 100% federally tax-free at exit. This is a free option. Lose it at your peril.

3. **Document the home office, the dedicated business phone, the business-purpose miles, and the Augusta-rule home rentals from now on.** These convert personal expenses into deductible business expenses at T1. Without contemporaneous records, the deduction is fragile in audit.
