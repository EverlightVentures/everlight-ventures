**Critical creative direction:**
The hive's metadata overindex on educator jargon (phonics, CCSS, CASEL). Kids don't care about blends -- they care about the ADVENTURE. All titles, descriptions, and marketing must lead with excitement, mystery, and fun. Educational value is the quiet backbone, not the headline. Think "Bluey" not "LeapFrog."

**What we're doing:**
Lock Book 3's character designs, tone, adventure energy, and quality as the canonical standard. Upgrade Books 1-2 to match. Rewrite all customer-facing copy to be adventure-first (educator copy stays for backend/institutional use). Build a marketing/content plan that works on $50.
