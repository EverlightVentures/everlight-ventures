# T7 -- SLAT + Dynasty Trust ($2.5M Net Worth)
## The Generational Transfer Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $2.5M+ OR married couple with $7M+ trajectory wanting to use TCJA exemption before Dec 31, 2025 sunset.

---

## The 4 T7 Moves

### 1. SLAT (Spousal Lifetime Access Trust) -- IF MARRIED AND TIMING ALLOWS

**The 2025 sunset play:**
- Lifetime gift/estate exemption: $13.99M per person (2025)
- Sunsets to ~$7M per person (Jan 1, 2026)
- "Use it or lose it" by year-end 2025

**For married couples:**
- Spouse A funds Spouse B's SLAT (using Spouse A's exemption)
- Spouse B's SLAT can distribute to Spouse B during life (and to kids)
- Assets removed from BOTH estates
- Future appreciation occurs OUTSIDE estate forever

**Reciprocal Trust Doctrine warning:**
- DO NOT make SLATs identical
- Stagger funding by 6+ months
- Vary trustees, vary beneficiary classes, vary terms, vary distribution standards
- IRS unwinds reciprocal SLATs

**Cost:** $10-25k per SLAT, $1-3k/yr trustee.

**Critical timing:** Estate attorneys typically stop taking new SLAT clients after September 2025 due to volume. If T7 trigger hits in 2024-2025, plan EARLY.

### 2. Dynasty Trust (South Dakota)

**Purpose:** Multi-generational tax-free wealth transfer. Up to 1,000 years.

**Why South Dakota:**
- No state income tax on trust earnings
- Perpetual trusts (no Rule Against Perpetuities)
- Strongest privacy laws (sealed court records)
- Strong asset protection
- Family-owned Private Trust Companies (PTCs) allowed

**Mechanics:**
- Engage SD-licensed attorney + SD trust company
- Allocate GST exemption ($13.99M in 2025) at funding
- Once allocated, ALL future appreciation is GST-tax-free
- Beneficiaries: children, grandchildren, and beyond
- Trustee: SD trust company (Bridgeford, South Dakota Trust Company, others)

**The math:** $13.99M into Dynasty Trust today, growing at 7% over 100 years = $11+ BILLION, all GST-tax-free, all out of any heir's estate forever.

### 3. IDGT (Intentionally Defective Grantor Trust)

**For larger asset transfers:**
- Sell appreciating assets to IDGT in exchange for promissory note at AFR (~4.5%)
- Trust pays you back over time
- Asset appreciates inside trust, OUTSIDE estate
- You pay income tax on trust earnings (the "defective" part) -- itself a tax-free gift to trust

**Best for:** Business interests, real estate, concentrated stock positions where appreciation is expected.

**The Walton play:** This is the structure they used for billions of dollars of intra-family transfer.

### 4. Family Education Initiative

T7 is when the family education layer becomes critical:
- First family meeting (annual cadence going forward)
- Adult heirs introduced to estate plan structure
- Investment principles education for any heirs over 12
- 529 superfunding for grandchildren
- Discussion of values, mission, charitable intent
