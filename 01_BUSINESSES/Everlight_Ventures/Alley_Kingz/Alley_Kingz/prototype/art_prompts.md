## NOTES FOR GENERATION

- **Consistent scale**: All unit sprites should be sized relative to each other. Street Racer is smallest, Monster Truck is largest, HQ Van is 1.5x largest.
- **Shadow direction**: All top-down sprites should cast shadows to the bottom-right (light source: top-left).
- **Color identity**: Each unit has ONE dominant neon color. This color should be used for UI highlights, ability VFX, and the card frame accent.
  - Muscle Car: Gold/Amber
  - Drive-By Sedan: Hot Pink
  - Molotov: Orange/Red
  - Shotgun Blast: Toxic Green
  - Monster Truck: Rust Orange/Brown
  - Sniper Van: Purple
  - Street Racer: Electric Blue
  - Boom Box: Cherry Red
  - Escort SUV: Dark Red
  - HQ Van: Blue/White
- **Resolution targets**: Card art at 1024x1024, sprites at 512x512, VFX at 256x256 sprite sheets, arena at 2048x1024.
- **Tools**: These prompts are optimized for Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, and Stable Diffusion XL. Append `--ar 1:1 --s 750 --q 2` for Midjourney. For SDXL, use the global prefix as the positive prompt and add `blurry, low quality, watermark, text, realistic photo` as the negative prompt.
