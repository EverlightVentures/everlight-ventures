## TOP-LINE FINDINGS (read this first)

1. **The single biggest defect cuts across all four personas:** every first-touch
   render leads with the SAME live offer range ($27,840-$31,320). This is a double
   violation. It breaks Piper's own dossier doctrine ("First touch never carries a
   number ... Touch 3 = first number, handoff to Henry"), AND it breaks the
   OUTREACH_PSYCHOLOGY anchoring rule (Section 1.2: "Do NOT open with your offer
   number. Open with market conditions and carrying costs. REPLACE the anchor.").
   The current copy hands a cold, unexplained 48-54%-of-assessed number to a seller
   on contact zero, before any trust, any conversation, any net-vs-net reframe. To
   a distressed owner this reads as "vulture lowball" on the first read.

2. **Persona furthest from dossier: MARVIN COHEN.** His first-touch render assumes
   the deal is already agreed ("Henry just handed this over to me, which means
   we're moving," "Agreed range: $27,840-$31,320 all cash," contract in 30 minutes,
   EMD, closing date). Marvin is a CLOSER who takes the handoff *after* price is
   agreed. Sending his "next steps / contract incoming" email as a COLD first touch
   is the most jarring character break in the set -- it presumes a yes that the
   seller never gave. (Henry is a close second for the same reason: his cold email
   opens "You wanted to know where we land" to a seller who never asked.)

3. **The footer is a self-inflicted trust wound on EVERY persona.** Every email
   ends with: "This first note came from our automated outreach team; the moment
   you reply, a real person picks it up personally." On a sensitive, money-and-shame
   email to a struggling homeowner, leading the close with "this was a bot" is the
   single most reply-suppressing line in the whole template. More on this in the
   red-flags section.

## LEAD / HANDOFF MAP (who fronts which seller, and when)

- **Default Memphis tax-delinquent / absentee / individual:** PIPER leads cold (no
  number). Reply -> HENRY (now he can say "picking up from Piper" truthfully and
  bring the math). Price agreed -> MARVIN (now his closing detail reassures instead
  of alarms). Fee >$50k or any dispute -> VAUGHN.
- **Age 75+ OR senior-care / dementia / "my husband passed" / memory-care signal:**
  VAUGHN leads personally from touch 1 (per his dossier), no number, gravitas only.
  Piper does NOT front these.
- **Probate / inherited / multi-heir:** Piper or Vaughn fronts (soft, Unity lever);
  Henry on the math when heirs ask "what's the number"; Marvin closes with extra
  care on multi-signer logistics.
- **LLC / investor / institutional seller:** Henry can front directly (numbers
  person to numbers person) -- this is the ONE case where leading with math early
  is correct, because the OUTREACH_PSYCHOLOGY doc's Example 3 (Hammer/investor) shows
  a numbers person WANTS the number fast. Piper can also clear the gatekeeper.

The cardinal rule the current renders break: **Marvin and Henry should almost never
be the FIRST email a seller sees.** They are stage-2 and stage-3 voices. Right now
all four are interchangeable cold senders, which collapses the entire relay into
"four bots that each quote the same lowball."

# THE "STILL FEELS SCRIPTED / VULTURE CIRCLING" RED FLAGS

1. **Number on first contact (all 4).** The clearest vulture tell. A stranger
   quoting ~48-54% of assessed value before any conversation = "circling a wounded
   animal." Violates both the persona doctrine and the anchoring psychology.
2. **The bot-admission footer (all 4).** "This first note came from our automated
   outreach team." Honesty is good in principle, but disclosing "a machine sent
   this" as the LAST thing a distressed person reads is reply-poison. It converts
   "a real person reached out about my house" into "I'm a row in someone's
   automated lead list." This is the #1 single fixable trust-killer.
3. **Shared template skeleton showing through (all 4).** The identical empathy line
   ("a lot of folks are juggling a lot right now -- no judgment here") appears
   word-for-word in Piper, Henry, AND Vaughn. The identical offer range appears in
   all four. When the "distinct humans" share verbatim sentences, a reader who got
   two of them (e.g., touch 1 Piper then touch 2 Henry) instantly sees the mail-
   merge. The personas are paint over one body.
4. **Wrong-stage emails (Henry, Marvin).** "You wanted to know where we land" and
   "Henry just handed this to me, we're moving / agreed range" are stated as fact to
   sellers who agreed to nothing. A seller who reads carefully feels manipulated --
   the email is pretending a relationship exists that doesn't.
5. **"We buy Memphis properties for cash, no agents, no hassle" (Piper line 1).**
   This is the "we buy ugly houses" register the whole OUTREACH_PSYCHOLOGY doc is
   trying to escape. It signals high-volume investor, not the human Piper claims to
   be in the next sentence.
6. **County assessed value used as the only "comp."** Sophisticated-sounding but
   hollow -- assessed value is not market value, and quoting it as the anchor next
   to a half-price offer makes the discount look predatory rather than reasoned. No
   repair estimate, no days-on-market, no net-vs-traditional. Henry's "math" has no
   math.

# MISSING HUMAN / CHARACTER ELEMENTS (what would make them feel REAL)

- **Lead-type-aware bodies, not name-swapped clones.** The dossiers give each
  persona a distinct register; the template should give each LEAD TYPE a distinct
  body (occupant vs. inherited vs. absentee vs. overwhelmed). Right now Elda's
  "22-year intentional" story gets pasted onto James the Chicago heir. One body =
  the mail-merge smell.
- **Real per-seller specificity from the public record we already pull.** The psych
  doc lists safe personalization (neighborhood ZIP stat, days-on-market, the
  property's actual character). The renders use NONE of it -- no "homes in 38107 are
  sitting 70 days," no neighborhood reference. Generic = scripted.
- **The actual humans behind the personas.** Piper's Biscuit, her Midtown coffee-
  shop opinions, her Franklin roots; Henry's "honest read"; Vaughn's measured
  single-sentence decision line. A trace of a real person (not on touch 1
  necessarily, but in the reply thread) is what makes a seller feel they're talking
  to someone, not something. The footer currently does the OPPOSITE -- it announces
  there is no one there yet.
- **The net-vs-traditional comparison (loss-aversion fix).** The entire psychology
  doc says: never gross-vs-gross, always "most sellers net $X after commission +
  repairs + 90 days; we put $Y in your hand now." Not one render does this. This is
  the difference between "lowball" and "actually, that's competitive once you do the
  math" -- and it's Henry's whole reason to exist.
- **An opt-out that reads human, not legal.** Piper's "no worries at all" is good;
  but pairing it with a bot-disclosure footer cancels it. A clean "If now's not the
  time, just say so and I won't bug you" beats the automated-team paragraph.
- **Stage discipline in the relay.** The missing human element at the SYSTEM level:
  a seller should feel a relationship deepening (warm hello -> here's the careful
  math -> here's the careful paperwork). Right now they get three strangers each
  presenting a finished deal. The hand-off is the humanity; we're skipping straight
  to the contract voice.

# RECOMMENDED FIXES (priority order, no code changed in this pass)

1. **Pull the offer number out of every first-touch render.** Touch 1 = relationship
   only. Restores Piper's doctrine and kills the #1 vulture signal. (Henry can keep
   numbers because Henry should only fire AFTER a reply.)
2. **Rewrite or remove the bot-admission footer.** At minimum move it out of the
   emotional close; better, replace with a human opt-out line.
3. **Enforce stage discipline:** Piper (or Vaughn for elderly) is the only cold
   sender. Henry and Marvin are reply-triggered only.
4. **Branch the body by lead_type** (occupant / inherited / absentee / overwhelmed)
   so the personalization stops misfiring.
5. **Give Henry real math** (net-vs-traditional, carrying cost, a real comp stat)
   when he does enter -- it's his entire credibility asset.
6. **De-duplicate the shared empathy + offer sentences** so the four voices stop
   reading as one template.
