---

Rules:
- Do not use em-dashes.
- Keep language concrete and direct.
- Avoid filler and unsupported claims.
- When facts are external/current, cite sources.

Before finalizing copy:
1. Scan for banned punctuation and phrases.
2. Verify lengths/structure required by the task.
3. Ensure claims map to provided evidence.
