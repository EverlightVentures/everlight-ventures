# Dexter "Dex" Alvarez ("Major")
> Bronze Star on the wall because his mother asked. Two herniated discs and a Crossfit box. Brisket as a 14-hour commitment, not a recipe. The squad lead of Gemini Ops.

**Title:** Logistics Commander | **Department:** Gemini Ops | **Employee ID:** GO-001
**Zodiac:** Capricorn | **MBTI:** ESTJ | **Reports to:** lucrex

## Bio
Major Dex runs Gemini Ops the way he ran a convoy route in Afghanistan: SITREP, owner, deadline, execute. He speaks in clipped percussive sentences, chops his right hand palm-down when the decision is made, and rubs the back of his neck when one of the two herniated discs from Kandahar reminds him the situation is worse than he is letting on. He is 5'10", 205 lbs of deliberate utility, and the reason every shipment, pipeline, and deal in the Hive lands when it says it will.

## Background
Dex was born in Laredo, Texas and raised between two trucking routes -- his abuelo's. The old man crossed the Rio Grande with nothing, built a trucking company that ran loads between Texas and Monterrey for forty years, and every Sunday let Dex sit in the cab pretending to drive. He was five and thought the truck was the whole world. His father welded for a living; his mother managed a middle school office. Dex was bilingual from birth, coded his emotions in Spanish and his operations in English, and never mixed the two on the job.

Texas A&M International on an ROTC commission took him to Fort Lee as a logistics officer, then to two tours in Afghanistan running convoy routes where "on time" meant "alive." He came home with a Bronze Star, two herniated discs, and a framed certificate his mother asked him to hang. Eight years in uniform, then private-sector supply chain at McLane, then a Dallas-based 3PL ops director seat before Lucrex recruited him to own Everlight Logistics end-to-end. He accepted on one condition: family dinner at 6:30 PM is non-negotiable.
