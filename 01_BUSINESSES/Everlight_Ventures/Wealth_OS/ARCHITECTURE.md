## How The Layers Compound (Worked Example)

**Setup:** You exit Hive Mind SaaS for $20M in 2030. Without the OS, you pay ~37% federal + 13.3% CA + 3.8% NIIT = ~$10.8M to taxes. You keep $9.2M.

**With the Stack:**

| Layer | Move | Tax Saved |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Hive Mind incorporated as Delaware C-corp at founding | enables QSBS |
| L4 | QSBS Sec 1202 -- first $10M federally tax-free | $3.7M |
| L3 | You moved domicile to FL or TX 2 years pre-exit | $1.5M state tax |
| L2 | Pre-exit, you transferred 25% of shares to SLAT for spouse | est exemption used pre-sunset, removes $5M from estate |
| L4 | Charitable Remainder Trust (CRT) holds 30% of remaining shares | defers $4.5M of gain into income stream over 20 yrs |
| L6 | Of the remaining cash, $6M goes into PPLI wrapper | future growth tax-free, accessible via policy loans |
| L7 | $1M into Dynasty Trust in SD via SLAT, perpetual GST exemption | removes from taxable estate forever |

**Net taxes paid:** ~$1.5M instead of $10.8M. **Saved: $9.3M on a single transaction.**

This is why the layers exist. Any one of them done alone gets you 10% of the savings. Stacked properly, they multiply.

## Layer Summaries (Click into `01_Layers/Lx_*.md` for Detail)

### L1 -- Entity Architecture (the chassis)
Multi-entity orchestra where each entity has one job and they pay each other. Operating Co does the work, Holdco owns the operating cos and IP Co, IP Co charges royalties to Operating Co (income shift), Management Co charges fees, Real Estate Cos hold property each in own LLC, Captive Insurance Co self-insures, FLP holds appreciating assets at valuation discount, Family Office Mgmt Co per the Lender Mgmt case (Section 162 deductibility).

**Activation tier:** T1 (form first WY anonymous LLC)
**Full deployment:** T4-T5 (Holdco + IP Co separation)

### L2 -- Trust Architecture (the fortress)
Trusts are how the Waltons moved $200B without paying estate tax. Each trust does a different job:
- Revocable Living Trust = probate avoidance
- ILIT = life insurance outside estate
- GRAT = transfer appreciation tax-free (Zuckerberg pre-FB-IPO)
- IDGT = sell appreciating assets to trust at low value (Walton playbook)
- SLAT = use lifetime exemption, spouse keeps access
- Dynasty Trust = 1000-yr tax-free generational wealth (SD/NV/AK/DE)
- CRT = sell appreciated asset tax-free, take income stream (Buffett-style)
- DAPT = self-settled, judgment-proof

**Activation tier:** T6 (ILIT + first GRAT)
**Critical deadline:** Dec 31, 2025 (lifetime exemption sunsets from $13.99M to ~$7M -- SLAT/Dynasty Trust must use exemption before this)

### L3 -- Domicile Arbitrage
Where you, your entities, and your assets sit determines effective tax rate. Personal domicile (FL/TX/TN/NV/SD/WA = 0% state income tax), trust domicile (SD/NV/AK/DE for perpetual + asset-protected trusts), entity domicile (WY for cheap+anonymous, DE for case law, NV for charging-order strength), federal arbitrage (PR Act 60 = 4% corp + 0% cap gains + 0% div with bona fide residency), foreign (FEIE $126,500/yr exclusion, Section 962 election).

**Activation tier:** T1 (entity domicile WY) -> T9 (PR Act 60 cost-benefit positive at $10M+)

### L4 -- Credits & Incentives Engine
Checks the IRS sends you. Most go uncashed. R&D Sec 41 (6-14% of qualified research, retroactive 3 years), QSBS Sec 1202 ($10M or 10x basis tax-free at exit, C-corp 5-yr hold), WOTC (up to $9,600 per qualifying hire), Section 179 + Bonus Depreciation (immediate expense), Cost Segregation (accelerate RE depreciation), REPS + STR loophole (depreciation against active income), Augusta Rule (rent home to business 14 days tax-free), Hire Your Kids ($14,600 each, no FICA), Solar ITC (30%), Opportunity Zones (defer + reduce + eliminate cap gains), 1031 (forever defer RE gains), 45L/179D (energy), NMTC/LIHTC/Historic (stackable RE).

**Activation tier:** T0 (start documentation today!) -> T3 (file R&D credit retroactively)

### L5 -- Asset Protection Fortress
Goal: be functionally unsue-able. Layered: personal name (zero) -> DAPT/Trust (judgment-proof) -> Holdco LLC (charging order) -> Series LLCs per asset (firewalled) -> equity-stripped via HELOCs (nothing to seize). Plus tenants by entirety (26 states, marriage), homestead exemption (FL/TX uncapped), ERISA-protected retirement (untouchable in BK), PPLI wrapper (asset-protected + tax-free hedge fund returns inside life policy).

**Activation tier:** T5 (DAPT in NV/SD)
**Aggressive tier:** T9+ (Cook Islands trust if profile warrants)

### L6 -- Borrow-Buy-Die Liquidity Engine
How billionaires actually live. Never sell, never pay tax. SBLOC (securities-backed line, SOFR + 0.5-2%, borrow against stock portfolio), HELOC + cash-out refi (tap RE equity tax-free), cash-value life insurance loans (4-6%, never repay, dies with you), margin/portfolio margin, crypto-backed loans (Ledn/Salt/Aave). Final move: step-up basis at death wipes capital gains forever.

**Activation tier:** T5 (first SBLOC against taxable brokerage), full power at T9+ (PPLI policy loans)

### L7 -- Generational Engine
Annual exclusion stacking ($19k/yr × recipients × spouses), 529 superfunding ($95k per kid per spouse front-loaded), 529-to-Roth ($35k lifetime per SECURE 2.0), Crummey withdrawal powers, Family Bank concept (intra-family loans at AFR rates), CLT (charitable lead trust for estate compression), GST tax planning, lifetime exemption use pre-sunset.

**Activation tier:** T2 (annual exclusion gifting starts), full power T7+ (Dynasty + SLAT)

## What This OS DOES NOT Do

- **It does not file your taxes.** That's a CPA's job.
- **It does not draft legal documents.** That's an estate attorney's job.
- **It does not provide regulated financial advice.** That's a fiduciary's job.
- **It does not guarantee any outcome.** Tax law changes. Strategies that work today may not in 18 months.
- **It does not deploy abusive shelters.** The line between aggressive-but-legal and abusive matters. The OS stays on the legal side.

What it DOES do: gives you the framework, the activation timing, the priority weights, and the always-on intel system so when you walk into the CPA's office you know what to ask for and what to refuse.
