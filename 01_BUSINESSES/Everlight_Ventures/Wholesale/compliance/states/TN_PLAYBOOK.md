## 1. Quick verdict

**Wholesaling is LEGAL in TN without a real estate license up to ~4 deals/year informal threshold.** Above 4 deals/year (the "engaged in the business" doctrine), TN Code §62-13-104 requires a real estate broker license + $50K surety bond. SB 909 (effective Apr 8, 2025) requires written wholesaler disclosure to seller AND any subsequent purchaser. 2-year statute of limitations.

## 2. Wholesale licensing requirement

- **Statute:** TN Code §62-13-104 (real estate broker licensure).
- **Trigger:** "Engaged in the business of" buying/selling/assigning real estate contracts.
- **Threshold:** No published numerical threshold; informal industry standard = 4 deals/year as the "occasional" ceiling.
- **Licensure if exceeded:** TN broker license (TREC) + $50K surety bond.
- **Penalty for unlicensed activity above threshold:** Class B misdemeanor under §62-13-110.

## 3. Required wholesaler disclosure

- **Statute:** TN SB 909 (signed Mar 25, 2025; effective Apr 8, 2025).
- **What:** Buyer-wholesaler must disclose equitable-interest position (wholesaler is selling option/assigning contract, not legal title).
- **To whom:** Seller AND subsequent purchaser/assignee.
- **When:** Before contract execution / before assignment.
- **Surface:** Written. Standalone notice via Documenso recommended; PSA paragraph acceptable if signed.
- **Penalty for non-disclosure:** Seller may cancel without penalty; TREC sanctions; 2-year statute of limitations on enforcement.

## 4. Volume thresholds

- **Informal ceiling:** ~4 deals/year before "engaged in business" doctrine triggers.
- **Above 4/year:** must obtain TN broker license + $50K bond OR cease TN activity.
- **No statutory number** — based on TREC interpretation + industry practice.
- **Operator strategy:** until proven volume justifies $50K bond, cap TN at 4 closes/year.

## 5. Surety bond / fee

- **$50,000 surety bond** under §62-13-104 ONLY IF "engaged in business."
- **No separate wholesaler bond** under SB 909.
- **Cold-call solicitor registration** under TN TSA §47-18-2002 if cold-calling.

## 8. Option / inspection period

- **Contractual** (no statutory option period).
- **Standard:** 10-14 days inspection in TN Realtors contract.
- **Earnest money:** held by title agent, refundable through inspection per contract.

## 9. Penalty regime

- **SB 909 violation:** seller may cancel + TREC sanctions + 2-year SOL.
- **Unlicensed brokerage above 4-deal threshold:** Class B misdemeanor (§62-13-110), fine + up to 6 mo.
- **DTPA-equivalent:** TN Consumer Protection Act (§47-18-101 et seq.) — civil penalty + private right of action.

## 10. Tax economics

- **Personal income tax:** 0% (Hall income tax repealed 2021). NO state income tax.
- **Capital gains:** 0% (federal ordinary income only).
- **Realty transfer tax:** $0.37 per $100 of consideration (~0.37%, paid by seller typically).
- **Mortgage tax:** $0.115 per $100 above $2,000 exempt threshold.
- **Recordation:** county-level, $10-15 per document.
- **Wholesale fees:** federal ordinary income (dealer income); no state tax burden.
