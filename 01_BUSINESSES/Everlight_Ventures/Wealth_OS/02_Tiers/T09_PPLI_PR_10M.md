# T9 -- PPLI + Puerto Rico Act 60 ($10M Net Worth)
## The Sovereignty Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $10M+ AND/OR sustained $1M+ annual income.

---

## The 5 T9 Moves

### 1. Puerto Rico Act 60 (Cost-Benefit Analysis)

**Now positive ROI** at this scale.

**Act 60 Individual Investor Decree:**
- 0% tax on capital gains accrued AFTER becoming PR resident
- 0% on PR-source dividends and interest
- Pre-move appreciation still federally taxable for 10 years
- $10k/yr donation to approved PR nonprofit
- Bona fide PR residency (183+ days, tax home, closer connection)
- Buy a home in PR within 2 years ($300k+)

**Act 60 Export Services Decree:**
- 4% corporate tax rate on PR-sourced services exported outside PR
- 0% on dividends to bona fide PR-resident shareholders
- Real economic substance required (employees, office, operations in PR)

**Combined savings at $1M income:** ~$500k/yr vs CA, ~$300k/yr vs federal-only baseline.

**Cost:** $25-75k attorney + accountant for setup, $10-25k/yr maintenance. Plus relocation costs.

**Decision matrix:**
- Income $1M+/yr sustained: positive ROI
- Willing to relocate: required
- Business is portable: yours is (AI-driven, remote-first)
- Family flexibility: required

### 2. PPLI (Private Placement Life Insurance)

**The Bezos-style wrapper:**
- Specialty life insurance policy
- Cash value invested in hedge funds, private equity, alternatives
- Cash value grows tax-free inside the wrapper
- Death benefit passes income-tax-free
- Asset protection in most states

**Mechanics:**
- Minimum premium $1-5M (private placement = "qualified investor" thresholds)
- Investment options: registered separately-managed accounts within insurance dedicated funds
- Provider: Lombard International, John Hancock, Crown Global, Pacific Life, Penn Mutual

**Why deploy at T9:**
- Tax drag on alternatives is severe (PE/HF can be 50%+ tax on returns)
- PPLI eliminates that drag
- Combined with ILIT funding, becomes generational tax-free vehicle

**Costs:** 0.5-1.5% policy charges, plus underlying investment fees.

### 3. Cook Islands Trust (Asset Protection Apex)

**For high litigation profile:**
- Cook Islands does not recognize foreign judgments
- US court orders cannot reach trust assets
- Beneficiaries cannot be compelled to disgorge
- Creditor must hire Cook Islands attorney, prove fraudulent transfer in CK court, beyond reasonable doubt, within strict statute of limitations

**Practical effect:** Plaintiff's lawyers tell clients "don't bother."

**Reporting:** Form 3520, 3520-A, FBAR mandatory. Penalties for non-filing severe ($10k+ per form).

**Cost:** $25-50k initial, $10-20k/yr maintenance + reporting.

**Combined with DAPT:** Cook Islands trust holds primary protected assets. DAPT in NV/SD as buffer. Belt-and-suspenders structure.

### 4. International Domicile Diversification (Optional)

If PRIORITIES weights GEOGRAPHIC_FREEDOM very high:
- UAE / Dubai (0% personal income tax, residency via investor visa or freelance permit)
- Singapore (territorial tax, only Singapore-sourced income taxed)
- Portugal (NHR program ended for new applicants, but grandfathered residents continue)
- Thailand LTR visa (foreign income exemption)

**FEIE (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion):** $130,000/yr (2025) of foreign earned income excluded for US citizens working abroad.

### 5. Direct Deal Flow Channel

At $10M+ net worth, family office can begin direct investing:
- Angel investments in startups (not via VC funds)
- Direct real estate acquisition (commercial scale)
- Private placements
- Co-investment opportunities through family office network
- Search funds (acquire established small business)

**Network building:** Tiger 21, YPO, Family Office Exchange, Institute for Private Investors -- annual fees $5-30k for membership.
