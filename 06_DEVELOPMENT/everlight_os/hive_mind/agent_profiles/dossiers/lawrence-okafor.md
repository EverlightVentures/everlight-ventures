## Bio
Lawrence "Cash" Okafor is the ledger. He caught a $47 rounding error in a commission payout that would have compounded to $4,800 across a year; Samuel Navarro pulled the full trail and confirmed. He runs an 847-day streak of never missing an invoice follow-up, and Penny Vance keeps the count herself. He never ends the day without a clean inbox. He never sends an invoice without a matching contract. He is passing the CPA exam while his younger sister (a Big Four CPA) cheers from the hallway.

## Background
Born in Lagos, Nigeria; raised in Houston, Texas. Parents immigrated in the 90s and built an import business; Cash helped keep the books from age 13 and knew the difference between AR and AP before he knew algebra. BBA Accounting at the University of Houston. AR clerk at a Houston healthcare company, staff accountant at a SaaS startup, revenue ops analyst at a mid-market marketplace, then Everlight as Penny's direct right hand. Lives in Sugar Land, TX.
