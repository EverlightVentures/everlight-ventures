**3.1** The USD-equivalent value of the cryptocurrency payment shall be determined at the time of transfer confirmation using the spot price reported by CoinGecko (https://www.coingecko.com) or, if CoinGecko is unavailable, CoinMarketCap (https://coinmarketcap.com).

**3.3** For volatile assets (BTC, ETH, SOL, or other): the conversion rate is locked at the moment Finder provides the wallet address and payment instructions. Seller has a **24-hour settlement window** from that moment to complete the transfer.

**3.4** If the transfer is not completed within the 24-hour window, a new conversion rate must be established and agreed upon in writing.

**3.5** If the crypto amount received -- converted to USD at the time of network confirmation -- falls short of the Commission amount, Seller shall pay the difference within seven (7) business days via Stripe or wire transfer.
