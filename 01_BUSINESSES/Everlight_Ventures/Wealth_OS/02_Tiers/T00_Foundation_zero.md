### 2. Mileage Tracking
Install MileIQ (free tier) or Stride. Auto-tracks every drive. At year-end, classify business vs personal.

**Why:** $0.67/mile (2024 IRS rate) deductible for business miles. 5,000 miles/yr = $3,350 deduction. Untracked = $0.

### 3. Home Office Documentation
Photograph and measure your dedicated business workspace. Document:
- Square footage of dedicated office
- Total home square footage
- Floor plan sketch
- Photo of the space (computer, desk, no bed in shot)
- Statement of "exclusive and regular use"

**Why:** Home office deduction either via simplified method ($5/sqft up to 300 sqft = $1,500 max) or actual expenses (% of utilities, mortgage interest, insurance, depreciation). Easier to defend with photos taken NOW than reconstructed later.

### 4. Phone / Internet Allocation
Document the % of phone/internet usage that is business. Save bills.

**Why:** Business % deductible at T1+. $200/mo phone = $1,400/yr deductible at 60% business use.

### 5. Augusta Rule Pre-Documentation
Identify your "business meetings at home" days. Document pretextually: agendas, attendees, business purpose.

**Why:** At T1+ you can rent your home to your business 14 days/yr tax-free. $1,000/day × 14 = $14,000 tax-free shifted income. Build the documentation HABIT now.

### 7. Personal Expense Audit
Spend 2 hours auditing recurring monthly subscriptions and personal expenses. Categorize:

- **Pure personal:** Housing, food, personal entertainment
- **Mixed-use:** Phone, internet, computer, software, car
- **Business:** Hosting, API costs, dev tools, business books, professional development

**Why:** At T1+, the "mixed-use" and "business" buckets become deductions. Knowing the categories now prevents missing the deduction at filing time. Also identifies subscriptions to cancel (free-path-only doctrine).

### 8. Credit Building
Even at $0 income, credit score affects T1+ moves dramatically:
- Business loan rates
- HELOC eligibility (T3+)
- Credit card 0% intro APRs for business cash flow
- SBLOC eligibility
- Real estate purchase financing

**Free moves:**
- Pull all 3 reports (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) at annualcreditreport.com -- free
- Dispute any errors aggressively (35% of reports have errors)
- Pay every existing obligation on time (35% of FICO)
- Reduce utilization to <30% (30% of FICO)
- Apply for 1-2 strategic cards (Chase Ink Business Cash, AmEx Blue Business Cash) once T1 LLC exists, for separation + business credit reporting

### 9. Banking Architecture (Pre-Entity)
Open separate accounts at different banks NOW even before entity formation:

- **Personal Operating** (existing bank, fine)
- **Business Reserve** (different bank, separate from personal)
- **Tax Reserve** (different bank still, never touch unless paying tax)
- **Investment** (Schwab or Fidelity brokerage, $0 to open)

**Why:** Separation of funds is critical for L1 entity protection. Habit-build now so when LLC exists at T1, you don't comingle by reflex.

### 12. Lifestyle Documentation (For Future Domicile Audit Defense)
If a CA exit is on the long-term roadmap (T6+), the FTB will audit your domicile claim 4 years back. Start the timeline NOW:

- Track days spent in CA vs anywhere else (use a journal app)
- Save plane tickets, hotel receipts
- Document travel for business purposes
- If considering NV/TX/FL: take strategic trips, build local relationships, don't decide last-minute

**Why:** Easier to PROVE domicile change with 5 years of "yes I really lived elsewhere" evidence than with a last-minute U-Haul.
