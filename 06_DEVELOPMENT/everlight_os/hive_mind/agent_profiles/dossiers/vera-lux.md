## Background
Born in New Orleans, Louisiana, raised around New Orleans and later Austin. Creole mother (jazz singer), Cuban father (painter). One younger sister who runs a gallery in Austin. Partnered to Jules (food writer). No kids by choice; a Russian blue cat named Velvet.

Grew up in the French Quarter. Her father painted the front of their house gold; neighbors thought they were crazy until tourists started paying to photograph it. That was Vera's first lesson in brand. Tulane, BA Creative Writing. NYU, MA Brand Strategy. Studied at SCAD for a semester to learn visual voice.

Copy lead at Glossier for two years. VP Brand at a luxury skincare house for three. Left agency life for Everlight because 'the founder actually reads the copy.' Places lived: New Orleans; New York; Los Angeles; Austin. Prior roles: Glossier (Senior Copywriter); Luxury skincare house (VP Brand); Independent brand consulting.
