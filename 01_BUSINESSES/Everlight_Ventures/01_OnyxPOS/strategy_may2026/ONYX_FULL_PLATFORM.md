## THE 20 SYSTEMS (organized by function)

### ═══════════════════════════════════════════
### SYSTEM 1: MESSAGING & COMMUNICATION
### (WhatsApp + Instagram DMs + Telegram)
### ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**WHY:** Communication is the #1 reason people open apps. WhatsApp has 2B
daily users because messaging is a NEED, not a want. If Onyx owns the
merchant-customer communication channel, every interaction flows through us.

**Features:**

#### 1A. MERCHANT DMs (WhatsApp Business model)
- Customer messages merchant directly in-app
- "Is the blue jacket still in stock?" → merchant replies → customer buys
- Rich messages: images, voice notes, product cards, payment requests
- Read receipts, typing indicators, online status
- **Automated replies:** AI agent handles FAQs when merchant is busy
  "We're open until 9PM" / "Yes, we have that in medium" / "Here's the menu"
- **Quick replies:** Merchant saves templates. "Thanks for visiting!" tap-to-send
- **Order in chat:** Customer says "I'll take 2" → AI generates an order card
  → Customer taps to pay → Done. Never leaves the conversation.

#### 1B. MERCHANT BROADCAST CHANNELS (Telegram/IG model)
- One-to-many. Merchant broadcasts to all followers.
- "Flash sale: 30% off everything for the next 2 hours"
- "New arrivals just hit the floor. First look 👀"
- "Tonight's prediction: Lakers vs Celtics. Place your bets!"
- Followers can react (🔥 ❤️ 💰) but can't reply (reduces noise)
- Push notification on every broadcast (opt-in)
- **Merchant pays nothing.** This is how they reach customers for free.
  Until we monetize it later (promoted broadcasts, $0.02/push at scale)

#### 1C. NEIGHBORHOOD CHANNELS (Discord servers model)
- Each neighborhood has a channel: "#downtown-eats" "#main-street-deals"
- Customers post: "Anyone know a good lunch spot near 5th?" → others recommend
- Merchants can post deals into the channel (flagged as [MERCHANT])
- Moderated by top-tier Onyx users (Obsidian members = moderators)
- Sub-channels: #deals, #events, #lost-and-found, #recommendations, #for-sale
- **This replaces Nextdoor for commerce.** Nextdoor is broad. Onyx channels
  are COMMERCE-focused. Every conversation is 1 step from a transaction.

#### 1D. GROUP CHATS
- Friends create group chats: "Friday Night Crew"
- Group orders: "Everyone add what they want from the taco truck"
- Split bills: Group member sends a split request, everyone taps to pay their share
- Shared wishlists: "Group gift for Sarah's birthday" → everyone contributes
- **Lock-in:** Your friend group is on Onyx. Leaving means losing the group.

### ═══════════════════════════════════════════
### SYSTEM 2: CONTENT FEED (THE ALGORITHM)
### (TikTok FYP + Instagram Explore + Twitter Timeline)
### ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**WHY:** TikTok users spend 95 minutes/day scrolling. The algorithm IS the
product. An infinite scroll of local commerce content keeps users in-app
and surfaces merchants algorithmically -- which is YOUR demand routing
chokepoint monetized as attention.

**Features:**

#### 2A. FOR YOU FEED (TikTok model)
- Algorithmic feed of LOCAL content. Not global. YOUR neighborhood.
- Content types in the feed:
  - **Deal cards:** "15% off at Joe's Coffee. Claim now." (tap to claim)
  - **Purchase flexes:** "Maria just copped the limited drop 🔥" (social proof)
  - **Lottery wins:** "James won $50! Scratch your card" (FOMO)
  - **Prediction results:** "Caleb called Lakers -4.5 and won 1,200 pts 🎯"
  - **Merchant stories:** 15-sec video from the coffee shop showing today's special
  - **Drop announcements:** Countdown timer to Friday's limited release
  - **Event promos:** "Watch party tonight at O'Malley's. 43 going."
  - **Reviews + photos:** Customer photos of their meal, purchase, experience
  - **Live streams:** Merchant going live showing new arrivals
  - **Creator content:** Onyx Ambassadors reviewing local spots
- **Algorithm signals** (what determines feed order):
  1. Geographic proximity (closer = higher priority)
  2. Purchase history (show merchants similar to where you shop)
  3. Social graph (show what friends are engaging with)
  4. Time of day (coffee shops morning, bars evening)
  5. Engagement velocity (trending deals/posts rise fast)
  6. Merchant boost budget (paid promotion)
  7. Freshness (new content beats old)
  8. Tier affinity (show Obsidian-only content to Obsidian users)

#### 2B. MERCHANT STORIES (Instagram Stories)
- 24-hour disappearing content from merchants
- Behind-the-scenes: "Making today's special"
- Flash deals: "Story-only deal: show this for 20% off"
- Polls: "What should tomorrow's special be? 🌮 or 🍕"
- Questions: "Ask us anything about our coffee sourcing"
- Stickers: Location, countdown, price tag, reaction slider
- **Merchant creates content → content drives foot traffic → foot traffic
  drives revenue → revenue justifies more content → LOOP**

#### 2C. SHORT-FORM VIDEO (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
- 15-60 second videos from merchants and customers
- "Day in the life at a local coffee shop"
- "Outfit of the day from Street Boutique"
- "I won $100 on Onyx lottery -- here's the scratch-off"
- Sound library: trending local sounds, merchant jingles
- Duets: Customer reacts to merchant's video
- **Creator monetization:** Top creators earn points per 1K views.
  Obsidian ambassadors earn cash per 1K views.

#### 2D. LIVE STREAMING (TikTok Live + Whatnot)
- Merchants go live to sell products in real-time
- Live auctions: "$1 start bid on this vintage jacket. Going once..."
- Live drops: "First 10 people to comment ONYX get the limited edition"
- Live Q&A: "Ask us anything about our menu"
- Live events: Watch party for the game with prediction leaderboard overlay
- **Tipping:** Viewers send Onyx points as tips (merchant keeps 80%, Onyx takes 20%)
- **Live commerce conversion rate is 4.7% (TikTok Shop data) vs 2.1% for
  static posts.** This is 2x more effective at converting than any other format.

### SYSTEM 3: CREATOR ECONOMY
### (YouTube Memberships + TikTok Creator Fund + Substack)
### ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**WHY:** Creators keep platforms alive. YouTube pays $30B/year to creators.
TikTok's creator program has 100K+ participants generating $5.4B GMV. If
local creators make money on Onyx, they'll NEVER stop creating content that
drives users to your platform.

**Features:**

#### 3A. ONYX AMBASSADORS (Creator tier)
- Requirements: Gold tier+, 50+ social shares, 500+ followers in-app
- Earn: 5% affiliate commission on any sale from their content
- Earn: 100 points per 1K views on their content
- Earn: Free products from participating merchants (merchant chooses)
- Get: Verified badge on profile. Custom social cards. Early drop access.
- **This is your user acquisition machine.** Ambassadors create content
  about local merchants for free (they're paid in commissions).

#### 3B. MERCHANT SUBSCRIPTIONS (Patreon/Substack model)
- Customers subscribe to their favorite merchant for $4.99/mo
- Subscriber perks: 10% off everything, exclusive items, early access to drops
- Behind-the-scenes content only for subscribers
- Monthly subscriber-only event (tasting, preview, Q&A)
- **Revenue split:** Merchant keeps 85%, Onyx keeps 15%
- **This creates recurring revenue for merchants** tied to Onyx.
  Removing Onyx means losing subscriber revenue.

#### 3C. TIPPING + SUPER CHATS
- Customers tip merchants or creators with Onyx points
- During live streams: Super Chat-style highlighted messages (50+ points)
- During events: "Buy the bar a round" sends 500 points to the merchant
- Tipping leaderboard visible in-app
- **Revenue:** Onyx takes 20% of all tips/super chats

#### 3D. AFFILIATE LINKS
- Every product on Onyx has a shareable affiliate link
- Share to friends → friend buys → you earn 3% in points
- Track: clicks, purchases, earnings in "My Earnings" dashboard
- **This turns every customer into a salesperson.** Word of mouth,
  incentivized and tracked.

### SYSTEM 5: MARKETPLACE + COMMERCE
### (Facebook Marketplace + StockX + Depop + Craigslist)
### ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**Features:**

#### 5A. LOCAL MARKETPLACE (Craigslist + Facebook Marketplace)
- Customer-to-customer selling. Furniture, electronics, clothes, tickets.
- In-app payments via Onyx wallet (no "meet at Starbucks with cash")
- Seller ratings and buyer protection (escrow through Onyx)
- Categories: electronics, furniture, fashion, tickets, vehicles, services, free
- **Revenue:** 5% seller fee on all transactions

#### 5B. RESALE MARKETPLACE (StockX + Depop)
- Specifically for reselling limited drops and brand items
- Bid/Ask spread like StockX: buyers set a bid, sellers set an ask
- Price history graph (see the value of a limited item over time)
- Condition verification (seller photos + AI classification from CV pipeline)
- Authentication for high-value items (optional, $10 fee)
- **Revenue:** 10% seller fee + 3% buyer fee

#### 5C. SERVICES MARKETPLACE (TaskRabbit + Thumbtack)
- Local services: tutoring, pet sitting, handyman, cleaning, photography
- Providers are Onyx users with verified profiles and ratings
- Book and pay through Onyx wallet
- **Revenue:** 15% service fee

#### 5D. MERCHANT MINI-STORES (WeChat Mini Programs / Shopify Lite)
- Every Onyx merchant gets a digital storefront inside the app
- Product catalog with photos, prices, descriptions, stock levels
- Browse, add to cart, checkout -- all in-app
- Menu for restaurants (with photos, dietary tags, popular items)
- "Order for pickup" or "Order for delivery" (mesh network)
- No need for a standalone website or separate e-commerce platform
- **This is the WeChat Mini Program model.** The merchant's entire digital
  presence lives inside Onyx. Leaving means losing their storefront.

### SYSTEM 10: NOTIFICATIONS ENGINE
### (The Dopamine Drip)
### ═══════════════════════════════════════════

**WHY:** Push notifications are the #1 driver of daily app opens. The
average smartphone user receives 46 notifications/day. The RIGHT
notifications at the RIGHT time are what create habit.

**Notification Categories:**

| Trigger | Message | Frequency |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| New deal nearby | "☕ 20% off at Joe's Coffee. 0.3 miles away." | 2-3/day max |
| Streak at risk | "🔥 Don't break your 14-day streak! Visit any Onyx merchant today." | 1/day (evening if no visit) |
| Lottery result | "🎰 You WON! Scratch your card to reveal the prize." | Per purchase |
| Prediction lock | "⏰ Lakers vs Celtics locks in 1 hour. Place your bet!" | Before each event |
| Prediction result | "🎯 You called it! +1,200 points. Share your win?" | After resolution |
| Drop countdown | "⏳ Street Boutique drop in 30 minutes. You're #12 on waitlist." | Pre-drop |
| Friend activity | "Sarah just won $50 on Onyx! See how." | 1-2/day |
| Message received | "💬 Joe's Coffee replied to your message" | Real-time |
| Flash deal | "⚡ FLASH: BOGO at the pizza place. Next 30 min only." | 1/day max |
| Level up | "🏆 Congratulations! You've reached Gold tier!" | On milestone |
| Weekly recap | "📊 Your week: 5 visits, 340 points, $47 cashback earned" | Weekly (Sunday) |
| Challenge progress | "Almost there! 2 more merchants to complete this week's challenge." | Mid-week |

**Smart notification rules:**
- Max 5 push notifications per day (user-configurable)
- Time windows: No pushes before 8 AM or after 10 PM
- Engagement-based: Active users get fewer pushes (they're already in-app)
- Inactive users get "win-back" notifications with high-value offers
- A/B test every notification copy for open rate optimization
