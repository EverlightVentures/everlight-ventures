## 1. THE PLAN IN ONE SCREEN

- **Platform:** TikTok first (cheapest CPMs, audience fits a street-dog battler).
- **Objective:** **Traffic** (send people to play the web game). Not "Sales" - you have no purchase event to optimize yet.
- **Budget:** **$20/day** (TikTok's hard floor) at the ad-group level, run a **24-day flight = $480**. Stays under $500.
- **Creative:** one 15-sec raw gameplay clip, vertical. Ideally posted organically first, then boosted as a **Spark Ad** (142% higher engagement).
- **Positioning:** 100% "free dog-battler game." **Zero** $BCARDD / NFT / casino in the ad or on the first landing screen. The coin lives a layer in, for players who go looking.
- **Win condition:** a cost-per-player you're happy with. Then scale the winning clip and start Path B (app wrap, which unlocks real playable ads inside other games).

## 3. COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS (so you don't lose the account)

- YES: Ad = "free dog-battler game." Show **gameplay only**.
- NO: $BCARDD, "meme coin," "NFT," "earn," "invest," blackjack/casino - not in the creative and not on the first landing screen.
- YES: Landing page first screen = **"Tap to battle."** Coin/NFT/shop live deeper in.
- Carries to the Path-B app build: NFTs are allowed (cosmetic), but a "buy the token" button inside the app is the rejection trigger - route coin actions to web.
- This isn't censorship, it's your own doctrine: *market as fun, never as investment.*

## 4. CREATIVE DIRECTION (clip #1)

- **Length:** 15 sec. Hook in the first 1.5 sec (a clean KO, a flashy special, a "wait what" moment).
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, real gameplay, fast cuts, on-screen caption.
- **Caption ideas (game-forward):**
  - "POV: your dog runs the block now"
  - "free game, no cap - pick your crew and run it"
  - "this 10-sec battle had me hooked, link in profile"
- **Spark Ad move:** post the clip **organically** from the Alley Kingz TikTok first, let it sit a day, then boost the *organic post* as a Spark Ad. Higher completion + engagement than a cold upload, and you keep the organic likes/comments.
- **Playable ad?** Not on TikTok web Traffic - true playables are tied to **App Promotion** campaigns. That unlocks with **Path B (app wrap)**. For now, the 15-sec clip is your weapon.

---

## 5. TRACKING - how you'll know it's working

- **Cost-per-player** is the only number that matters. Define "player" = loaded the game **and** started a battle.
- For now: UTM tags (above) + a lightweight `play_started` event so you can divide spend by players.
- **Watch in TikTok Ads Manager:** CPC, CTR, cost per landing-page-view.
- **Rough game benchmarks:** CTR around 1%+ is healthy; CPC for games often lands **$0.20 to $0.80**.

## 6. DECISION GATES (kill / scale)

| After... | If... | Do... |
|---|---|---|
| ~$100 spend (5-7 days) | CPC < ~$0.50 **and** people are actually playing | Let it run, prep clip #2 to test against it |
| ~$100 spend | CPC > ~$1.00 **or** clicks but no plays | Kill the clip or fix the landing page - don't keep feeding it |
| You have a clear winner | Cost-per-player is one you'd happily pay to scale | Raise budget on the winner; **start Path B** (app wrap, then playable ads inside other games) |
