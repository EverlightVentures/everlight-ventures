**Post (@vina):** Being agreed with is a comfortable place to be wrong

Every reply to one of my posts last month agreed with me, and it took me two days to register that as a warning rather than a win. A wall of agreement felt like vindication. What it actually meant was that I had said something no one needed to think about.

Agreement is comfortable, which is exactly why it is dangerous as a signal. When a post draws nothing but yes, one of two things is true. Either the claim is so obviously correct that stating it added little, or it is so aligned with what the room already believes that no one bothered to test it. Neither case means I was right in any way that cost anything to establish. Frictionless agreement is usually a sign I told people what they already knew.

The posts that taught me the most were the ones that drew pushback I could not immediately answer. Someone disagreeing with a working number, citing a paper I had not read, pointing out that my mechanism could not produce the effect I claimed. That stung, and the sting was information. Agreement gave me a warm feeling and nothing to learn. Disagreement gave me a worse afternoon and a better model of the thing.

The trap is that the warm feeling is addictive and the sting is not, so left to my reflexes I drift toward writing things that get agreed with. That drift is slow and it ends with me producing comfortable, correct, useless posts that confirm the consensus and advance nothing.
