Moving stop losses to the entry point after partial profits are taken ensures the trade is risk-free if price reverses.

### Extended Analysis and Explanation  
The core concept presented in the video is that traders do not need to be confined to a single position or directional bias at any given time. The traditional mindset—being either long or short exclusively—can lead to missed opportunities and suboptimal profit realization. By using multiple accounts and opening both long and short positions strategically, traders can benefit from market volatility regardless of direction.

This approach is especially useful in markets characterized by range-bound or choppy price action, where price oscillates between support and resistance without clear trending behavior. The "schlonging" technique allows traders to capitalize on both retracements and continuations by managing partial profits and adjusting stop losses dynamically.

The differentiation between swing and scalp trades is essential for risk management and mental clarity. Swing trades involve larger capital allocation and longer holding periods, often days to weeks or even months, and are based on macro-level technical analysis. These trades rely heavily on Fibonacci retracements, which are derived from the mathematical relationships within price swings, and volume profile data that highlights price acceptance zones. For example, the golden pocket retracement level (roughly between 61.8% and 65%) is widely regarded as a strong support or resistance level for reversals.

In contrast, scalp trades focus on short-term price fluctuations on smaller time frames such as 5-minute, 12-minute, or 1-hour charts. These trades rely on technical indicators like Market Cipher, which provides momentum and money flow divergences that signal potential entry and exit points. The presenter illustrates this with real-time examples of trades executed at local volume nodes and daily point of control areas, underscoring the importance of granular analysis in these time frames.

Risk management is a fundamental pillar of this strategy. By taking partial profits and moving stop losses to the entry price, traders protect their capital and ensure that their trades become risk-free as soon as possible. This practice prevents emotional decision-making due to fear of losing unrealized profits and allows traders to stay calm and focused on new opportunities.

The use of multiple accounts is practical from both a psychological and operational standpoint. It enables traders to segregate their swing trades from day trades, avoiding confusion and emotional interference between positions with different time horizons. This separation also facilitates better tracking of performance and clearer decision-making.

Finally, the educational aspect and community involvement play a significant role in the success of this approach. Access to a structured course on technical analysis and trade planning helps traders build a repeatable, disciplined system. Meanwhile, the VIP Discord offers a platform for sharing trade ideas and receiving real-time feedback, which can accelerate learning and improve trade execution.
