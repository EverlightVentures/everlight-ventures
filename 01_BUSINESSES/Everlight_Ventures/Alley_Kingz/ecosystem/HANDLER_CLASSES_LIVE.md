# Handler Classes -- LIVE on Alley Kingz

**Shipped 2026-06-15. Verified in a real browser. Zero code errors.**

Every match now has a **commander** -- a dog "handler" you pick before you play.
Each one gives you a passive boost that's always on, plus a **special move** you
fire from a button next to your cards. It's the DMZ / battle-commander layer you
asked for, built right on top of the cards that are already in the game.

## The 6 commanders

| Handler | Special (tap to fire) | Always-on passive |
|---|---|---|
| **The Mender** | Drops a healing kennel -- heals your dogs in a circle | All your dogs slowly self-heal |
| **The Tracker** | Marks enemies so they take extra damage + reveals hiders | Kills charge your special faster |
| **The Shadow** | Cloaks one of your dogs (untargetable) + speed burst | All your dogs move faster |
| **The Rigger** | Drops a turret (gun / tesla / flak -- you pick) | Your buildings last longer |
| **The Bruiser** | War cry: nearby dogs hit harder + get a shield | Your whole squad takes less damage |
| **The Dealer ($BCARDD)** | Flips a card for a random jackpot (coins / pups / heal / gamble) | Earns you bonus value as the match runs |

The Dealer **is** card #0001, the $BCARDD mascot -- so the coin's character is the
casino commander. Its ultimate (a Bones unlock) is the "$BCARDD Blessing" coin bomb.

## How you use it
1. Tap **Handlers** on the main menu (new tile, next to Drip/Crew).
2. Pick your commander -- tap **Equip**. It sticks for every match.
3. In a match, a round **special button** sits left of your cards. It fills up over
   time. When it's ready, tap it (it glows), then tap the board to place/fire.
4. Win matches to earn **Bones**. Spend Bones in the Handlers screen to unlock
   **skill-tree upgrades** -- bigger radius, faster recharge, extra charges, and each
   commander's capstone (the Dealer's is the coin-bomb ultimate).
