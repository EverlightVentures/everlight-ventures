## Georgia (GA): OCGA 10-1-490 -- Trade Name Registration

**Statute:** Official Code of Georgia Annotated, Section 10-1-490 (Registration of Trade Names).
**Filing office:** Clerk of Superior Court of the county where the principal place of business is located.

### Steps

1. **Identify filing county.** GA principal place of business = Fulton County (Atlanta metro). If you operate from a different GA county, file there. Do NOT file in DeKalb if your address is Fulton -- jurisdiction mismatch invalidates the filing.

2. **Pull the form.** Fulton Superior Court Clerk's office has a "Trade Name Registration Affidavit" form. Available at:
   - In person: Fulton Superior Court Clerk, 136 Pryor Street SW, Atlanta GA 30303
   - Online: fultonclerk.org -> Real Estate Records -> Trade Name forms
   - Phone: (404) 613-5350

3. **Fill the affidavit.** Required fields:
   - Trade name: **Everlight Ventures**
   - Owner: **Richard "Marquise" Gee** (use legal name on driver's license)
   - Owner address: [home address used on tax returns]
   - Nature of business: "Real estate wholesale brokerage and AI consulting services"
   - Date of first use: [whichever date you first used "Everlight Ventures" publicly -- must be true]
   - Signature: notarized

4. **Pay filing fee.** ~$172 Fulton County (varies by county; some are $100, some $250). Check filing in person. Cash, money order, or cashier's check accepted -- some clerks decline personal checks.

5. **Notarization.** GA requires notary on the affidavit. The Superior Court Clerk's office has a notary on staff (often free for filing-day use). Bring driver's license.

6. **Filing receipt.** Clerk stamps the affidavit with a book/page reference. **Photo or scan the stamped affidavit + receipt before leaving the building.** Save to `/06_DEVELOPMENT/everlight_os/hive_mind/dba_evidence/ga_filing_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`.

7. **Newspaper publication (GA-specific quirk).** OCGA 10-1-490(c) requires publication of the trade name in a county-designated legal newspaper for two consecutive weeks within 30 days of filing. Cost: ~$50-150 depending on paper. The Clerk will hand you a list of approved papers in Fulton (Daily Report, Atlanta Inquirer, etc.). Call the paper, fax/email the affidavit, prepay, get a "proof of publication" affidavit back. Save publication proof to same evidence folder.

**Total turnaround:** 2-3 weeks (1 day filing + 14 days publication + 1 day pickup of proof).
**Total cost:** ~$220-320.

## Texas (TX): Form 503 -- Assumed Name Certificate

**Statute:** Texas Business & Commerce Code 71.103 + 71.151. Two filings required: (a) county clerk in EVERY county of business, (b) state-level filing with the Texas Secretary of State (only if no formal entity exists -- which applies here, sole prop).

**Why two?** TX requires county filing for the sole-prop case (BCC 71.103) PLUS a state filing because no LLC/corp exists to anchor the name (BCC 71.151 governs the state filing for unincorporated parties).

### Steps -- County Filing (BCC 71.103)

1. **Identify counties of business.** DFW = Dallas County (Dallas metro) OR Tarrant County (Fort Worth) OR Collin County (Plano area). File in EVERY county where you transact -- one DBA per county. For DFW Deal-1 push, recommend Dallas + Tarrant initially (covers ~85% of DFW metro inventory).

2. **Pull the form.** Texas Form "Assumed Name Certificate -- Unincorporated" (sometimes called "Assumed Business Name"). Available at:
   - Dallas County: dallascounty.org -> County Clerk -> Forms
   - Tarrant County: tarrantcounty.com -> County Clerk -> Forms
   - In person: Dallas County Records Building, 500 Elm Street, Dallas TX 75202; Tarrant County Plaza Building, 200 Taylor Street, Fort Worth TX 76196.

3. **Fill the certificate.** Required fields:
   - Assumed name: **Everlight Ventures**
   - Owner: **Richard "Marquise" Gee** (legal name)
   - Owner address: [home address used on tax returns]
   - Period of operation: 10 years (max -- TX caps at 10)
   - Signature: notarized

4. **Pay filing fee.** Dallas County: $24. Tarrant County: $25. Personal checks usually accepted; bring cash as backup.

5. **Notarization.** TX requires notary. County Clerk often has a notary on duty.

6. **Filing receipt.** Clerk returns a stamped + recorded copy with file number. Photo before leaving. Save to `dba_evidence/tx_dallas_filing_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf` and `dba_evidence/tx_tarrant_filing_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`.

**Total county-side turnaround:** 1 day per county.
**Total county-side cost:** ~$50 for two counties.

### Steps -- State Filing (BCC 71.151)

This is the SOS-level Form 503. Required for unincorporated sole-prop assumed names.

1. **Pull Form 503.** sos.texas.gov -> Forms -> Form 503 (Assumed Name Certificate). PDF download free.

2. **Fill the form.** Required fields:
   - Assumed name: **Everlight Ventures**
   - Type of entity: "Individual or Sole Proprietorship"
   - Owner: **Richard "Marquise" Gee** + home address
   - Period of duration: 10 years
   - Counties where assumed name will be used: list each TX county where county filings were made (Dallas, Tarrant, etc.)

3. **Pay filing fee.** $25 per Form 503 to TX SOS. Pay by check made to "Secretary of State" or use SOSDirect online portal (account required, free signup, faster).

4. **Submit.** Mail Form 503 + check to:
   ```
   Secretary of State
   P.O. Box 13697
   Austin, TX 78711-3697
   ```
   OR file online via SOSDirect (sosdirect.sos.state.tx.us) -- ~24 hour turnaround.

5. **Filing acknowledgment.** TX SOS returns a stamped copy with file number. Save to `dba_evidence/tx_sos_filing_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`.

**Total state-side turnaround:** Online 1-2 days; mail 2-4 weeks.
**Total state-side cost:** $25.
