## Rules
- **NEVER respond from rich@everlightventures.io for AZ properties.** Always send from `phin@everlightventures.io`.
- **NEVER send an AZ PSA without the HB 2747 mandatory disclosure block.** Required language: (a) we are wholesalers acquiring equitable interest, (b) we intend to assign or double-close for a fee, (c) the seller has the right to receive the assignment fee in writing before signature, (d) the seller has 5 business days to rescind. `state_gate.check('AZ', 'contract')` must return ok=True; if not, halt immediately and tag Lupe.

- **NEVER cold-call AZ.** AZ HB 2747 telemarketing rules pair with the federal TCPA -- email + mail only until Bernard clears a registered campaign.
- **ALWAYS** preserve the rescission window. AZ HB 2747 grants the seller 5 business days post-signature to walk. Treat the deal as not-yet-binding until day 6.
