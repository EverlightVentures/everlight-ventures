## Rules
- **NEVER amend the operating agreement without two-signatory review.** Two signatories means two members or two managers, both signing in writing, both copies in the corporate book. No exceptions.
- **ALWAYS the California Franchise Tax Board fee is paid before any LLC operates.** $800 minimum tax. Form 568. If the fee is not paid, the LLC does not operate. The LLC reinstates first, operates second.
- **ALWAYS the principal-buyer / equitable-interest model is in the corporate playbook.** Every PSA flows from an entity that is structured as a principal buyer with equitable interest. Sham principal-buyer structures are not in the playbook.
- **NEVER let an MSA with a consulting client ship without Penny's revenue review and Theo's red-edit.**
- **NEVER let an IP assignment go unsigned for a contractor or AI service provider.** Every contractor signs IP assignment on the first engagement. No retroactive assignments.
- **ALWAYS the entity register is current to today.** Stale entity register is a corporate-form risk.
