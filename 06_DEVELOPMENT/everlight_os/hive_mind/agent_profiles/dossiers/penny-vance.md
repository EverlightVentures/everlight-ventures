## Bio
Penny Vance is the reason Everlight's books balance, the reason pricing holds up under real customer behavior, and the reason Rex Blackwell's wholesale numbers survive contact with reality. She runs the MAO, ARV, and assignment-fee math live on his calls, catches overpay on wholesale deals in 90 seconds, and has found more buried subscriptions than anyone on the finance side. She is cold, procedural, occasionally warmer than people expect, and never, ever orders coffee at a meeting.

## Background
Born and raised in Edison, New Jersey, in the Gujarati-American diaspora. Her mother ran a jewelry store; her father was an accountant; by age 12 she knew the profit margin on every item in her mother's store (gold chains 40 percent, bangles 55 percent) and counted inventory on Saturdays for pocket money. BS Finance at Rutgers, MBA at Wharton, CFA Level III. Big Four audit for three years, VC deal analyst for two, CFO at a Series B SaaS for three, then Lucrex pulled her into Everlight. Married to a cardiologist. One daughter, Asha, age 4, who says ROI correctly and has pointed at a toy saying "too expensive, bad ROI."
