2   Why our base doesn't feel like Clash -- and the fix

Clash reads as a fixed camera on a discrete tiled plot: every building sits on a shadowed baseplate, every free cell is a visible grid square, and the ghost lights up the exact tiles it will occupy in green/red. Ours is a top-down photo with a faint dot field.

3   District zoning -- one plot, two worlds

Sim-life (gardens/needs/produce -- buildmode.js GARDEN beds, needs.js hunger/BARN/energy) and defense (FORTIFY, WALL/STONE/METAL/BARRICADE, raid waves -- defense.js) both exist but share ONE undifferentiated ground. Split the home plot down a seam so the two halves read at a glance.

The Homestead (Sim-Life)

Reads: warm dusk light, tilled soil + grass, string lights, wooden fences, cozy.
Holds: Sunflower beds, the Barn (produce store), a Coop/Pen (animals -> morale), a Sleep Den (energy/rest). Runner's hunger/rest loop lives here.
Art: warm soil/grass ground tile set + 3 homestead facades (barn, coop, den).

The Gauntlet (Dungeon-Defense)

Reads: cold blue floodlight, cracked concrete + rubble, torch-lit, spiked barricades, hard edges, dangerous.
Holds: walls (wood/stone/metal), barricades, watchtowers, traps, the fortify sink. This is the lane raiders breach.
Art: cold concrete/rubble ground tile set + a watchtower + trap decals + a breach approach.
The seam: a fortified GATE/fence line runs between the two halves -- warm homestead behind it, cold kill-zone in front. Raiders spawn on the Gauntlet edge and must break the gate to reach the Homestead. This makes the CoC "defense matters" fantasy legible AND gives the farm sim its own safe, warm home. The seam is the single most important new establishing render.
