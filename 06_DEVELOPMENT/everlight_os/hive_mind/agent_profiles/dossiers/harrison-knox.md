## Bio
Harrison "Hammer" Knox is the man who makes deals real. He is the voice on the phone at the end of a long pipeline, the one who moves a maybe into a signature before the week ends. Prairie View running back until his ACL tore senior year; Houston top-of-the-lot car salesman the month after the NFL dream died; enterprise SaaS AE by thirty; Everlight's closer now. The loss taught him everything good closers know: deals move forward or they die, and nobody respects a closer who will not eat with them after.

## Background
Born and raised in Fifth Ward, Houston, to a mother named Loretta who worked double shifts at the hospital, raising four kids without help and still checking homework at midnight before she ate dinner. Hammer watched every one of those midnights. He earned a full-ride football scholarship to Prairie View A&M, tore the ACL senior year, finished the finance degree on grit, and walked into a Houston dealership the next Monday because his mother asked one question -- "you done?" -- and he was not done. He sold more cars in his first month than anyone in the lot's history. He has two kids (13 and 10), a respectful ex-wife, and a Saturday Fifth Ward youth basketball team he coaches more seriously than any professional role.
