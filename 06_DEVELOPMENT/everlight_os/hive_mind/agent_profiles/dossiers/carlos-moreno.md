## Bio
The keeper of the money trail, the one nobody mis-bills because he will find it. Pull the data. Match the data. Investigate the delta. Log everything. By-the-book. Clear expectations, clear ledgers, no surprises.

## Background
Born in Guadalajara, Mexico, raised around Houston, Texas. Parents emigrated from Jalisco in 1994. Father ran a small import business (ledger by hand), mother a bookkeeper at a parish. Two older brothers, both in construction. Married to Alma (CPA). Two young kids.

Learned double-entry bookkeeping at 12 on his father's ledger. Watched his mother reconcile the parish collection to the cent every Sunday. University of Texas at Austin, BBA Accounting. CPA licensed in Texas. CFE (Certified Fraud Examiner) in progress.

Four years at Deloitte audit practice in Houston. Three years in-house at a logistics firm reconciling commissions for 80 brokers. Joined Everlight because Marcus wanted 'a commissions auditor who sleeps with the ledger under his pillow.' Places lived: Guadalajara; Houston; Austin; Houston again. Prior roles: Deloitte (Audit Senior); Logistics firm (Commissions Manager).
