## How to use this

Champ, three calls, 6-8 minutes each. You read the opener, you ask the three closing questions, you log the answers. Do not improv. Do not over-explain. Title firms talk to wholesalers all day, they want signal not story.

## OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection A: "We don't work with wholesalers."

> Understood, champ. I am not asking you to work outside your model. I am buying as principal, holding contract, and closing through your office. Whether I assign or double-close depends on the deal, but the closing instructions come from me as buyer. Same as any cash investor, just with a wholesale exit. Worth a 5-minute meeting?

### Objection B: "We'd need to see your LLC docs."

> I'm sole prop DBA right now, Everlight Ventures, registered DBA paperwork on file. LLC is the next move post-first-close. If sole prop is a hard stop for your office, tell me now and I'll route my first deal to a firm that takes DBA, then circle back to you when the LLC is stamped. No hard feelings either way.

### Objection C: "We don't do double-close in 14 days."

> Good, I assignment-close only. Double-close is the structure where the wholesaler pays title fees on both legs and that's not how I operate. I bring you a signed assignment and a cash buyer, the buyer pays your standard closing costs, I get my assignment fee on the closing statement. Clean and simple. Worth a 5-minute follow-up?

### Title-fee posture: TAKE THE DEAL if it's profitable

**Default ask:** buyer pays title fees through standard closing costs, no charges on our wholesale side.

**If they push back with a wholesale-side fee:** stay calm, negotiate, but **don't walk over fees**:
1. "Can that be baked into the buyer's closing costs?"
2. "Can it come off the seller's side instead?"
3. If both no -- **accept it and close the deal**, as long as we still walk away net-positive.

**The only walk-aways:** (a) the deal goes net-NEGATIVE after fees, or (b) the structure is illegal (RESPA kickbacks, advance fees, undisclosed-at-table deductions).

The principle: **we need the first deal more than we need to win the fee fight**. See `MONEY_FLOW.md` "TAKE THE DEAL Rule" for the full posture.
