# MASTER SHOTS
## Full-city, trailer, and site-hero plates

These are the shots that establish the world rather than a single district. Generate
these FIRST per the pipeline doctrine (trailer first, then P1/P2/P3 batches) -- they
set the look every district plate then has to match.

---

## CITY ESTABLISHING (the money shot)

```
Epic drone shot pulling back from a scarred central plaza at dawn, revealing a full
three-by-three grid of distinct city districts, each with its own color signature,
warm gold home turf at the center, cold teal glass heights to the northeast, rust
and forge orange industry to the east and west, magenta neon hustle to the north and
south, cold violet tech at the southeast, a razor-wire checkpoint sealing the
northwest, a collapsed bridge chasm sealing the southwest, surrounding walls holding
it all in, a city of dogs built on hierarchy and scrap, golden hour light, massive
scale, cinematic scope, 35mm anamorphic, film grain
```

## FACTION CONFRONTATION (trailer beat)

```
Rapid intercut sequence, four dogs each in their own district and their own light,
a chrome-armored K9 in cold teal glass heights, a fast-moving Zoomie hustler in
magenta neon commerce, a scrap-welded Boneguard in rust-orange forge light, a
flickering Leashbreak phantom in cold violet dock haze, each shot holding just long
enough to read the color, then cutting to a single empty gold-lit street in the
neutral center where all four converge, tension building with no dialogue, pure
visual storytelling, epic trailer energy, anamorphic, film grain
```

## GRID TRANSITION (UI / map-screen plate)

```
Slow overhead orthographic drift across a stylized city grid at night, nine distinct
district tiles each glowing its own signature color, connecting streets lit between
them, two tiles darkened and sealed, the neutral gold tile at the center brightest,
clean readable top-down composition suitable for a game map interface, atmospheric
haze between tiles, cinematic but legible, no text overlays
```

## BLOOD MOON VARIANT (season event -- NeonReach)

```
Wide shot of a glass-tower skyline under a blood moon, the sky turned arterial red,
cold teal neon refusing to warm against it, reflections doubling the moon in every
surface, a single dog silhouetted on a rooftop edge looking out, the district beautiful
and wrong at once, seasonal event key art, dramatic and ominous, anamorphic, film grain
```

Canon note: the blood-moon flavor line lives in `game/systems/seasons.js` and names
the drop **NeonReach**. That name applies to the skyline drop, not to the district --
the district is **NEON HEIGHTS**. Do not swap them.
