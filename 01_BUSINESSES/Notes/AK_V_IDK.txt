Research Summary: Best Garage Systems for Your War Rig Shop

1. CSR Racing 2 — The Gold Standard for Part-Upgrading Economy
This is probably your closest reference. CSR2 uses a 7-part upgrade system with 5 stages each (35 total upgrades per car), and it ties directly into a gacha/economy loop that mirrors your card-copy + gold system.

The Parts & Upgrade Stages:
Engine → Re-mapped ECU → High-performance camshafts → Full race cylinder head → Forged bottom end → Fully blueprinted engineering- Turbo → Updated actuator → Improved intercooler → Enlarged turbo housing → Ball bearing conversion → Twin turbos
- Intake → Performance panel filter → Induction kit → Stainless steel exhaust → Stainless steel header → Racing inlet manifold
- Nitrous → Dry system → Higher capacity bottle → Wet system → Direct port delivery → Re-jetted system
- Body → Strip unnecessary weight → Polycarbonate windows → Remove power steering/AC → Sump shield/smooth undertray → Painted carbon fiber panels
- Tires → Premium road → Sports → Soft compound sports → Grooved slicks → Slicks
- Transmission → Upgraded clutch → Composite axles/propshaft → Quickshift gear lever → Limited slip differential → Sequential transmission
Key Economy Mechanics:
Stage 4 & 5 parts are imports that take real time to deliver (2 min to 40 min depending on tier) — or you can pay gold to rush them. This creates a natural monetization sink.
- You can strip duplicate cars for rare Stage 6 parts and fusion parts. This is huge for your design: players who pull duplicate War Rigs from packs can break them down for exclusive parts.
- Each part upgrade has trade-offs (e.g., tire upgrades increase grip but reduce top speed; body upgrades reduce weight but sometimes reduce grip). This creates meaningful build diversity. 
How this maps to your game:
Your War Rigs could have 7-8 component slots (Engine, Turbo, Armor Plating, Weapon Mount, Tires/Suspension, Nitrous/Boost, Transmission, Exhaust)
- Each component has 5 upgrade stages with real-world mechanical names (like CSR2 does) to give car junkies that garage feel
- Duplicate War Rigs = scrap for Stage 6 "Legendary" parts or exclusive weapon blueprints
- Stage 4-5 upgrades have delivery timers that can be rushed with gold (ties into your existing economy)

2. CarX Street — Open-World Tuning with Fuel & Physics
CarX Street offers expansive part-swapping where you tune rides for specific race types (drift vs. grip vs. speed). It also has a fuel system that gates how much you can play, and club systems for social progression. 

Takeaways for your game:
Contextual tuning: Let players save "loadouts" for their War Rig — one build optimized for speed/racing, another for heavy combat, another for raid defense
- Fuel/battery system: War Rigs could require "fuel cells" or "energy cores" to deploy in battle — creates a resource sink and prevents overuse of maxed-out rigs
- Club/guild garages: Let faction members share garage space or donate parts to each other

3. NFS No Limits — 2.5 Million Customization Combos
NFS No Limits offers body kits, performance boosts, paint jobs, and over 2.5 million visual combinations. It also runs limited-time events for exclusive cars. 

Takeaways for your game:
Visual depth matters: Custom wheels, paint jobs, decals, body kits, exhaust tips, underglow, window tint — all of this feeds player identity
- Limited-time event cars: Release "Legendary War Rigs" during seasonal events that can only be obtained for a window, creating FOMO
- Body kits that change the rig's silhouette and hitbox — not just cosmetic, but affects gameplay (smaller rig = harder to hit, larger rig = more armor/weapon slots)

4. Twisted Metal (2012) — Vehicle + Weapon Synergy
This is your combat reference. In Twisted Metal, each vehicle has a unique Special Weapon with infinite ammo but a recharge timer. Some vehicles have two Special Weapons that create combo opportunities. 

Examples of Special Weapons:
Death Warrant: Chain Gun + Zoomy Missiles combo
- Road Boat: Magnetic projectile that pulls enemies in, then machine-guns them or charges a magnet launch
- Reaper: Chainsaw (homing, but weaker) vs. charged chainsaw (no homing, but 2x damage) — risk/reward choice
- Shadow: Soul projectile that can be detonated remotely, bounce off walls, or go through walls
- Talon: Mega-Gun Turret + Mega-Magnet that can pick up enemies and drop them from height (Death Drop technique)
Key Design Insight: In Twisted Metal (2012), any character can pilot any vehicle — but the vehicle's Special Weapon defines its playstyle. In your game, the Dog (hero) + War Rig (vehicle) pairing should create synergy abilities:
A "Pyro" Dog in a flamethrower War Rig = amplified fire damage + fire resistance
- A "Speed" Dog in a lightweight Porsche-style rig = nitrous recharge boost + drift damage
- A "Tank" Dog in a heavy armored rig = ramming damage multiplier + shield overcharge

5. Apex Racing — Differential Tuning & Real Tracks
Apex Racing allows players to modify all car parts and tune differentials for performance tweaking. It also features real-life tracks. 

Takeaways:
Granular tuning sliders: Let advanced players adjust suspension stiffness, gear ratios, brake bias, differential lock — this is the "car junkie" depth you want
- Test drive mode: A garage feature where players can test their rig on a dummy track before committing to a build
