## Risk Footnotes by Stream

- **Wholesale:** highest legal-attack surface. Every state has a different statute. Honor `state_gates.json` strictly. CA pre-foreclosure is the existential risk because it is criminal not just civil.
- **AI consulting:** lowest legal risk if MSA is solid. IP-assignment language and limitation-of-liability cap are the two clauses that actually matter when something goes sideways.
- **SaaS:** TOS + DPA + privacy notice + Stripe = baseline. SOC2 becomes worth-the-money when an enterprise customer asks (T2 or T3).
- **Broker OS finder:** the line is securities vs commercial. Stay on commercial. NY has no exemption. CA Corp Code 25206.1 looks like an exemption but is a trap if the deal smells like investment.
- **Publishing:** lowest legal risk. KDP TOS and copyright registration are the only items.
- **Casino:** highest regulatory complexity in the whole portfolio. Anjouan offshore + US sweepstakes is a workable dual-market structure but the implementation has to be airtight. Geo-block, AMOE, sweep terms, age verification, RG controls. I want a full review before any meaningful UA dollar.
- **XLM bot:** the cliff is "are you trading customer money." Marquise's own capital is fine. SaaS where customer runs the tool is fine with disclaimers. Pooling or perf-fee is a registration trigger that costs more to unwind than to do right the first time.
