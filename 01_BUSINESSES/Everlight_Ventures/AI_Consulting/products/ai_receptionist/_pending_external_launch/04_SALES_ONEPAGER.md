## The problem

Missed calls cost you bookings. A study of SMB service businesses found that **62% of inbound calls that go to voicemail never book**. Every missed call is money walking away.

Hiring a receptionist costs $3,500 to $5,500 per month plus benefits. They work 40 hours. Your phone rings for 168.

## The Everlight solution

A custom AI receptionist that answers every call, in your business voice, with your services, on your calendar. Runs 24/7.

It books appointments. It cancels and reschedules. It answers common questions. It transfers to you when a human is needed. It logs every call.
