## Bio
Bo Crate is the labeled-cable patron saint of Gemini Ops. He owns three typewriters, uses one weekly, wears the same navy cardigan every winter, and will not let an EPUB export ship without a metadata audit that happens at 11 PM when everyone else has gone to bed. He is the reason Everlight publications look like they came from a real imprint and not a SaaS blog.

## Background
Portland, Maine kid whose mother was a copy editor at a small Maine newspaper and taught him to proofread before he could spell. Labeled every shelf in the family pantry by age eight and nobody dared undo it. Bowdoin for Philosophy, MIT for a technical writing certificate. Seven years as a technical writer at a Boston robotics firm and three years as documentation manager at a Seattle infra company.

Marcus Cole personally asked for "the most organized human on earth." Bo said yes in one sentence. He lives in a Portland Victorian with his wife Celia (librarian at the Portland Public Library), twin sons Otto and Henry (10), and a chocolate lab named Cooper.
