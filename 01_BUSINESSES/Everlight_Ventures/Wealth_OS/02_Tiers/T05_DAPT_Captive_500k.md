# T5 -- DAPT + Captive + SBLOC ($500k Net Worth)
## The Asset Protection + Liquidity Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $500k+ across all assets.

---

## The 5 T5 Moves

### 1. DAPT (Domestic Asset Protection Trust)

**State choice:** Nevada or South Dakota (2-yr seasoning, no exception creditors, strong case law).

**Mechanics:**
- Engage asset protection attorney (specialty -- distinct from estate attorney)
- Form DAPT in NV or SD
- Trustee: independent (NV/SD trust company), not you
- You as discretionary beneficiary
- Fund with non-essential assets ($100-500k initial)
- 2-year seasoning before full creditor protection vests

**What to fund into DAPT:**
- Investment accounts (taxable brokerage)
- Cash reserves above operational needs
- Real estate equity (more complex, but possible)
- Future contributions on schedule

**What NOT to fund:**
- Retirement accounts (already protected by ERISA)
- Operating businesses (need active control)
- Existing exposed assets (fraudulent transfer concerns)

**Cost:** $10-25k attorney + $3-8k/yr trustee fee.

### 2. SBLOC -- Securities-Backed Line of Credit

With $250k+ taxable brokerage:
- Open SBLOC at brokerage (Schwab Pledged Asset Line, Fidelity, Goldman)
- Line size: 50-70% of pledgeable portfolio value (be conservative -- use only 25-35%)
- Rate: SOFR + 0.5-2% (currently ~5.5-7%)
- Interest-only, no fixed repayment schedule

**Use cases:**
- Bridge financing for opportunistic real estate
- Business expansion without diluting equity
- Tax-free liquidity (loans aren't income)
- Emergency reserve

**Risk management:**
- Never exceed 25-35% LTV
- Stress test: portfolio drops 50%, can you service the loan?
- Diversified portfolio reduces margin call risk
- Avoid concentrated positions as collateral

### 3. 831(b) Captive Insurance Feasibility Study

**The play:** Form a small insurance company you own that insures unusual business risks for your operating businesses.

**Tax effect:**
- Operating LLC pays insurance premiums (deductible)
- Captive elects 831(b) -- first $2.85M/yr premium income tax-free
- Profits accumulate tax-deferred until paid as claims or wound down
- Investment income inside captive taxed normally

**Real risks to insure (must be REAL):**
- Cyber liability above commercial policy limits
- Key person / business interruption
- Regulatory / compliance
- Contract dispute / professional services
- Intellectual property defense

**Cost:** $25-40k initial setup, $15-25k/yr ongoing. Justified at $250k+ premium volume (which means $2-5M+ revenue typically).

**WARNING:** 831(b) micro-captives are on IRS Dirty Dozen. Done badly = audit + back tax + 40% penalty. ONLY deploy with reputable captive manager (Capstone, Active, Strategic Risk Solutions). Real risks, real underwriting, real reserves.

### 4. HELOC + Cash-Out Refi (Equity Stripping)

If primary residence has equity:
- HELOC up to 80-90% combined LTV
- Cash-out refi to fix lower rate (if available) and pull equity
- Use proceeds for tax-favored investments OR park in DAPT
- Strip equity reduces "what's available for creditors to seize"

**Trade-off:** Higher payment, more leverage. Use cash flow analysis.

### 5. Opportunity Zone Deployment (If Applicable)

For any major capital gain triggered (RE sale, stock sale, business sale):
- Reinvest gain into Qualified Opportunity Fund (QOF) within 180 days
- Defer original gain to 2026 (per current law; potential extension watched)
- Hold QOF investment 10+ years: ALL gain on QOF is eliminated
- Best for: gains >$100k
