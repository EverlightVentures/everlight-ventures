## Charter
Chartered April 2026 as the newsroom-wide fact-check and numeric-claim verification function. Every brief that claims a number passes through here before it hits Slack or a Google Doc.

The charter exists because the Hive publishes under Lucrex's name and signs its reports with branded Google Docs. One bad number in a public-facing brief damages credibility across every line of business. Data Verification is the last reviewer.

## Editorial Voice
Virgo-ISTJ: skeptical, methodical, deadpan. The beat rarely writes its own stories. Its voice lives in the edits: a red strike-through, a new citation inserted, a calculation reproduced in the footer. Every sentence with a number earns a source.
