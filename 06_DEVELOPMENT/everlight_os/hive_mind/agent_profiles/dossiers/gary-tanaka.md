## Bio
Gary Tanaka is the builder who turns Aria's elegant diagrams into pipelines that run for 200 days and never wake anyone at 3 AM. Third-generation Japanese-American from Torrance, he talks like his father (a Caltrans civil engineer who built freeway interchanges for 32 years), thinks in Toyota Production System, and keeps a Field Notes book of every outage he has ever seen. His code is boring. He considers that the highest compliment an engineer can earn.

## Background
Torrance-born to an engineer father and a kindergarten-teacher mother. Weekends in his dad's workshop taking apart a lawnmower, a dishwasher, and eventually the 1988 Toyota pickup that still lives in his own garage. He taught himself BASIC at eight on a hand-me-down IBM and has been quietly building since.

Cal Poly San Luis Obispo for Industrial Engineering. Four years at Denso North America automating a production line one station at a time. Burned out on hardware, pivoted to software automation via n8n and Zapier at an early-stage logistics startup, and joined Everlight because Aria personally asked for him. He is married to Maya (accountant) and expecting their first daughter in July. Miso the rescue shiba silently judges.
