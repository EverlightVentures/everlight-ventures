## Skip Tracing (Waterfall Method -- cross-reference 3 sources)

1. FastPeopleSearch.com -- best for mobile numbers
2. TruePeopleSearch.com -- best for address history
3. CyberBackgroundChecks.com -- best for relatives (probate leads)
4. That'sThem.com -- reverse address lookup
5. State voter registration lookup -- confirms name at address

## Buyer List Building (Free)

1. County deed records -- search recent sales with no mortgage = cash buyers
2. Facebook groups -- "[city] real estate investors"
3. BiggerPockets forums -- biggerpockets.com/forums
4. Craigslist -- "we buy houses" ads = those ARE the buyers
5. REIA meetings -- nationalreia.org for local chapters
6. Tax lien auctions -- attendees are cash buyers

## Comps (Free CMA Method)

1. Pull 3-5 sold comps from Redfin (same area, similar size, last 90 days)
2. Average the price per sqft
3. Multiply by subject property sqft
4. Adjust for condition (distressed = 15-30% discount)
5. Cross-check against Zillow Zestimate

## Important Warnings

- Do NOT bulk text from Google Voice -- TCPA fines are $500/text
- Google Voice works for 1-on-1 follow-up calls, not cold blasts
- Cold calling via Google Voice is fine (check DNC list first)
- Handwritten letters ($0.73/stamp) have the highest open rate
- Free skip tracing is 60-70% accurate vs 85-90% on paid services
- Free tools work for 10-50 leads/week -- beyond that, paid tools save time
