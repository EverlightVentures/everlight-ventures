# THE YARDS -- `THE_YARDS`
## 🦴 The Rusted City (Boneguard Crew) · west (0,1) · SCRAP district · 6% tax

**Canon:** industrial yards, scrap, **stray turf**. Ground `docks`. Tint `12,15,13`.
Ambient rust `#C9772E`. Ethos: underground, resourceful, underestimated.
Crew aliases: The Boneyard Mob, Crypt Kings, Marrow Syndicate.

**Must not contradict:** THE YARDS is where **strays** live -- dogs with no crew and
no colors. The Boneguard hold the turf, but the strays are the population. Both must
be visible. Nothing here is wasted; everything is repurposed. That is the whole ethos.
