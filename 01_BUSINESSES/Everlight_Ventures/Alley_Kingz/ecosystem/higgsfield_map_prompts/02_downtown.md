# DOWNTOWN -- `DOWNTOWN`
## ⚡ The Unbound City (Zoomie Syndicate) · north (1,0) · PRODUCE district · 6% tax

**Canon:** commerce, the come-up. Ground `midtown`. Tint `14,12,18`.
Ambient magenta `#FF2E88`. Ethos: hungry underdogs, all speed.
Crew aliases: Zoomie Riot, Nitro Pack, The Burnouts.

**Must not contradict:** Unbound colors are magenta `#FF2E88` (district) and green
`#7CFFB0` (crew badge). NOT yellow. Downtown is hustle, not luxury -- everything here
is bought, sold, or flipped fast. Money moves; nobody keeps it.
