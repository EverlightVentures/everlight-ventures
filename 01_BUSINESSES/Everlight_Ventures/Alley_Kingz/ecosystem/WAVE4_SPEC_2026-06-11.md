# WAVE 4 SPEC -- operator live-test feedback, 2026-06-11 night

## 1. Skill points ON the card (UI relocation + attribute expansion)
- Kill the bottom-of-page dropdown picker. The per-card tuning lives on the CARD:
  tap a card in the collection -> detail view -> plus/minus controls per attribute.
- Expand tunable attributes beyond HP/DMG: Defense (damage taken mult), Special
  Defense (spell/ability damage taken), Agility/Speed (move speed), Attack Speed.
  Engine: snapshotPerks/card overlay gains the new keys, each clamped (~1.0-1.25)
  so the power budget holds. TUNE_CAP rises modestly (e.g. 8 pts across 6 attrs).
- Upgrade button + copies X/Y on the same card detail (already partly built).

## 2. Melee stacking (separation v2)
- Separation works at approach but CLOSE-RANGE units overlap once attacking.
  Apply the separation pass to units in combat state too (attackers keep their
  full collision radius; allies fan around the target ring instead of stacking).
  Target ring: melee attackers distribute on slots around the victim
  (Clash-style surround), max N per ring, overflow waits a half-tile back.
- Acceptance: pump 8 melee units on one tower/unit -> every token visibly whole.

## 3. Air vs ground elevation language
- AIR units: drawn ~0.5 tile UP from their logical position with a soft drop
  shadow at the true position, gentle hover bob, slightly smaller shadow when
  "higher". Ground units: shadow tight under the token.
- Air attacking ground: projectile/strike angles DOWN to the shadow point;
  ground attacking air: angles UP (longer beam/arc). Air units NEVER share the
  exact draw position with a ground unit (the elevation offset + separation
  handles it).
- Acceptance: a mixed air/ground brawl reads at a glance: who flies, who walks,
  who is shooting up vs down.
