## Vetting Criteria (For Every Professional)

```
[ ] Licensed in their field
[ ] No disciplinary history (state bar / state board check)
[ ] References from clients in similar profile
[ ] Initial consultation went well (clear, candid, asks good questions)
[ ] Transparent fee structure
[ ] Comfortable saying "I don't know, let me research" when appropriate
[ ] Will write things down (not just verbal advice)
[ ] Annual review cadence agreed
```

**Red flags:**
- Pushes a specific product (especially insurance commissions)
- Won't put advice in writing
- Promises "100% audit-proof" or "guaranteed" results
- Vague about fees or hours
- Has no other clients in your profile
- Disciplinary history
- Stale credentials (CPA license expired, etc.)

---

## Annual Review Cadence

Every January:
- [ ] Each professional's services appropriate for current tier?
- [ ] Fee structure still competitive?
- [ ] Annual deliverables actually delivered?
- [ ] Adequate response time when questions arise?
- [ ] Proactive about new opportunities or risks?
- [ ] Replacement decision made (if any)?

Bad professionals are expensive even when they're cheap. Replace promptly.
