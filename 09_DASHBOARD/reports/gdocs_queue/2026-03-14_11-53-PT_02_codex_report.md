### Assessment
You need a production-grade **Daily Drop Engine** that guarantees at least 1 publish/day from highest-rated items/sellers, then pushes that selection to `everlightventures.io` and `him-gear-drop.lovable.app` automatically. The correct path is a hybrid ranking pipeline with hard business constraints, a scheduler with fallback queue, and conversion instrumentation from day one.

### Consolidated Findings
- Build a single autonomous pipeline that guarantees one publish per day with retries and fallback stock.
- Use hybrid ranking plus hard business gates (rating, inventory, margin, seller reliability).
- Publish once, distribute everywhere (site, email, social) via one canonical “daily drop” payload.
- Instrument every step (selection, publish, click, conversion, margin) for rapid weight tuning.
- Treat seller sourcing, matching, and deal ops as part of system reliability, not an afterthought.
