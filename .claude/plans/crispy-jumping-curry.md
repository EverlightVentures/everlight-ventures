# Fire Team Doctrine -- Hive Mind Military Restructure

## Context
The 42-agent Hive Mind is flat -- department heads manage 8-13 people directly with no sub-structure. Inspired by military fire teams (4-person tactical units), the buddy system (mutual verification), and Meta's parallel agent architecture, we're restructuring into a proper hierarchy: Platoon > Squad > Fire Team > Role. This adds delegation depth, redundancy via buddy pairs, and expands the roster from 42 to 63 agents to fill proper fire teams + assistants. Also staffs the new AI Consulting pipeline with its own fire team.

## Fire Team Roles (4 per team + 1 assistant)
- **TL** (Team Leader): Routes sub-tasks, makes calls, reports to Squad Leader
- **S1** (Specialist 1): Primary executor
- **S2** (Specialist 2): Complementary executor
- **B** (Buddy/Verifier): Verifies S1/S2 output, takes over on failure
- **A** (Assistant): Junior -- handles prep, drafting, CRM, formatting

## Failover Rules
- **Agent down**: Buddy takes over immediately
- **TL down**: Verifier (B) promotes to acting TL
- **Squad Leader down**: Senior TL (Alpha team) promotes to acting SL
- **Platoon Leader down**: Major Dex assumes command
