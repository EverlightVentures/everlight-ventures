## Why this language vs the standard pre-call email

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Hi Marco," not "Hi Mr. Williams" | Memphis 38106 cultural register -- formal feels distancing |
| "down the street" callback | Establishes we did our homework but in a neighborly not creepy way |
| "$10,000-$20,000 net" range | Per Henry's psa_prefill: $40k appraisal × 40% = $16k offer minus ~$5-6k closing costs = ~$10-15k net. Conservative estimate keeps us truthful. |
| "no inspection required" | Repeats Marquise's actual stance, builds trust |
| "tax handled at the title firm, you don't write a check" | Honest tax-math version (per `02_OFFER_MATH_AND_BACK_TAX.md`) |
| "or just leave a note in your mailbox" | Gives him a passive-acceptance channel beyond email/phone |
| "If this isn't the right time, no worries. Just toss this letter." | Real CarMax framing -- low-pressure, leaves dignity intact |
| Handwritten signature | Personal letter, not a form letter |

## Print + delivery instructions

1. Print on Everlight Ventures gold-accent letterhead (use the existing brand template).
2. Sign by hand with blue or black pen (NOT printed signature -- the hand-sign is the entire credibility move).
3. Fold once, business envelope.
4. Address envelope to "Marco Williams · 1241 Dunnavant St · Memphis TN 38106" -- no fancy company name on front, just looks like personal mail.
5. Drop in mailbox at 1241 Dunnavant. Don't ring the bell -- the no-pressure framing depends on it being received passively.
6. Log delivery time + date in the lead record (`leads_db.json` parcel `026056  00056`).

## What happens after

Best case: Marco texts or emails within 24-48 hrs. Hammer takes the call.
Likely case: 5-7 days, maybe a callback.
No-response case: drop second letter Day 14 with "still here, no pressure" message.
Hard no: respect it, lead drops to dormant 90-day queue.
