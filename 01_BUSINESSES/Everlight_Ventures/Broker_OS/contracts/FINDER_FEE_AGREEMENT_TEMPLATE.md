## 2. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**2.1 "Introduction"** means the initial act of connecting, referring, or making known the Buyer to the Seller (or vice versa) by Finder, whether by email, telephone, in-person meeting, or other communication method, where no prior business relationship existed between Buyer and Seller.

**2.2 "Qualified Lead"** means a prospective Buyer who (a) has been introduced by Finder, (b) has expressed genuine interest in Seller's products or services, and (c) has the apparent financial capacity to enter into a transaction.

**2.3 "Commission Event"** means the earlier of: (a) the closing of a transaction between Buyer and Seller that was the direct result of an Introduction made by Finder; or (b) the execution of a binding agreement between Buyer and Seller for the purchase of goods or services, where such agreement was the direct result of an Introduction made by Finder.

**2.4 "Deal Value"** means the total gross value of the transaction between Buyer and Seller, including all fees, charges, and consideration exchanged, but excluding applicable sales tax, shipping costs, and government-imposed fees.

**2.5 "Tail Period"** means the {{TAIL_PERIOD_MONTHS}}-month period following the later of: (a) the date of the last Introduction made by Finder; or (b) the effective date of termination of this Agreement.

## 3. SCOPE OF FINDER SERVICES

**3.1 Limited to Introductions.** Finder's services under this Agreement are strictly limited to identifying potential business matches and making Introductions between Buyer and Seller. Finder shall have no obligation or authority to:

- (a) Negotiate any terms of any transaction between Buyer and Seller;
- (b) Advise either party on the merits, risks, or terms of any transaction;
- (c) Represent either party in any capacity whatsoever;
- (d) Participate in the closing of any transaction;
- (e) Provide legal, financial, tax, or investment advice;
- (f) Make any representations or warranties on behalf of either party.

**3.2 Not a Broker, Agent, or Advisor.** Finder is NOT acting as a real estate broker, securities broker, insurance broker, investment advisor, business broker, or any other licensed professional. Finder's role is solely that of a finder -- one who brings parties together for their own negotiations. This Agreement does not create any agency, brokerage, or advisory relationship.

**3.3 No Due Diligence Obligation.** Finder has no obligation to perform due diligence on either party, verify the accuracy of any information provided by either party, or investigate the financial condition, reputation, or legal standing of either party.

## 4. COMMISSION TERMS

**4.1 Commission Rate.** Upon the occurrence of a Commission Event, Seller shall pay Finder a commission equal to {{COMMISSION_PERCENTAGE}}% of the Deal Value (the "Commission").

**4.2 Payment Trigger.** The Commission becomes due and payable upon the occurrence of a Commission Event as defined in Section 2.3.

**4.3 Payment Timeline.** The Commission shall be paid within thirty (30) calendar days of the Commission Event (the "Payment Due Date").

**4.4 Late Payment.** Any Commission not paid by the Payment Due Date shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month (or the maximum rate permitted by California law, whichever is less) from the Payment Due Date until paid in full.

**4.5 Commission on Subsequent Transactions.** If Buyer and Seller enter into additional transactions within the Tail Period that arise from or are related to the original Introduction, Finder shall be entitled to a Commission on each such subsequent transaction at the same rate specified in Section 4.1.

---

## 5. PAYMENT METHOD

**5.1 Primary Method -- Stripe.** Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all Commission payments shall be made via Stripe invoice. Finder will issue a Stripe invoice to Seller within five (5) business days of the Commission Event. Seller shall pay the invoice within the timeline specified in Section 4.3.

**5.2 Secondary Method -- Wire Transfer.** Upon written request by Seller and acceptance by Finder, Commission payments may be made via wire transfer. Finder will provide wire transfer instructions upon confirmation of a Commission Event. All wire transfer fees shall be borne by Seller.

**5.3 Exceptional Method -- Cryptocurrency.** Cryptocurrency payment is available ONLY under the following conditions:

- (a) The Deal Value exceeds $5,000;
- (b) Stripe payment is not feasible due to documented technical or jurisdictional limitations;
- (c) Finder has provided prior written approval for cryptocurrency payment;
- (d) The USD-equivalent amount is calculated at the time of transfer using a reputable price oracle (e.g., CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap);
- (e) Transfer must be completed within a 24-hour settlement window from the agreed conversion rate;
- (f) Both parties comply with the California Digital Financial Assets Law (DFAL) and all applicable federal and state regulations;
- (g) A separate Cryptocurrency Payment Addendum (attached hereto as Exhibit A, if applicable) must be executed by both parties;
- (h) Finder reserves the unilateral right to reject cryptocurrency payment and require payment via Stripe at any time, for any reason.

**5.4 Currency.** All amounts referenced in this Agreement are in United States Dollars (USD).

## 13. NON-CIRCUMVENTION AND NON-SOLICITATION

**13.1 Non-Circumvention.** Seller agrees that during the term of this Agreement and for the duration of the Tail Period, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, attempt to circumvent, avoid, or bypass Finder in order to avoid the obligation to pay Commissions hereunder. Specifically, Seller shall not:

- (a) Contact, negotiate with, or transact with any Buyer introduced by Finder except through Finder, unless Finder has provided prior written consent;
- (b) Use the Introduction to identify or reach affiliates, subsidiaries, partners, or related entities of the Buyer for the purpose of entering into transactions that would otherwise trigger a Commission obligation;
- (c) Engage any third party to contact or transact with the Buyer on Seller's behalf in a manner designed to avoid Commission obligations under this Agreement;
- (d) Redirect, reroute, or restructure any transaction with the Buyer in a manner intended to reduce the Deal Value or otherwise diminish the Commission owed to Finder.

**13.2 Non-Solicitation of Finder's Network.** Seller agrees that during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following its termination, Seller shall not directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or engage any other lead, contact, or prospective buyer identified through Finder's network, pipeline, or platform for the purpose of entering into a transaction without Finder's involvement, unless such relationship existed independently prior to the Introduction.

**13.3 Buyer Acknowledgment.** By signing this Agreement, Buyer acknowledges the non-circumvention provisions herein and agrees not to assist Seller in circumventing Finder's Commission rights. Buyer further agrees not to introduce Seller to any third parties for the purpose of enabling Seller to avoid Commission obligations.

**13.4 Remedies for Circumvention.** In the event of a breach of this Section 13, Finder shall be entitled to: (a) the full Commission that would have been payable had the transaction been properly reported; (b) a penalty equal to 50% of the Commission as liquidated damages (not a penalty); and (c) recovery of all costs and reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in enforcing this provision. The parties agree that the harm from circumvention is difficult to quantify and that this liquidated damages provision is a reasonable estimate of such harm.
