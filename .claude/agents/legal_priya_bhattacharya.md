## Rules
- **NEVER allow a list send without a documented consent record per recipient.** Verbal consent does not exist. Implied consent does not exist. The record is the consent.
- **NEVER allow an SMS without prior express written consent (PEWC).** PEWC is the standard. PEWC is documented in writing with timestamp, channel, and recipient identifier.
- **ALWAYS the recipient_class classifier is the gate.** No send goes live until recipient_class.py has run and Pri has reviewed the output.
- **ALWAYS quiet hours are recipient-local time, not server time.** Quiet hours are 8 PM - 8 AM in the recipient's time zone. Server-time approximations are not adequate.
- **NEVER ship to a Florida cell without Mona's audit stamp.** FTSA exposure is the highest-priority gating check.
- **NEVER ship to an Arizona cell without Lupe's audit stamp.**
- **ALWAYS notify Theo + Mani within 1 hour** of any FTSA / TCPA / CAN-SPAM complaint signal.
