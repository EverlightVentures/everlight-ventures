## OHIO -- Cleveland metro

Ohio is a title-state. Title companies handle the full closing without a mandatory attorney, which is the wholesaler-preferred environment. Average title insurance: roughly 0.5% to 1.0% of purchase price (Bankrate, Houzeo, 2025). On a $150k assignment-target deal, expect $750 to $1,500 in title premium plus $300 to $600 in escrow/settlement fees.

The wholesaler-friendly play is to find a closing-attorney firm that has a published investor practice and does not balk at assignment fees on the settlement statement. Expect $750 to $1,250 attorney closing fee per JVM Lending 2025.

## How to Engage -- 5-Step Outreach Sequence (run in week 1)

Goal: onboard 1 to 2 of the top picks (one per active state) within 14 days. Standard "test-deal" approach. Hammer leads phone, Piper leads email. Use branded_mailer with budget_category=nurture for emails and branded_calendar for the followup intro call.

**Step 1 -- Day 1, phone (Hammer).** Call the top pick in each active state during business hours. Script: "Hi, this is [name] with Everlight Ventures. We run an assignment-of-contract wholesale operation in [city]. I am vetting title partners. Three quick questions: do you hold EMD in escrow on contracts where the buyer is assigned, do you do double closings if the seller objects to seeing the assignment fee, and what is your typical closing turnaround on a clean cash file." Take notes, do not commit. Calls go to: Ohio Real Title 216-373-9900, Katz Durell 404-487-0040, 1st Option Title 972-271-1700.

**Step 2 -- Day 1, email (Piper, same day as call).** Send a 5-line followup via branded_mailer (budget_category=nurture) thanking them for the call, summarizing what they said, and requesting in writing: (a) sample HUD-1 / CD from a recent assignment closing with sensitive fields redacted, (b) their written assignment-of-contract policy, (c) their EMD holding terms and bank, (d) a fee sheet for cash purchases under $200k. Subject line: "Everlight Ventures wholesale -- followup from today's call."

**Step 3 -- Day 3-5, evaluate response.** Score each firm on: response speed (under 24h = green), willingness to put assignment policy in writing (yes = green), HUD-1 sample provided (yes = green), EMD held in firm trust account at named bank (yes = green). Two greens minimum to advance.

**Step 4 -- Day 5-10, test deal.** Route the next live contract through the top-scoring firm in each state as a test. Open escrow with $1,000 EMD. Watch for: file opened within 1 business day, title commitment within 5 business days, clear communication on assignment-fee handling at the table. If the test deal closes clean within 14 days, that firm is the primary going forward.

**Step 5 -- Day 14, decision and document.** Pick one primary and one backup per active state. Add their contacts to the wholesale CRM (broker_ops or wholesale workbook), tag with state and metro. Save HUD-1 sample and written policy to `/mnt/sdcard/AA_MY_DRIVE/01_BUSINESSES/Everlight_Ventures/Wholesale/title_companies/<state>/<firm_name>/`. Post a one-line summary to #ft-hunters or #broker-pipeline naming the chosen firm per state.

## Risks and Uncertainties

- Several phone numbers and most emails are unverified; flagged in the file as "unverified, call to confirm." Do not paste any unverified contact into outbound automation without a live call first.
- Old School Title (OH #3) has thin web presence; may be defunct or rebranded. Recommend the OH list runs first via cold call so any gap is caught early, with Harvard Title or Erie Title as fallback.
- Morris Hardwick Schneider was deliberately excluded due to the DOJ embezzlement matter against its former managing partner / CFO (Land Castle Title escrow funds).
- Georgia attorney-state requirement means GA closings will always cost more (~$750-$1,250 attorney fee) than OH or TX. This is statutory, not negotiable.
- Texas title insurance rates are TDI-promulgated and identical across firms, so price-shopping the premium is wasted effort. Speed and assignment policy are the differentiators.
