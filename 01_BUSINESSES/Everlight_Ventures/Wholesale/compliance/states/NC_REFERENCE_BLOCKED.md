## Why NC is blocked

NC HB 797 (S.L. 2025-6 equivalent) amended GS Chapter 93A as of October 1, 2025. **Residential property wholesaling is now brokerage activity requiring an NC broker license.** This is the single most operator-impactful 2025 wholesale law in the country.

**Penalty for operating without license:**
- Class 1 misdemeanor (§93A-8)
- UDAP (Unfair and Deceptive Acts and Practices, GS §75-1.1) — **treble damages + attorney fees**
- 30-day non-waivable seller right of cancellation
- Ohio REC enforcement

**This is not a state where we operate without a license.** Period.

## What HB 797 does

1. **Wholesaling residential property = real estate brokerage** under GS Chapter 93A — requires license.
2. **Wholesaler must provide full copy of contract at signing.**
3. **30-day non-waivable rescission right** for the homeowner.
4. **Refund within 10 business days** if seller cancels.
5. **No threshold** — applies to any residential wholesaling activity, not just "engaged in business."

## Pre-HB 797 baseline (historical context only)

Before Oct 1, 2025: pure assignment of contract by a bona fide buyer was permitted per NCREC bulletins. NCREC always emphasized that you cannot market the property itself without a license, but assigning a contract you actually intended to close on was OK.

HB 797 collapses that distinction.

## Tax economics (academic — we don't operate here)

- Personal income tax: 4.25% (2025), **3.99% (2026)**.
- Capital gains: taxed as ordinary income.
- Real estate transfer tax (excise): $1 per $500 (~0.20%); 7 coastal counties have additional local transfer tax up to 1%.

## If we ever want to operate in NC

1. Marquise enrolls in 75-hour NC broker pre-license course.
2. Pass NC state license exam.
3. Affiliate with a sponsoring NC broker (or form own brokerage).
4. Pay $100 application + ~$45 renewal.
5. Operate as licensed broker — wholesaling is then permitted under GS Chapter 93A.

**ROI calculus:** if NC volume justifies the time + license + brokerage affiliation, do it. Otherwise stay blocked. Currently: zero NC pipeline, no anchor buyer, no immediate revenue case. **STAY BLOCKED.**
