# THE LOT -- `HOME_TURF`
## 🏙️ Central Plaza (neutral) · center (1,1) · WOOD district · TAX-FREE

**Canon:** spawn / home block. Ground `uptown`. Tint `10,14,24`. Ambient gold `#e8c55a`.
Act I is "BORN IN THE DIRT." City boss is **THE LOT WARDEN**, a scarred old Bullmastiff
who taxes every stray in the yard. His line: *"Born in this dirt. Don't you die in it."*
Also hosts the **BLOCK WAR** defense loop (4 defense posts).

**Must not contradict:** THE LOT is the ONLY tax-free district. It is neutral -- no
faction banners, no crew colors on the walls. That neutrality is the whole point:
it is the one place all four crews can stand without it being a war.
