# Stress Tests Engine
## What If The World Changes

**Strategy that survives only one future is fragile. This engine pre-tests OS resilience against major plausible scenarios. When (not if) one materializes, you have a pre-built response.**

---

## Macro Scenarios

### Scenario A: TCJA Estate Exemption Sunsets in Full ($13.99M -> $7M)
**Probability:** Default outcome unless legislation acts. ~40-50% probability of full sunset.

**Impact:**
- Anyone with $7M+ has new estate tax exposure
- Pre-funded SLAT/Dynasty Trust holders are PROTECTED
- Late movers face emergency planning crunch

**Pre-positioned response:**
- If wealth trajectory crosses $7M before late 2025: deploy SLAT (married) or use exemption via Dynasty Trust funding
- Quarterly Intel monitoring for legislative developments
- Backup plan: rapid deployment of GRAT/IDGT structures

### Scenario B: TCJA Income Tax Rates Sunset (37% -> 39.6% top)
**Probability:** ~50-60% (less politically protected than estate exemption)

**Impact:**
- Top marginal rate rises 2.6 percentage points
- 199A QBI deduction (20%) eliminated
- Standard deduction halves
- SALT cap removed (good for high-tax states)

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Accelerate income into 2025 if rates rise
- Defer deductions to 2026 if rates rise
- Reverse if rates extended (defer income, accelerate deductions)

### Scenario C: Step-Up Basis Eliminated
**Probability:** Has been proposed multiple times but never passed. ~15% probability medium-term.

**Impact:** Borrow-Buy-Die strategy weakened. Heirs would inherit donor's basis, owe cap gains on full appreciation.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Watch closely. If law passes, adjust strategy to:
  - Lifetime gifting via SLAT/Dynasty Trust BEFORE elimination effective date (still get basis carryover, but at lower current FMV)
  - Charitable structures (CRT) if charitable intent exists
  - 1031 / Opportunity Zone instead of "hold forever"

### Scenario D: Wealth Tax Imposed (1-3% annual on >$50M)
**Probability:** ~10-20% medium-term, rising in some political scenarios.

**Impact:** Material drag on wealth above threshold. Multi-jurisdictional restructuring may become important.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- T9+ international diversification becomes more important
- Asset valuation becomes critical (illiquid assets harder to value, lower wealth tax exposure)
- Trust structures designed for wealth tax avoidance (jurisdictional and timing)

### Scenario E: Major Recession (2008-style, S&P -40%, Real Estate -25%)
**Probability:** Cyclical, ~20% in any 5-year window.

**Impact:** Asset values crash. Margin calls possible. Forced sales catastrophic.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Conservative LTV on all borrowing (<25-35%)
- 12-18 months Layer 1+2 cash reserves
- Diversified collateral (don't pledge one asset class to multiple lenders)
- Cash on the sidelines for OPPORTUNISTIC PURCHASES (this is when wealth is built)
- Roth conversion at depressed asset values (convert at low value, growth tax-free)

### Scenario F: Hyperinflation (5%+ sustained)
**Probability:** ~25-30% over decade timeframes.

**Impact:** Cash and bonds destroyed. Real assets (RE, equities, commodities) preserve. Fixed-rate debt becomes valuable (paid back in cheaper dollars).

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Real estate ownership (paid for with depreciating dollars)
- Fixed-rate mortgages on properties (NOT variable)
- Equities (long-term beat inflation)
- Some commodity exposure (gold, silver)
- TIPS for inflation-adjusted bond exposure
- Avoid long-duration cash holdings

### Scenario G: Currency Crisis / USD Loses Reserve Status
**Probability:** ~5-10% over 20-year timeframe. Higher tail risk.

**Impact:** USD purchasing power could decline 50%+. Foreign asset holdings become critical.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- T9+ international diversification across non-USD assets
- Foreign real estate
- Multi-currency bank accounts
- Some Bitcoin / hard money exposure
- Foreign trust structures already in place (Cook Islands, Nevis)

## Personal Scenarios

### Scenario H: You Become Disabled / Unable to Work
**Probability:** Statistical lifetime risk ~25%.

**Impact:** Loss of active income. Long-term care costs.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Long-term disability insurance (private market, $100-500/mo at younger ages)
- Long-term care insurance (cheap when young, expensive when old)
- Passive income streams (real estate rentals, dividends, royalties)
- Spouse / family / co-trustee succession plans

### Scenario I: You Get Sued for $5M+
**Probability:** Varies by industry, profile. Real estate + content + AI services: medium.

**Impact:** Personal exposure if insufficient asset protection. DAPT/Holdco/insurance critical.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Decision Tree #4 (Lawsuit Filed)
- Pre-existing umbrella policy ($5-10M)
- Professional / cyber / E&O policies
- DAPT seasoning complete
- Holdco structure mature
- Cook Islands trust at T9+ if profile high

### Scenario J: Divorce
**Probability:** ~40% lifetime US divorce rate. Lower for those who reach $1M+ wealth (selection bias) but still material.

**Impact:** Property division, ongoing alimony, asset valuation disputes.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Prenuptial agreement BEFORE marriage (or postnuptial after, if state allows)
- Premarital assets clearly documented and segregated
- DAPT funding pre-marriage (state law varies on whether marital assets reach the trust)
- Family Office governance documents addressing divorce scenarios
- Engage family law attorney specializing in HNW divorce IMMEDIATELY if dispute arises

### Scenario K: IRS Audit
**Probability:** Statistical; varies by income and aggressive positions.

**Impact:** Time, fees, possible adjustments and penalties.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Audit Defense Engine (this OS)
- Form 8275 disclosure for aggressive positions (drops 20% accuracy penalty)
- Engage enrolled agent or tax attorney upon notice
- Documentation in audit-ready format from contemporaneous records

### Scenario L: Death of You (Unexpected)
**Probability:** Statistical; risk increases with age.

**Impact:** Family must execute estate plan, business succession, ongoing operations.

**Pre-positioned response:**
- Comprehensive estate plan (RLT, ILIT, beneficiary designations)
- Business succession plan (who runs Operating LLC if you're gone)
- Letter of intent / digital estate plan (passwords, crypto keys, accounts list)
- Life insurance sized appropriately (especially in ILIT)
- Family meeting cadence ensures family knows the plan

## Annual Stress Test Procedure

Every January:
1. Run each scenario above against current OS state
2. Identify gaps in preparation
3. Schedule 1-2 fixes per quarter
4. Document changes to OS files
5. Refresh insurance coverage / structure / professional engagements as needed

## Quantitative Stress Tests

### Portfolio Stress Tests
- 50% equity drawdown -- Layer 1+2 covers 12+ months expenses?
- 30% real estate drawdown -- can service all mortgage debt?
- 200 bps rate rise -- variable-rate debt service still affordable?
- 50% income reduction -- runway from Layer 1+2 is 12+ months?

### Liquidity Stress Tests
- All credit lines frozen for 18 months -- can survive on cash + liquid investments?
- Forced sale of one asset class -- which? When?
- Margin call from broker -- can meet without forced sale of pledge?

### Tax Stress Tests
- Federal rates +5pp permanent -- what's the effective rate impact?
- State residency challenged -- can defend the domicile change?
- All aggressive positions audited simultaneously -- which fail? Why?

If ANY stress test fails, fix it BEFORE the scenario materializes.

## Black Swan Reserves

A small allocation (1-5% of net worth) to "black swan" hedges:
- Out-of-the-money put options on broad market indices
- Some BTC holding (anti-fiat hedge)
- Some gold/silver holding (anti-inflation hedge)
- Pre-positioned offshore relationships

These are insurance, not investments. Expected to lose money in normal times. Pay enormously in tail events.
