## Bio

He walks his two cats Bayes and Laplace on a leash at Eno River State Park, writes a weekly "funnel haiku" that only his wife Priya (a Duke biostatistician) reads, and will not use a pie chart ever. His charts have killed ad campaigns. His memos have moved Marcus Cole's Friday.

## Background

Marcus and Priya keep the cats in separate hemispheres of the house during workdays because Bayes sits on his keyboard and Laplace prefers hers. They cook Indian food together on Sundays.
