# T3 -- S-Corp + R&D Credit ($100k Net Worth / $80k+ Profit)
## The Tax Optimization Tier

**Trigger:** Operating LLC profit projected at $80k+/yr (S-corp election math becomes positive).

---

## The 5 T3 Moves

### 1. S-Corp Election (Form 2553)

**The savings:** Self-Employment tax = 15.3% on first $168,600 (2024). As S-corp:
- Pay yourself a "reasonable salary" (FICA on that)
- Distribute the rest as S-corp distributions (no FICA)

**The math:** $200k profit, $80k reasonable salary, $120k distributions.
- Sole prop: ~$25k SE tax
- S-corp: $80k × 15.3% = $12.2k FICA on salary, $0 on $120k distributions
- **Savings: ~$13k/yr**

**Reasonable salary defense:**
- Get a written comp study (RCReports, $400-1,000)
- Document your role responsibilities (CEO, sales, ops)
- Use BLS data + industry comp surveys
- Aim for low end of reasonable range, not lowest possible

**Mechanics:**
- File Form 2553 within 75 days of tax year start (or up to 3 yrs late with reasonable cause)
- Set up payroll (Gusto $40/mo, Justworks, OnPay) -- handle federal/state/local withholding, W-2 prep, quarterly 941s
- Run reasonable salary on regular payroll cadence
- Take distributions as needed (with basis tracking)

### 2. R&D Credit Annual Filing

Now an annual move (T1 was retroactive cleanup, T3+ is ongoing):
- Document QRE quarterly with project/contractor/software detail
- Engage R&D specialist annually (or in-house with CPA collaboration)
- File Form 6765 with annual return
- Continue payroll-tax-offset election if eligible (startup <5 yrs, <$5M revenue)

**Annual value:** $5-50k depending on R&D spend.

### 3. Solo 401(k) Maximize

With S-corp salary, Solo 401(k) supercharges:
- Employee deferral: $23,000 (2024), $30,500 (50+)
- Employer match: 25% of W-2 salary
- $80k W-2 salary: $20k employer match available
- Combined: ~$43k tax-deductible into Solo 401(k)/yr

**Roth split decision:** Some employee deferral can be Roth if Solo 401(k) provider supports. Roth grows tax-free forever. Trade lower current deduction for higher long-term tax savings.

### 4. First Real Estate Consideration

If real estate fits your profile (per PRIORITIES.md weights):

**Primary residence:**
- Already owned: refinance if rates favorable, HELOC for liquidity
- Buying: VA loan if eligible, FHA, conventional 20%+ down
- Strategy: live-in flip (tax-free up to $250k single / $500k married per Sec 121 every 2 years)

**First rental property:**
- Hold in dedicated LLC under Holdco
- Cost segregation study within 6 months of close (5-15x ROI)
- Track all expenses meticulously
- File Schedule E with personal return; LLC is disregarded if single-member

**Short-term rental loophole consideration:**
- If avg stay <7 days AND material participation: depreciation against ACTIVE income (W-2, S-corp salary)
- Massive tax shelter potential ($30-100k of paper losses against active income in year 1 of cost-seg)

### 5. Cash-Value Whole Life Foundation (Long Game)

Open a participating whole life policy with a top mutual company:
- Penn Mutual, MassMutual, Northwestern Mutual, Guardian, NY Life
- Structure for CASH VALUE, not death benefit (paid-up additions, low base, blended)
- Use a fee-only or specialty advisor (NOT captive agent)
- Premium: $5-25k/yr realistic for T3
- 10-year breakeven on cash value typical
- 20+ year compounding becomes the borrowing source for L6 strategies

**Why now and not later:** Insurance underwriting depends on age and health. Younger = cheaper. Healthier = cheaper. Lock in rates early.
