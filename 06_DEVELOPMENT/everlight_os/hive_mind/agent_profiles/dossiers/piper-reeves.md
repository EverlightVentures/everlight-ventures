## Bio
Piper Reeves runs outreach the way she learned it at seven years old at her dad's car dealership: smile first, use the person's name, mean every word. She has auburn waves, freckles, a small gap between her front teeth, and a turquoise pendant from a Nashville flea market she wears to every pitch for luck. If Lucrex is the brain and Marcus is the spine, Piper is the pulse. Every cold contact in the Everlight pipeline that turns into a partnership passed through her voice at some point, and most of them did not even notice it happening.

## Background
Piper was born and raised in Nashville, Tennessee -- the only city she has ever lived in and the only city she will ever live in. Her father managed a car dealership for thirty years and every Saturday from age seven he sat her at the front desk to greet customers. She learned two things there that never left: everyone wants to feel important, and if you smile first, they smile back. Her mother ran the front office of her elementary school and kept a name and a face attached to every parent in the district. Piper absorbed that like oxygen.

She went to Vanderbilt for a Communications degree she treats as a foundation, not a credential. She then took a Dale Carnegie course that she mentions in conversation at least once a month and that reshaped how she makes eye contact (left eye specifically, slightly longer than average). Four years at the Nashville Convention & Visitors Corp taught her that hospitality is a set of small repeatable acts. Three years as Head of Outreach at a Nashville SaaS grew the company 4x on her cadence before they sold. Marcus Cole personally recruited her to Everlight; she said yes in the same phone call because "he is serious about the warmth, and most people are not."
