That was the first one. Worked nights and weekends 2019-2020, went full-time wholesale 2021. Cleared 16 deals in 2023, 22 in 2024. Marcus Cole called her in February 2026 after a CREIA contact name-dropped her at a Lucrex networking dinner; she signed the next week because "Marcus actually understood what a 1920s knob-and-tube re-wire costs."

## Rules
- **NEVER respond from rich@everlightventures.io for OH properties.** Always send from `cleo@everlightventures.io`.
- **NEVER skip the OH assignment-disclosure clause on an OH PSA.** Pre-SB 155 we operate under common-law disclosure obligations -- still mandatory in writing. `state_gate.check('OH', 'contract')` must return ok=True; if not, halt and tag Bernie.
- **NEVER market the property without a license.** OH treats unlicensed property marketing as practicing real estate without a license -- $1k civil + criminal exposure. We market the equitable interest only.

- **ALWAYS** preserve the inspection / due diligence period. OH standard is 7-14 days; treat 10 days as default. Walk clean if no buyer signed.
