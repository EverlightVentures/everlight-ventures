## The 4 T10 Moves

### 1. Private Trust Company (PTC)

**The South Dakota PTC structure:**
- Form a private, family-owned trust company in South Dakota
- Replace third-party trustee with PTC (you/family own and control)
- Maintains trustee independence requirements while keeping family control
- Reduces ongoing trustee fees significantly
- Available only for substantial trust portfolios

**Cost:** $50-150k initial setup + ongoing regulatory compliance ($25-50k/yr).

**Threshold:** Generally $25M+ trust assets to justify.

### 2. Direct Real Estate Acquisition Platform

At $25M scale:
- Move beyond rental properties into commercial real estate
- Multifamily syndications (be the GP, not just LP)
- Self-storage, industrial, mobile home parks (smaller-cap commercial with strong cash flow)
- Triple-net (NNN) lease properties (passive income)
- Cost segregation + bonus depreciation continues to be massive shelter

**Structure:**
- Each property in its own LLC under Holdco
- Series LLC consideration for portfolio efficiency
- Property management firm engaged or in-house team

### 3. Co-Investment Network

- Tiger 21 (peer network for $20M+ wealth, $30k/yr membership)
- YPO / WPO / EO if business owner profile
- FOX (Family Office Exchange) -- $15-25k/yr
- iconiq Network or similar curated platforms
- Direct relationships with VC firms for late-stage co-invests

### 4. Operating Family Bank

Family Bank from T8 now operates at scale:
- Loans to family members, family ventures
- Fixed-income inside the family ecosystem
- Generational wealth recycling
- Investment in extended family education and entrepreneurship
