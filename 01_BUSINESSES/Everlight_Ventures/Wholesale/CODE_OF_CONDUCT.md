# Everlight Ventures Wholesale -- Code of Conduct

_Every agent in the Hive, every VA, every team member acknowledges this annually. Violations end engagements immediately._

## What we promise sellers

1. *Honest pricing.* Cash offers are based on real comps and real repair estimates. We will not lowball with the intent to renegotiate later.
2. *Required disclosure.* Every contract states clearly that we may assign the contract to an investor partner before closing. Sellers know what we are doing.
3. *No pressure.* Sellers can say no, no thanks, or remove me at any point and we honor it within one business day. Forever.
4. *No predatory targeting.* We do not target distressed sellers using fear-based tactics. We do not target the elderly with exploitative pricing. We do not approach a property in active probate without the executor present.
5. *Honor every contract.* If we sign a purchase agreement, we close on the agreed terms or honor the cancellation provisions in the contract. We never just walk and leave the seller holding the bag.

## What we promise buyers (cash investors)

1. *Off-market only when promised.* If a deal is presented as off-market, it is not on MLS, not in another wholesaler's queue, not double-listed.
2. *Real numbers.* ARV, rehab estimate, comps, area data are sourced and citable. No invented stats.
3. *Clear assignment.* If we are assigning, the assignment fee is disclosed in the contract. No surprise fees at title.
4. *Timeline honesty.* If we say 14-day close we mean 14 business days, not "we will see what happens."
5. *Buyer-list discipline.* We do not blast every buyer on every deal. Tier-1 buyers get first look on inventory in their criteria.

## What we promise title companies and attorneys

1. *Complete packages.* Every file goes to title with PSA, assignment (if applicable), proof of funds, EMD receipt, contingency removals.
2. *No pressure on dates.* Title sets the closing date when title is clear, not before.
3. *No directing the closing.* We do not pressure title to expedite past required steps (lien clearance, payoff verification, deed prep).
4. *Single source of truth.* Every document we send is the final version, dated, signed, complete. No "I will send the rest later."

## What we promise other wholesalers (JV partners)

1. *Honor splits.* The agreement on JV split is honored on every deal. No re-negotiating after the buyer is sourced.
2. *No poaching their buyers.* If they share a buyer for a JV deal, that buyer relationship belongs to them on future deals.
3. *Fast disposition.* We move on JV contracts within 72 hours of receipt. If we cannot, we say so on day one.

## Internal standards

1. *No deception inside the team.* If a deal is in trouble, the team learns immediately. We do not hide bad news.
2. *Clear authorship.* Every Slack post, email, and report names who wrote it (Marcus, Piper, Hammer, Rich).
3. *Document everything.* Every conversation with a seller, buyer, title, or attorney is logged in the dashboard. The record is the source of truth.
4. *Pay people on time.* VAs, JV partners, contractors, vendors. Net-7 or as written, every time.

## Reporting violations

Anyone (team, VA, JV partner, seller, buyer) can email `conduct@everlightventures.io` to report a violation. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours by Rich. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Acknowledgment

By working with Everlight Ventures, every team member, contractor, VA, and JV partner agrees to operate under this code. Annual re-acknowledgment required for ongoing engagements.
