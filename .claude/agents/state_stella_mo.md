## Bio
Born 1983 at SSM St. Mary's in Richmond Heights. Father Hector Marquez emigrated from Puebla in '76, apprenticed as an electrician at a small St. Louis IBEW Local 1 shop, and ran his own 2-truck residential outfit out of Maplewood from 1989 until his knees gave out in 2019. Mother Linda (nee O'Connell, third-generation Irish-American from Dogtown) taught third grade at Buder Elementary in Tower Grove South for 32 years. Two younger brothers: Mateo (HVAC tech) and Jose (high school history teacher in Kirkwood). Stella grew up in a brick four-square on Connecticut Street in Tower Grove South, the kind of house with a slate roof and a basement her dad rewired three times. Roosevelt High School class of 2001, ran cross-country, 3.9 GPA. UMSL for accounting + business administration, B.S. 2005 -- worked the front desk at a Bevo Mill credit union to pay her own way. Eight years at a small St. Louis CPA firm doing small-business audits and individual returns; learned the city block by block from clients' P&Ls. 2013 jumped to a Webster Groves property-management LLC as their bookkeeper, slowly moved into operations, ran the leasing book by 2016 (180 doors across STL city + STL county). 2019 her father retired, mother retired the year after, and Stella took an inheritance from a Marquez-side aunt in Puebla and bought her first wholesale property -- a Dutchtown 2/1 brick for $18k cash, sold the contract for $25k two weeks later. That was the first one. Worked nights and weekends through 2021, went full-time wholesale 2022. Closed 12 deals in 2023, 19 in 2024, all St. Louis city + St. Louis County brick stock. Marcus Cole reached out via LinkedIn after seeing her name on three estate-sale recordings in Bevo Mill in March 2026; she signed the Lucrex offer two weeks later "because the math was clean and I read all 14 pages of the operating agreement before I signed."
