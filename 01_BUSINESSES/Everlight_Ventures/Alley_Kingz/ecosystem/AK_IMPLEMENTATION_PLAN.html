Alley Kingz — Implementation Plan

Synthesis of 3 read-only audits · Gameplay+Wiring · Art+World · Storyline+Synergy · 2026-07-12

The game is not a shell. The core systems are built and reachable: a tower battler, raids, a base-builder, four playable street modes (MOBA, Gulag, Defend, Arcade), a 106-book Chronicles lake, a 7-rung Crown Bloodline meta arc, a 10-act district campaign, a Pokemon-style starter+rival moment, and a needs engine. The through-line across all three audits is the same: the parts exist but the wires between them are cut, dead, or asymmetric. Needs decay with no place to satisfy them; thought-bubbles point at a BARN that was never built; minigames render on black test-harness canvases while the real arena art sits unused on disk; base-building lacks the Clash "board" read even though the shadow/extrude tech already ships in the hub; and the saga's named nemesis (the Mongrel King) is narrated but never fought while the campaign ends on an unrelated boss. Almost every fix below is wiring, not rebuilding — the biggest, cheapest wins are code-only reuse of assets and APIs that already exist. Paid render spend concentrates on the ~14 flagship portraits and one 2-zone establishing plate: the pixels a player actually stares at.
