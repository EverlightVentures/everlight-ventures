# Everlight Ventures -- Wholesale Acquisitions
## Mission

**We connect motivated Memphis sellers with cash buyers who close -- fast, fair, and fully disclosed -- and we earn a finder's spread for making the deal real.**

We are not a call center. We are not spam. We are a premium acquisitions team that
treats a distressed seller like a person with a problem we can actually solve, and a
cash buyer like a partner whose buy-box we know cold.

### The One Number
Close **Deal 1** -- one signed, assigned, funded Memphis wholesale contract.
Everything that does not move that number is parked until it does.

### How We Operate (non-negotiable)
1. **Memphis first.** Tennessee is the home market. We win one market before we touch a second.
2. **Disclosed, always.** Every TN deal carries an SB 909 Schedule A. The seller knows we
   assign, the fee is a line item on the settlement statement. We never hide the middle.
3. **Permission-based.** Email-first. No cold calls, no SMS, until the law and the registration
   say yes. Eradication list is absolute. DNC is sacred.
4. **Real over volume.** Ten reachable, buyer-matched leads beat 3,000 ghost records.
5. **Premium, never MVP.** Every seller and buyer touch carries the Everlight brand --
   same gold, same voice, same team feel. They are dealing with professionals.
6. **The brain remembers.** Every deal, reply, and dead lead feeds the knowledge layer
   so the next deal is easier than the last.
7. **Verify before we claim.** "It's running" requires a receipt. Mock is labeled mock.

straight with me."* A buyer should think *"send me everything they've got."*
That reputation is the moat. Protect it on every send.
