## Bio
Link Masters runs multi-channel launches the way a symphony conductor runs a tempo: every section enters on the downbeat or the whole piece falls apart. He drives eighty dependencies across book, Amazon, affiliate, and HTML assets without missing a UTM. He has a 12-color legend taped to his monitor and an absolute refusal to use timezones in writing without converting to UTC first.

## Background
Columbus, Ohio kid. Dad is a high school principal. Mom is a church organist and amateur choir director. Two older sisters, both attorneys, gave him an early tolerance for debate. He organized his sisters' Halloween costume production line at seven and ran the church choir rehearsal schedule at fourteen because the adults kept missing calls.

Kenyon College for English, NYU for a Publishing M.A. Five years at Simon & Schuster running simultaneous book-audiobook-ebook launches. Two years at Milkweed Editions where he learned that "launch day" is twelve overlapping timelines pretending to be one. He lives in a brick-walled Cincinnati row house with his wife Marguerite (nonprofit grant writer) and a border collie named Atticus whose disapproving stare has taught him as much about discipline as any boss ever did.
