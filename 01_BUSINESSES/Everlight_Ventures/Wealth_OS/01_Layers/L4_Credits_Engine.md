## The Two Highest-Value Credits For You SPECIFICALLY (T0-T3 Priority)

### 1. R&D Tax Credit (Section 41) -- This Is Money You've Already Earned

**The bombshell:** You spent 2024-2026 building XLM trading bot, Hive Mind orchestration platform, Broker OS, Vantaris casino, Onyx POS. **All of that is qualified research expense (QRE) under Section 41.** Time spent, contractor costs, software/cloud costs, supplies all count.

**The math:**
- Federal credit: 6-14% of QRE (varies by calculation method)
- California credit: 7.5-15% additional (stackable!)
- Total potential: 13.5-29% of qualified spend back as a credit

**Estimated 2024-2026 QRE for Everlight:** $50-150k (your time at reasonable hourly rate + contractor costs + Anthropic API + Coinbase + AWS + tooling + dev tools)
**Estimated credit value:** $7-40k that you can claim retroactively against future tax liability

**Critical mechanics:**
- Credit can be carried back 1 year, forward 20 years
- For startups (gross receipts <$5M and <5 yrs old): up to $500k/yr can offset PAYROLL TAX instead of income tax. This means even if you have no tax liability yet, you can get cash back via reduced payroll deposits.
- File Form 6765 with annual return, OR amend prior years
- Sec 174 capitalization rules (since 2022) require R&D to be amortized over 5 yrs domestic / 15 yrs foreign. This makes R&D MORE expensive to deduct, but the CREDIT is unaffected.

**Action items at T0:**
- Start a "QRE tracking" spreadsheet TODAY: date, project, hours, hourly rate, software/contractor expense, description of technical uncertainty
- Categorize every commit, every API expense, every contractor invoice from January 2024 forward
- At T1 with new entity formed: engage a R&D credit specialist firm (alliantgroup, KBKG, McGuire Sponsel, Source Advisors). They typically work on contingency (15-25% of credit captured)

### 2. QSBS -- Qualified Small Business Stock (Section 1202)

**The opportunity:** If you exit any of your SaaS businesses (Hive Mind, Onyx, Vantaris) for $10M+, the FIRST $10M of gain (or 10x your basis, whichever is greater) is **100% federally tax-free**.

**The strict requirements:**
1. **C-corporation** (NOT LLC, NOT S-corp). Conversion is possible but resets the clock.
2. **Domestic** (US-incorporated)
3. **Gross assets <$50M** at time of stock issuance (and immediately after)
4. **Active business** (not investment company, not pro services like law/accounting/consulting/health -- but software/tech IS qualified)
5. **5-year hold** (stock must be held 5+ years before sale)
6. **Acquired at original issuance** (you got it directly from the company, not on secondary market)

**The math at exit:**
- $20M sale of Hive Mind SaaS C-corp, held 5+ years
- First $10M: 100% federally tax-free (saves ~$3.7M federal cap gains tax)
- Remaining $10M: standard cap gains
- **States that conform to QSBS:** ~half. CA does NOT conform (so you'd still pay CA tax). Yet another reason to consider domicile change before exit.

**Action items at T0-T1:**
- Decide which businesses are exit-track: Hive Mind SaaS, Onyx, Vantaris likely candidates
- Form them as **Delaware C-corps** at T1 (not LLCs, not S-corps)
- Issue stock to yourself at incorporation (this is the moment the 5-year clock starts)
- Document gross assets at issuance (must be <$50M)
- Hold for 5+ years before any sale

**The cost of getting this wrong:** Forming as LLC and converting later resets the QSBS clock. If you convert at year 4 and sell at year 6, you only have 2 years of QSBS treatment. Lose 60% of the benefit. Get this RIGHT at incorporation.

## "Free Money" Moves at T0 (Do These NOW)

### Augusta Rule (Section 280A(g))
**The play:** Once you have an entity (T1+), you can rent your home to your business for up to 14 days/yr. The rent is:
- DEDUCTIBLE to the business (~$500-3,000/day depending on local comp)
- TAX-FREE to you personally (Section 280A(g) exemption)

**The mechanics:**
- Business uses home for: board meetings, planning sessions, training, content recording, photoshoots
- Document with: meeting minutes, agenda, attendees, business purpose
- Rent rate must be "reasonable" (comparable hotel meeting room rate in your area)
- Max 14 days/yr (calendar)

**Annual value:** $5,000-30,000 in shifted income (deductible to business, tax-free to you).

### Hire Your Kids (If Applicable)
**The play:** If you have kids under 18 AND they're doing real work for the business:
- Pay them up to $14,600/yr (2024 standard deduction) -- they pay $0 federal income tax
- No FICA on their wages if business is sole prop OR LLC owned 100% by parent(s)
- Business deducts the full wage
- Use proceeds for: their 529, their Roth IRA (yes, even kids can have Roths if they have earned income), their future expenses

**Annual value:** $5,400+ tax savings per kid in 24% bracket parent ($14,600 × 24% = $3,504, plus reduced SE/FICA).

### Solo 401(k) (At T2-T3)
**The play:** Once you have S-corp salary or self-employed earnings:
- Contribute $23,000 (2024) as employee + 25% of salary as employer = up to $69,000/yr ($76,500 if 50+)
- Deductible to business
- Grows tax-deferred
- Roth Solo 401(k) variant available for tax-free growth
- Loan against it (up to $50k or 50%, whichever less)

**Annual value:** $69k of deductible retirement contributions = ~$25k in tax savings at 37% bracket.

### HSA (If High-Deductible Health Plan)
**The triple tax advantage:**
- Deductible going in
- Tax-free growth
- Tax-free withdrawals for medical (or after 65, anything taxed at ordinary rates like a Traditional IRA)

**2025 limits:** $4,300 individual / $8,550 family.

**The UHNW move:** Pay current medical out of pocket (don't use the HSA). Save receipts. Decades later, withdraw HSA funds tax-free to "reimburse" yourself. The HSA grew tax-free for decades.

## Documentation Requirements (CANNOT SKIP)

Every credit/deduction has documentation requirements. The IRS denies aggressive claims at audit when documentation is sloppy. For each:

- **R&D credit:** Project descriptions, technical uncertainty memo, time tracking by project, contractor invoices, software licenses
- **Augusta Rule:** Meeting minutes, attendee list, business purpose, rent comp study (3 comps), check from business to you, Form 1099 not required
- **Home office:** Floor plan, photos, exclusive-and-regular-use proof, utility bill allocations
- **Mileage:** Date, miles, business purpose, starting/ending point (MileIQ or similar app, automatic)
- **Hire your kids:** Real work product, real time tracking, real W-2, age-appropriate task documentation
- **Cost seg:** Engineering study from qualified firm
- **REPS:** Time logs, calendar entries, project lists -- audit-tested standard

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not tracking R&D contemporaneously.** Reconstructing QRE from memory at year-end captures 30% of what real-time tracking captures. Leave $20k+ on the table.
2. **Missing 5-year QSBS hold by months.** Sell at 4 yr 11 mo, lose entire QSBS exclusion. Pay $3.7M tax that could have been $0.
3. **Treating the Augusta Rule as a slush fund.** No real meeting, no documented business purpose = audit reversal + penalties.
4. **Bonus depreciation on year 1 of new STR purchase.** WORKS IF: avg stay <7 days, material participation. Doesn't work if you treat it like a passive investment.
5. **Ignoring 1031 timing rules.** 45 days to identify replacement, 180 days to close. Miss either, full gain recognized.
6. **Stacking too many credits in same year.** AMT can claw back. Plan multi-year sequencing.
