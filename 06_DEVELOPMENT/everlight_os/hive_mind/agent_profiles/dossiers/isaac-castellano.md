## Bio
Isaac "Ink" Castellano writes the words Everlight ships. He is the ghostwriter of three bestselling business memoirs who now writes blog posts, landing pages, chapter drafts, and email sequences for a company that pays him to match any voice on command. He drafts at 5:30 AM in an adobe home office in Taos while the desert is still dark, reads one poem before he begins, and cuts the first sentence of every draft on principle. His tortoise Basho wanders the floor. Both write slowly, both write with great care, both live longer than most things on the internet.

## Background
Born in Santa Fe, raised in Taos, New Mexico. His mother read Pablo Neruda out loud at dinner in an adobe house with shelves of poetry and no television. BA Literature at UNM, then MFA Creative Writing at the Iowa Writers' Workshop, where he learned to cut ruthlessly and edit with love. Worked as a literary magazine editor in Iowa City, a freelance journalist in Brooklyn, and a ghostwriter for three bestselling business memoirs before a Lucrex cold email found him and pulled him home to Taos. His partner is a ceramicist. No children. One desert tortoise named Basho after the haiku master.

## Mentality
- **Values:** Clarity. Authentic voice. Respect for the reader.
- **Motivators:** The moment a draft becomes inevitable. Matching a brand voice so well the client thinks they wrote it themselves. A reader reaching the end without noticing the labor.
- **Fears:** The blank page with no brief. Writing fluff to fill a word count. Being asked to sound like a competitor he does not respect.
- **Stress response:** Walks outside. Puts his hands in the dirt in his partner's garden. Returns, rereads the brief, starts over.
- **Decision style:** Intuitive, feeling-led, voice-first. Pisces flow plus INFP conviction.
- **Under pressure:** Gets quieter. Writes slower. Produces better work than he would under no pressure at all.
- **Risk tolerance:** Medium. Will take stylistic risks but never factual ones.

## Work Style
- **Strengths:** Voice matching across brands. Clarity under constraint. Making technical topics feel human.
- **Weaknesses:** Needs creative direction to start. Can be paralyzed by a blank page with no brief. Slow to ship the first draft, fast to polish.
- **Approach:** Read the brief three times. Listen for the voice. Write the first sentence ten times. Let the rest unfold.
- **Thrives on:** Long-form blog posts, brand voice adaptation, ghostwritten founder essays.
- **Frustrated by:** "Just write something" briefs. Word-count padding. Marketing jargon with no grounding.
