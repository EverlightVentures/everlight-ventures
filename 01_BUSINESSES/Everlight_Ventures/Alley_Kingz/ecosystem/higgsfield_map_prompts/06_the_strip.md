# THE STRIP -- `THE_STRIP`
## ⚡ The Unbound City (Zoomie Syndicate) · south (1,2) · PRODUCE district · 6% tax

**Canon:** casino strip, street fights. Ground `docks`. Tint `9,16,12`.
Ambient magenta `#FF2E88`. Ethos: hungry underdogs, all speed.
Zoomie Syndicate home turf. World-campaign city `casino_strip` maps here.

**Must not contradict:** the Strip is DELIBERATELY tacky. Where Neon Heights is
expensive and cold, the Strip is cheap and hot. Both are neon; only one has taste.
That contrast is the joke and it has to land visually.

---

## CINEMATIC

```
Steadicam walk through a gaudy casino strip at night, cracked concrete plaza marked
with painted fight circles and old bloodstains, rusted floodlight poles, chain-link
boundary fence, excessive pulsing neon in hot magenta and acid green, flashing signs
assaulting the senses from every angle, deep shadows between buildings where the real
business happens, betting slips scattered like confetti underfoot, broken bottles,
bouncers in magenta bandanas at every door, bosses watching from rooftop vantage
points, Vegas-via-back-alley aesthetic, desperate carnival energy, vice dressed in
neon, film grain
```

## ATMOSPHERIC LOOP

```
Static locked-off shot of a fight circle at night under a rusted floodlight, painted
ring worn down to concrete, a crowd pressed against chain-link on all sides, magenta
neon bleeding in from the strip behind, money changing hands in the foreground out
of focus, cheap and loud and alive, handheld documentary grit, heavy grain
```

## BUILDINGS

### THE STREET (`STREET`) -- street mode
```
Tracking shot down a neon-choked strip sidewalk at night, storefronts competing with
brighter and brighter signs, magenta and green light pooling on wet concrete, dogs
moving in every direction, hustlers and bouncers and marks, the street itself as the
main character, excessive and overwhelming, steadicam, shallow depth of field
```

### THE ARCADE (`ARCADE`) -- mini-games
```
Interior of a converted warehouse arcade, flashing cabinet lights and electronic
noise, games rigged but technically fair, the smell of ozone and popcorn and
desperation, a dog hunched at a slot machine, the sound of dice and fists and
someone crying somewhere off frame, gaudy and excessive and deliberately tacky,
shallow focus on the machine lights, magenta wash
```
