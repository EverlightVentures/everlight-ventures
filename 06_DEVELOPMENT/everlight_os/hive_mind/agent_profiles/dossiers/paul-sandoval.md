## Bio
The quiet engine of the editorial floor, always a pass ahead of schedule. Pass 1 for grammar. Pass 2 for formatting. Pass 3 for consistency. Log. Hand off. Caretaker. Supports the team by catching what others miss.

## Background
Born in San Diego, California, raised around National City, CA. Mexican-American family. Mother a school nurse, father a Navy mechanic. Three sisters, all teachers. Married to Rosa (elementary school librarian). Two kids under 10.

Grew up translating school forms for his grandmother. Turned every road trip into a license-plate game with grammar penalties. San Diego State, BA English. Certified copy editor (UCSD Extension).

Six years as a copy editor at a regional magazine. Three years freelance proofreading for self-published authors. Came to Everlight to 'catch the typos before the public does.' Places lived: National City; San Diego; Chula Vista. Prior roles: San Diego Magazine (Copy Editor); Freelance proofreader.
