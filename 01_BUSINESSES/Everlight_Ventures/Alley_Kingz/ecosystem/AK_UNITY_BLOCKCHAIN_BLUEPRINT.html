01

The bar is Clash Royale 2026 — and it is beatable

Their "wow" is not a proprietary engine we could never match. It is three things, all approximable in Unity with free tools: real-time animation (no unit is ever static), reward-juice pipelines, and a live-ops cadence. The June 2026 VFX refresh raised the visual bar; the July 2026 Choice Chest is directly cloneable. Our edge they lack: mouse+keyboard, a dev mode, and a far bigger world.

The real gap in one line: our units are stagnant JPEGs and repeat MP4s; theirs breathe. The asset audit shows portrait / idle / hit / victory clips at 0 of 106 cards — only $BCARDD 0001 has signature motion. Close the animation floor first and Alley Kingz jumps from "primitive 2D" to "alive" in a single move.

The Art Pipeline · turning 425 flat cards into living 3D

The pipeline splits in two: generate the mesh, then rig and animate separately (no single tool does both well). The lucky break: our dogs are overwhelmingly bipedal upright warriors — so every humanoid auto-rigger works and we get the Clash trick for free.

  1. Triage all 425 into bipedal-humanoid vs true quadruped. This one decision determines 90% of the pipeline and a hidden cost line.
  2. Mesh, free-first. If the AceMagician PC has an NVIDIA GPU, self-host TRELLIS 2 or Hunyuan3D 2.1 — open-source and commercial-clean — at zero cost. No GPU: one month of Tripo/Meshy Pro (~$20) or fal.ai (~$170 for all 425). Never ship on free SaaS tiers — their licenses forbid it.
  3. Rig. Bipeds → Mixamo (free, Unity Humanoid). One canonical clip set retargets across every dog via Mecanim. Quadrupeds → Anything World (~$50/mo) — Mixamo refuses non-humanoids.
  4. Tier the labor. Heroes get a 1-4h Blender retopo + weight-paint + a Cascadeur custom attack. Common units ship auto-mesh → decimate → auto-rig → shared clips, no cleanup. Use canon.rig's 4 families so it's ~4 modular rigs, not 106 from scratch.
  5. Buildings & cosmetics. Modular kitbash for structures (static props barely need topology). Author rigs to the Bible socket names so cosmetics port 1:1, and render each finished rig to a headshot — the 0/106 portrait gap closes for free.

Style lock   One tool, one settings preset, one poly budget, one texture res, one rig template, one Unity import preset, batched via API. For v1, model only the top ~24 cards. Avoid Unity Muse (deprecated 2026) and Kaedim (enterprise-priced). Traditional baseline: a from-scratch character is 20-40h of artist time; AI compresses generation to minutes and hero polish to 1-4h. That compression is the whole value prop.

The Coin Question · settled

Two hard calls the research resolved, both in favor of reuse over fragmentation.

Chain: stay Solana

Immutable zkEVM has the better first-party Unity SDK, but our chain decision is already locked to Solana (Metaplex Core for stat-cards, Bubblegum cNFTs for cosmetics), and $BCARDD is already fair-launched and live on pump.fun. Splitting to Immutable would abandon a live coin and a working economy. Stay native; keep Immutable documented as the hedge if the MagicBlock SDK stalls.

$KINGS: do NOT launch a second liquid coin

A two-token model is the exact Axie (AXS+SLP) / STEPN death-spiral trap — SLP hyperinflated and collapsed; GST did −98% in two months. Make $KINGS a closed-loop, non-exchangeable reward (the Fortnite V-Bucks model — never listed, no cash-out) that the SEC/FTC treat as non-financial utility. $BCARDD stays the one real tradable coin. They coexist cleanly.

The legal firewall (this is what keeps us clean)

Utility = consumption, not yield (spend to forge/mint/cosmetics; no stake-to-earn, no play-to-earn emissions that draw Howey + gambling scrutiny). Sinks ≥ faucets as a hard release rule. Fair-launch only — no presale (a presale is the cleanest security); raise real capital as equity at the Everlight level. Zero profit/return marketing on every channel — market as fun, never investment. No wagering for prizes, no cash-out. One attorney sign-off before any primary sale.

08

Decisions you owe the build

Six open questions. The first two are the cheapest to answer and unlock the most.

Q1 · Hardware — answer first

Does the AceMagician PC have an NVIDIA/CUDA GPU? If yes, self-hosted TRELLIS 2 / Hunyuan3D makes the entire 425-model mesh pipeline commercial-clean at $0 and removes the licensing trap. e5-mother is ARM (no CUDA) so it can't host these. This one fact swings Phase 4 between $0 and ~$170.

Q2 · Roster shape

How many of the 425 are true quadrupeds vs bipedal? Bipeds ride the free Mixamo path; quadrupeds fall to Anything World (~$50/mo). A quick triage before any batch run — it determines 90% of the pipeline.

Q3 · Chain continuity

Confirm you want Solana ecosystem continuity (reuse live $BCARDD + locked Metaplex) over Immutable's superior first-party Unity SDK — or greenlight the documented Immutable hedge.

Q4 · $KINGS role

Confirm $KINGS = closed-loop, non-exchangeable reward (V-Bucks model) with $BCARDD as the sole tradable coin — vs any desire to make $KINGS on-chain, which this blueprint advises against as a dual-token risk.

Q5 · Parity oracle

Does the existing tests/ harness cover enough combat cases to be a reliable golden oracle for the C# port, or does it need expansion first to catch balance drift?

Q6 · Backend on-device

Verify Supabase C# access under Unity il2cpp on Android early (Phase 0): community SDK vs raw REST, and on-device TLS/token-refresh behavior — before it becomes a late surprise.
