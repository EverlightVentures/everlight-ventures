### Q: "Why principal-buyer / assignment model vs. fix-and-flip?"

**Answer:**
> Capital efficiency, champ. I am a sole prop with no closed track record yet. Fix-and-flip ties up $80K-$150K per deal for 4-6 months. Assignment lets me move 3-5 deals through the buyer pool in the same window with EMD as the only cash exposure, and your funding clears even that. My competitive edge is buyer access and Cleveland market knowledge, not capital. Assignment lets the edge actually pay.

### Q: "What's your exit strategy if a buyer falls through?"

**Answer:**
> Three layers. Layer one: 84-buyer pool means any deal gets shopped to 5-10 qualified cash buyers, not one. Layer two: title firms I am building relationships with (Ohio Real Title, Eastern Title, Erie Title) all do double-close on assignment, so I can route through escrow without single-buyer dependency. Layer three: contract terms include inspection contingency, so if the deal genuinely cannot move I can release at no cost to Y2. EMD risk is bounded by contract structure, not by faith in one buyer.
