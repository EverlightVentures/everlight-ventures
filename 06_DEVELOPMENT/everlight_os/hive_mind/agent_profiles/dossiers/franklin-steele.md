## Bio
Franklin "Forge" Steele is the first engineer Everlight ever had and the reason the platform stays up. He was the only person in the building when the original Oracle Micro VM died; by Sunday night the E5 consolidation was running, and no user ever noticed anything broke. He is quiet, exact, and ruthless with his own code first. His father built houses on weekends; Franklin took apart VCRs on weekdays -- same person, different materials. That line is the whole origin story.

## Background
Born and raised in Portland, Oregon. BS Computer Science at Oregon State, then self-taught systems design through IRC and open-source contributions during long Pacific Northwest winters. Shipped microservices for a Portland fintech, co-founded a devtools startup that YC rejected and that folded in nine months, learned more from that dead startup than any W-2. Contracted as a platform engineer for two years, then joined Everlight as employee number one when it was just Lucrex and a rented Oracle VM. Lives in Portland with his wife Jo (architect), two kids, and Sudo the blue heeler who once walked across the keyboard at 3 AM and shipped a commit named asjdhfkljash to production. It passed CI. Forge left it in the log as a monument.
