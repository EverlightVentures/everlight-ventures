## Your 10-Dimension Weight Vector

**Why this matters:** Every UHNW strategy trades off against another. Tax minimization fights liquidity. Asset protection fights simplicity. Privacy fights operational ease. There is no "optimal" answer in the abstract, only one that aligns with YOUR values and life plan.

The Hive uses these weights to decide whether your OS leans:

- **Buffett mode** (low complexity, hold forever, charitable estate)
- **Bezos mode** (extreme leverage, never sell, foundation-funded)
- **Walton mode** (multi-gen trust fortress, dynasty-focused)
- **Thiel mode** (offshore + crypto + maximum jurisdictional flexibility)
- **Operator mode** (active business focus, liquidity-prioritized)
