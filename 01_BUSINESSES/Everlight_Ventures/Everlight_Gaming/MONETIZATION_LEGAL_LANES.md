# Monetization Legal Lanes - Loot Box (Gacha) vs Sweepstakes
**Date:** 2026-06-03 | Applies to B Card Blackjack + the Alley Kingz marketplace.

Gambling = **pay + chance + cashable prize** (all three). Kill ANY one = legal. There are two clean ways to
take money for a chance-based reward without being illegal gambling. They kill DIFFERENT elements.

## Lane A - LOOT BOX / GACHA  (COD Mobile, PUBG, EA, Genshin)
- You PAY, it is CHANCE, but the reward is **IN-GAME ONLY (not cashable for real money)** -> no cashable
  prize -> **legal.** You can **sell the draws directly.**
- COD Mobile mythic "lucky draws" (~$300 to pull a mythic via escalating draws) and PUBG crates are legal in
  the US and have not been successfully sued. The reward is a weapon/skin with no cash-out value.
- **Escalating-draw pricing** (each draw shifts the pool/price until the mythic is guaranteed at ~$X) is a
  strong, legal, high-margin model. Rich's friend reverse-engineered COD's at ~$300/mythic = by design.
- Watch: some COUNTRIES regulate (Belgium ban, China odds-disclosure). US best practice = **disclose odds**
  + never allow real-money cash-out of the items.

## Lane B - SWEEPSTAKES  (Chumba, Stake.us, the B Card Blackjack cash-out)
- The reward IS cashable (SC -> $), so you kill the **PAYMENT** instead: free entry (AMOE), sell Gold not SC.
- This is the lane for anything that pays out **real money.**

## Which lane for which product
- **B Card Blackjack cash-out** -> **Lane B (sweepstakes)**, because SC cashes out. You can ALSO run Lane-A
  cosmetic / Gold-pack draws inside the casino (in-game only).
- **Alley Kingz marketplace** -> **Lane A (loot box/gacha)** works great and is SIMPLER (sell draws directly,
  no free-entry needed) **IF the cards/skins are IN-GAME value only**, exactly like COD. Apply the escalating
  mythic-draw pricing.

## THE WRINKLE - Alley Kingz cards are NFTs tied to $BCARDD
If the NFT cards have **real secondary-market / resale value**, a paid lucky-draw for them edges back toward
gambling (you paid for a chance at a real-value asset). This is the unsettled NFT-gambling frontier (regulators
have flagged "blockchain gacha"). Safest options:
1. **Keep draw-obtained cards in-game value only** (pure loot box = clearly legal like COD), OR
2. If they are tradeable NFTs: get the attorney on the **draw mechanic specifically**, disclose odds, and
   never market the resale value. Keep the card GAME utility fun-first.

## The one rule you never break
**Never sell a paid draw whose prize is cashable for real money.** Paid + chance + cashable = gambling.
- Loot box = SELL the draw, prize stays in-game.  Sweeps = FREE entry, prize cashes out.
- Keep the two lanes clean and separate; never blend "buy a draw to win cashable SC."
