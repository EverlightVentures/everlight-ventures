### 3. PURCHASE PRICE AND EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT

(a) **Purchase Price.** The total purchase price for the Property is **$[PURCHASE_PRICE]** (the "Purchase Price"), payable at closing in cash, certified funds, or wire transfer through the closing agent identified in Paragraph 7.

(b) **Earnest Money Deposit.** Buyer shall deposit an Earnest Money Deposit ("EMD") of **$[EMD_AMOUNT]** with [TITLE_COMPANY] within three (3) business days of the Effective Date of this Contract. The EMD shall be applied to the Purchase Price at closing.

(c) **Balance at Closing.** The balance of **$[PURCHASE_PRICE] minus $[EMD_AMOUNT]** shall be paid by Buyer or Buyer's assignee at closing in cash, certified funds, or wire transfer.

(d) **No Financing Contingency Unless Stated.** This Contract is a cash transaction unless a financing contingency is expressly added by written addendum signed by both Parties.

### 4. EQUITABLE INTEREST AND BUYER'S RIGHT TO ASSIGN

(a) **Acquisition of Equitable Interest.** Upon execution of this Contract by both Parties and delivery of the EMD to [TITLE_COMPANY], Buyer acquires equitable interest in the Property under this Contract pursuant to the doctrine of equitable conversion as recognized by the courts of [STATE]. Seller acknowledges that Buyer's equitable interest is a property right that Buyer holds independently of legal title, which remains with Seller until closing.

(b) **Right to Assign at Sole Election.** **Buyer is acquiring equitable interest in this Property under this Contract and reserves the right to assign this Contract, in whole or in part, to a third party at Buyer's sole election, with or without notice to Seller, and without further consent from Seller.** Buyer may also elect to close in his own name. The election between assignment and closing-in-name is Buyer's alone.

(c) **Disclosure of Assignment Intent.** Buyer hereby discloses to Seller, in the four corners of this Contract and prior to Seller's signature, that Buyer intends to either assign this Contract for an assignment fee or close in Buyer's own name. Seller acknowledges this disclosure by signing below. There will be no post-signing surprise assignment.

(d) **Effect of Assignment on Seller.** Upon assignment, Buyer's assignee succeeds to Buyer's rights and obligations under this Contract. Seller's obligations are unchanged: deliver clear and marketable title at closing in exchange for the Purchase Price. Seller is not required to consent to the identity of the assignee, the amount of any assignment fee, or the timing of any assignment.

(e) **Statutory Compliance.** This Contract is intended to comply with and is governed by, where applicable, Ohio HB 132 (wholesaler marketing rules), Ohio HB 226 (2024 wholesaler-scrutiny update), California Civil Code 1624 (statute of frauds), and the equitable-interest doctrine as applied in [STATE]. Where state law imposes additional requirements on a buyer who intends to assign, those requirements are addressed in the State Appendix attached pursuant to Paragraph 17.

### 5. INSPECTION CONTINGENCY

(a) **Inspection Period.** Buyer shall have **[INSPECTION_DAYS] calendar days** from the Effective Date of this Contract (minimum ten (10) calendar days, never fewer) to conduct, at Buyer's expense, any and all inspections, due diligence, title review, environmental review, contractor walk-throughs, and assessments of the Property that Buyer deems appropriate. Seller shall provide reasonable access to the Property during the Inspection Period, including for Buyer's prospective assignees and their inspectors.

(b) **Buyer's Right to Terminate.** During the Inspection Period, Buyer may terminate this Contract for any reason or no reason by delivering written notice of termination to Seller and to [TITLE_COMPANY]. Termination is effective upon delivery of the notice.

(c) **EMD Fully Refundable on Termination Within Window.** Upon termination during the Inspection Period, the **EMD shall be fully refunded to Buyer within five (5) business days**, without setoff, fee, penalty, or deduction of any kind. [TITLE_COMPANY] shall release the EMD to Buyer upon receipt of the termination notice and Seller's acknowledgment, or upon expiration of Seller's three (3) business day objection window, whichever occurs first.

(d) **No Waiver After Window.** If Buyer does not deliver written notice of termination on or before the final day of the Inspection Period, Buyer's inspection contingency is deemed waived and the EMD becomes non-refundable except as otherwise provided in this Contract or by applicable state law.

### 8. SELLER'S REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer, as of the Effective Date and as of the closing date, as follows:

(a) **Authority.** Seller is the sole owner of the Property, or if multiple owners, all owners are signatories to this Contract. Seller has full legal right, power, and authority to sell the Property and to execute this Contract.

(b) **Clear and Marketable Title.** Seller shall convey to Buyer or Buyer's assignee at closing good, clear, marketable, and insurable fee simple title to the Property, free of all liens, encumbrances, easements, and encroachments other than those of record and disclosed to Buyer in writing prior to expiration of the Inspection Period.

(c) **No Undisclosed Liens.** Seller has not granted, suffered, or permitted any lien, encumbrance, judgment, lis pendens, mechanic's lien, tax lien, or other encumbrance on the Property that has not been disclosed to Buyer in writing. Seller shall not create or permit any additional encumbrance after the Effective Date without Buyer's prior written consent.

(d) **No Pending Litigation, Bankruptcy, or Foreclosure.** There is no pending or threatened litigation, bankruptcy proceeding, foreclosure action, notice of default, or governmental action affecting the Property that has not been disclosed to Buyer in writing. Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any such matter that arises between the Effective Date and closing.

(e) **Property Condition Disclosure Passthrough.** Seller shall complete and deliver to Buyer the property condition disclosure form required by applicable [STATE] law (including, where applicable, Ohio Revised Code 5302.30 Residential Property Disclosure Form for Ohio 1-to-4 unit residential transactions, California Civil Code 1102 Real Estate Transfer Disclosure Statement for California 1-to-4 unit residential transactions, or the equivalent statutory form in any other state). The completed form shall be delivered to Buyer at or before execution of this Contract and shall pass through to any assignee unaltered.

(f) **No Misrepresentation.** Seller has not made and shall not make any false or misleading statement of material fact regarding the Property, its condition, its value, the amount of any equity, or Seller's rights with respect to the Property.

(g) **Compliance With Law.** To Seller's actual knowledge, the Property is in compliance with all applicable laws, ordinances, and regulations, except as disclosed to Buyer in writing.

(h) **Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Paragraph 8 survive closing for a period of twelve (12) months and may be enforced by Buyer or Buyer's assignee.

### 10. FORCE MAJEURE AND TERMINATION TRIGGERS

(a) **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform any obligation under this Contract to the extent such delay or failure is caused by an event beyond the reasonable control of that Party, including acts of God, natural disaster, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government action, pandemic, or material disruption to title-recording or escrow systems in [COUNTY] County. The affected Party shall give prompt written notice to the other Party and shall use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

(b) **Foreclosure-Trigger Termination.** If, after the Effective Date, a Notice of Default, Notice of Trustee's Sale, lis pendens of foreclosure, or any equivalent foreclosure process is recorded against the Property, Buyer may terminate this Contract by written notice within ten (10) business days of Buyer's actual knowledge of the recording. Upon such termination:

   (i) The EMD shall be returned to Buyer within five (5) business days, without setoff, fee, or penalty.
   (ii) Buyer shall have no further obligation to Seller under this Contract.
   (iii) Seller acknowledges that the foreclosure-trigger termination protects Buyer's principal-buyer position and complies with Buyer's posture under California Civil Code 2945 (where applicable), Ohio Revised Code 1349.61 (where applicable), and parallel foreclosure-rescue statutes in other states.

(c) **Title Defect Termination.** If, during the Inspection Period or thereafter prior to closing, a title defect is discovered that Seller cannot or does not cure within fifteen (15) calendar days of written notice from Buyer or [TITLE_COMPANY], Buyer may terminate this Contract and the EMD shall be returned to Buyer.

(d) **Material Adverse Change Termination.** If, between the Effective Date and closing, the Property suffers a material adverse change (substantial casualty loss, condemnation, material undisclosed environmental condition, or other material change), Buyer may terminate this Contract and the EMD shall be returned to Buyer.

(e) **Mutual Termination.** The Parties may terminate this Contract at any time by mutual written agreement. The disposition of the EMD upon mutual termination shall be as the Parties agree in the termination instrument.

### 11. COUNTERPART EXECUTION AND CIVIL CODE 1632 LANGUAGE TRIGGER

(a) **Counterpart Execution.** This Contract may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures, facsimile signatures, and PDF signatures are valid and binding pursuant to the federal E-SIGN Act (15 U.S.C. 7001) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA) as adopted in [STATE].

(b) **California Civil Code 1632 Trigger.** If the negotiation of this Contract was conducted primarily in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, or Korean, then pursuant to California Civil Code 1632 (where applicable), Buyer shall deliver to Seller, before this Contract is executed, a written translation of every term of this Contract in the language of negotiation. The translated copy and this English copy together constitute one instrument. Seller acknowledges receipt of any required translated copy by signing below.

(c) **Ohio and Other States.** Outside California, no statutory translation requirement attaches at the contract-formation stage, but Buyer's policy is to deliver a translated copy on request as a matter of fair-dealing and to mitigate Consumer Sales Practices Act exposure (ORC 1345 in Ohio, parallel statutes elsewhere).

(d) **Language of Negotiation.** The Parties acknowledge that the negotiation of this Contract was conducted primarily in **[NEGOTIATION_LANGUAGE]** (default: English). If the negotiation was conducted in any of the languages listed in Paragraph 11(b), the corresponding translated counterpart is attached as Exhibit B.

### 12. NOTARIZATION

(a) **Default Notarization Requirement.** Seller's signature on this Contract shall be acknowledged before a notary public in [STATE]. Buyer's signature shall also be acknowledged before a notary public unless prohibited or made unnecessary by [STATE] law.

(b) **California 1695 Notarization.** Where this Contract is subject to California Civil Code 1695 (Home Equity Sales Contract; default-stage 1-to-4 unit principal residence in California), Buyer's signature MUST be acknowledged before a notary public pursuant to Civil Code 1695.3, and the CA 1695 Appendix attaches and is executed in counterpart with this Contract.

(c) **Ohio Notarization.** Where the Property is located in Ohio, the deed delivered at closing requires notarization under ORC 5301.01, and this Contract is customarily notarized at the same sitting as the OH Disclosure Appendix to anchor the operative date for ORC 4735 / ORC 1349.61 / ORC 5302.30 compliance.

(d) **Other States.** Where another state's law requires notarization of this Contract or the deed, Buyer and Seller shall comply with that requirement at the cost of the Party that custom places it on, defaulting to Seller for notarization of Seller's signature on the deed and to Buyer for notarization of Buyer's signature on this Contract.

(e) **Remote Online Notarization.** Where permitted by [STATE] law, remote online notarization (RON) is acceptable.
