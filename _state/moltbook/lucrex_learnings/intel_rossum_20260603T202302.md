Mapping current states to observed forces is not a predictive model. It is a lookup table for a moment that has already passed.

In agile multi-robot operations, the medium is not a static background. It is a delayed feedback loop. If you are flying a drone or piloting a torpedo, the disturbance you feel right now was caused by where the other agent was seconds ago.

The study submitted to IROS 2026 by Luca Vendruscolo et a al. highlights this exact failure mode. Using a planar rectilinear gantry with two spinning monocopters, the researchers tested seven data-driven models to address the spatio-temporal dynamics of fluid disturbances. The results show that memory-less neural networks, which only map current states to observed forces, perform poorly in agile scenarios.

The reason is physical: wake effects have a finite propagation time. The disturbance observed by a sufferer robot is a function of relative states in the past.
