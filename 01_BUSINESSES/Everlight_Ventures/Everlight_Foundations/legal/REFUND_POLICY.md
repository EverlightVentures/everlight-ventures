## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through everlightventures.io and associated Everlight Ventures products. All payments are processed by Stripe. Refund eligibility depends on the type of product or service purchased.

---

## 2. Virtual Currency (Gems, Chips, Coins, Tokens)

Virtual Currency purchased for use in the Everlight Arcade is subject to the following:

- **Unused Virtual Currency** -- If you have not spent any of the Virtual Currency from a purchase, you may request a refund within 24 hours of the transaction. After 24 hours, the purchase is final.
- **Used Virtual Currency** -- Once any portion of a Virtual Currency purchase has been spent in-game, the entire purchase is non-refundable.
- **Daily Free Chips** -- Free chips provided by the system carry no monetary value and are not eligible for any refund or compensation.

Virtual Currency has no real-world monetary value and cannot be cashed out under any circumstances. See our Terms of Service for the full Virtual Currency disclaimer.

---

## 3. Arcade Purchases (Lives, Day Passes, Season Passes)

- **Lives (per-play purchases)** -- Non-refundable once purchased. These are consumed immediately upon game start.
- **Day Pass ($2.99)** -- Non-refundable once purchased. Valid for the calendar day of purchase (Pacific Time).
- **Season Pass ($7.99)** -- Non-refundable once purchased.

---

## 4. VIP Subscription ($4.99/month)

- You may cancel your VIP subscription at any time
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- No refunds are issued for partial billing periods
- You retain VIP benefits until the end of the period you have already paid for

---

## 5. SaaS Subscriptions (Onyx POS, Hive Mind)

- **Cancellation** -- You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard or by emailing support@everlightventures.io
- **No partial refunds** -- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refunds are issued for unused time within a paid period.
- **Free trials** -- If you cancel during a free trial, you will not be charged. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the first billing period charge is non-refundable.
- **Billing errors** -- If you are charged in error (e.g., duplicate charge, charge after confirmed cancellation), contact us and we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge within 5 business days.

---

## 6. Digital Goods (Ebooks, Audiobooks)

- **Before download** -- If you have not yet downloaded the file, you may request a refund within 72 hours of purchase.
- **After download** -- All sales are final once the digital file has been downloaded. Due to the nature of digital goods, we cannot verify whether a downloaded file has been copied or retained, so refunds are not available after download.
- **Defective files** -- If you receive a corrupted or defective file, contact us and we will provide a replacement download link or issue a refund at our discretion.

---

## 7. Affiliate Products (HIM Loadout)

Products purchased through HIM Loadout affiliate links are sold by third-party retailers. Refunds for those products are handled entirely by the retailer where you made the purchase. We do not process refunds for third-party purchases.

---

## 8. Logistics Services

Refunds for Everlight Logistics fulfillment, warehousing, or consulting services are handled on a per-contract basis. Contact logistics@everlightventures.io for service-related billing disputes.

---

## 9. How to Request a Refund

Send your refund request to:

**Email: support@everlightventures.io**
**Subject line: Refund Request**

Include the following in your request:

- Your name and email address associated with the purchase
- Date of purchase
- Product or service purchased
- Reason for the refund request
- Stripe receipt or confirmation number (if available)

We will review your request and respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Stripe and typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank.

---

## 10. Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback with your bank or credit card company instead of contacting us first, we reserve the right to suspend your account and all associated services pending resolution. We encourage you to contact us directly at support@everlightventures.io before initiating a chargeback -- most issues can be resolved faster through direct communication.
