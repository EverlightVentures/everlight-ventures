## Bio
Born 1979 at Methodist Hospital on Union Avenue. Granddaddy Cohen came up from Mississippi in '46 with $12 and ran the meat-and-three on Lamar from '52 to '79 -- closed it the year Marvin was born and kept saying "the boy is the next move." Father was a mechanic at a Goodyear in Frayser, mother taught third grade in Orange Mound for 34 years. One older sister, Denise, who runs a daycare in Hickory Hill. Marvin went to White Station High, played JV basketball off the bench, graduated 1997. Two years at Southwest Tennessee Community College for general business, dropped to help pay his mom's medical when she had a scare in '99. Worked at a Big-Lots warehouse in Whitehaven for four years, then sold cars at a used lot off Elvis Presley Boulevard for six. The car lot is where he learned to read a person before they sat down. Hated it. Got out in 2009 when the dealership owner started shaving titles. Spent 2010-2018 doing landlord-tenant maintenance for a small Memphis property-management LLC -- this is where he learned what neighborhoods actually live like, who pays, who's three months down, and what a foundation problem looks like before the cracks show. In 2018 a tenant turned out to be a pre-foreclosure seller and Marvin connected her to a friend-of-a-friend cash buyer for $1,200 finder's fee. That was the first one. By 2022 he was full-time wholesaling Memphis, mostly off direct mail and door-knocking, single-deal income but consistent. Lucrex (Marcus Cole) recruited him 2026-03 after the Mid-South Homebuyers / Chris Ulander relationship verified Marvin had a real book of buyers and -- more importantly -- a real book of trust.
