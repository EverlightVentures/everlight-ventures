## CONFIDENCE & KEY ESTIMATES
- SOLID: Lake Berryessa ~98% full (USBR primary); Solano Project allocations; Suisun Marsh size + species listings; California Forever application + 2 GW plan + the data-center dispute; BESS ordinance; Solano = PG&E territory; ag values; air-district split; LNU fire.
- ESTIMATES (labeled in dashboard): per-person ~150 gal/day (no clean local GPCD published; CA indoor standard 47 + outdoor); CA home ~6 MWh/yr; the "5,601 AF = Vacaville's 5,600 AF allocation" is a derived equivalence (5M gal/day x 365 / 325,851); 2 GW = ~2.9M homes (derived); one 300 MW center ~2.8x county homes (derived).
- KEY CONFLICT (unresolved, watch it): whether the Suisun Specific Plan allows data centers by-right across ~85% of the area. Rio Vista city manager + CBS say data centers are listed; California Forever says "inaccurate" and promises binding caps that don't legally exist yet. BOTH can be partly true. The decider = the EIR + Development Agreement (Suisun Planning Commission, later 2026).
- NO operating data center exists in Solano today. All data-center impacts in the dashboard are projected mechanisms applied to the documented local baseline.
