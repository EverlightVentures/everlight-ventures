5. The unknown — what's on the orphan that may NOT be in e5_data

Without mounting the orphan, we cannot know with certainty. Here's the educated-guess list of categories worth verifying when Pass 2 runs:
  • System logs: /var/log/journal/* (journald history of the dead system -- useful for post-mortem)
  • Cron state: /var/spool/cron/crontabs/* (any system or user crons that weren't migrated)
  • PostgreSQL data: /var/lib/postgresql/* (if .250 ran Blinko on real Postgres rather than the SQLite fallback we recovered)
  • Docker volumes: /var/lib/docker/volumes/* (any container persistence not captured by e5_data)
  • SSL/TLS state: /etc/letsencrypt/*, /etc/ssl/private/*
  • Nextcloud / Documenso state: if those ran on .250, their app data is at /var/www/* or /srv/*
  • Browser/SSH state: ~/.cache/*, ~/.config/* beyond what came over in e5_data
  • The original dispatcher_relay/relay.py: I reconstructed it from memory; binary diff will tell us if my reconstruction is bit-accurate.
