# L3 -- Domicile Arbitrage
## Where You, Your Entities, and Your Assets Sit

**Same income, different domicile, different effective tax rate. The difference between CA and PR on $1M cap gains is $373,000 in saved tax. Per year. Forever.**

---

## Three Domicile Decisions

You make three SEPARATE domicile decisions that compound:

1. **Personal domicile** -- where YOU live, where your W-2 / personal income is taxed
2. **Entity domicile** -- where your LLCs, corps, partnerships are formed
3. **Trust domicile** -- where your trusts are sited

Each has its own optimal answer. They don't have to match.

## 1. Personal Domicile -- The 9 No-Income-Tax States

**United States states with NO personal state income tax:**

| State | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Florida** | Strongest homestead protection (uncapped on primary residence in many counties). Warm. Major UHNW destination. |
| **Texas** | No state income tax. Lower cost of living than FL. Strong asset protection. |
| **Tennessee** | No income tax (Hall tax repealed 2021). Cheap housing. |
| **Nevada** | No state income tax. Strongest charging-order protection. Vegas/Reno ecosystem. |
| **Washington** | No state income tax (but capital gains tax for high earners now exists). |
| **South Dakota** | No state income tax. Trust capital of the US. |
| **Wyoming** | No state income tax. Entity-friendly. Outdoor lifestyle. |
| **Alaska** | No state income tax. Pays YOU dividends (Permanent Fund). Cold. |
| **New Hampshire** | No income tax on wages. Tax on dividends/interest >$2,400 (phasing out by 2027). |

**The savings:** California top marginal rate is 13.3% (plus 1% mental health surcharge over $1M = 14.4%). On $1M income, moving to FL/TX saves $144,000/yr.

**The proof bar:** Domicile is HARD to establish for ex-California residents. CA Franchise Tax Board hunts ex-residents for years. To establish a new domicile:

- Spend >183 days outside CA (CRITICAL, single biggest factor)
- Sell or rent out CA primary residence
- New driver's license in new state
- New vehicle registration
- New voter registration
- Move bank accounts (or open new primary in new state)
- Update IRS, SSA, employer, insurance with new address
- New doctors, dentists, gyms, professionals in new state
- New church, civic affiliations
- Family in new state if possible
- Get a homestead exemption in new state

**The aggressive CA audit:** Franchise Tax Board can audit "domicile change" up to 4 years back. They check cell phone tower records, credit card transactions, social media check-ins. If you "moved" but kept your CA condo and visited 100 days/yr, they win.

## 4. Federal Arbitrage -- Puerto Rico Act 60

**Act 60 (formerly Acts 20/22) is the only legitimate way for US citizens to dramatically reduce federal tax without renouncing citizenship.**

### Act 60 Individual Investor Decree (former Act 22)
**The deal:**
- 0% tax on capital gains accrued AFTER becoming a PR resident
- 0% tax on dividends and interest from PR sources
- Capital gains accrued BEFORE becoming a PR resident: still federally taxable for 10 years, then 0% (this is the catch)

**Requirements:**
- Bona fide PR residency: physical presence test (183+ days/yr in PR), tax home in PR, closer connection to PR than elsewhere
- $10,000/yr donation to PR-approved nonprofit
- Buy a home in PR within 2 years of decree ($300k+ typical)
- Decree application + annual reporting

### Act 60 Export Services Decree (former Act 20)
**The deal:**
- 4% corporate tax rate on PR-sourced services exported outside PR
- 0% on dividends from the Act 60 corporation to bona-fide-resident shareholders

**Requirements:**
- PR-incorporated entity
- Real economic substance in PR (employees, office, operations)
- Services provided to clients OUTSIDE PR
- Decree fee + annual reporting

### Combined Power
At $1M revenue with mostly profit margin:
- **CA-based founder/owner:** 37% federal + 13.3% CA + 3.8% NIIT = $540k tax
- **PR Act 60 (corp + individual):** 4% PR corp on $1M = $40k. Dividend to PR-resident owner = $0 tax. Total: $40k.
- **Savings: $500k/yr.**

### The Catches
- You actually have to live in PR most of the year. Not a paper move.
- You lose the FEIE ($126,500/yr) since PR is technically US.
- Pre-move appreciation still federally taxable for 10 years.
- IRS scrutinizes Act 60 residents heavily. Sloppy compliance = audit.
- Hurricane risk, infrastructure, lifestyle considerations.

### When It Makes Sense for Everlight
- T9+ ($10M+ liquid net worth or sustained $1M+/yr income)
- Willing to physically relocate
- Business is portable (which yours is -- AI-driven, remote-first)
- Pre-move planning 12-24 months out

## 5. Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE)

**For US citizens working outside the US:**
- **2025 limit:** $130,000/yr of foreign earned income excluded
- **Plus housing exclusion:** ~$36,000/yr for high-cost cities

**Requirements (one of):**
- **Physical Presence Test:** 330+ days outside US in any 12-month period
- **Bona Fide Residence Test:** Established residence in foreign country for full tax year

**Compatible domiciles for digital nomads:**
- **UAE/Dubai:** 0% personal income tax, residency via investor visa or freelance permit
- **Portugal NHR:** 10-year window, low tax on foreign income (program changed in 2024 but grandfathered)
- **Singapore:** Territorial tax, only Singapore-sourced income taxed
- **Thailand LTR:** Long-term resident visa, foreign income exemption
- **Costa Rica / Panama:** Territorial, residency programs

## 6. Section 962 Election (For International Operations)

If you operate a foreign corporation as a US individual, your share of foreign income flows up at INDIVIDUAL tax rates (37%). Section 962 election lets the income be taxed at CORPORATE rates (21%) instead, with a foreign tax credit.

Best for: foreign C-corp owners with high foreign income, US tax residency.

## Common Mistakes

1. **"Moving" but keeping CA primary residence.** FTB destroys this. If you "moved" to NV but kept the CA house, FTB declares you're still CA-domiciled. Sell, lease long-term, or convert to investment property.
2. **Forgetting CA's 4-year audit lookback on domicile changes.** They go back 4 tax years. Document EVERYTHING for at least 5 years post-move.
3. **Forming everything in your home state.** Defeats half the purpose of L1.
4. **Forming in DE without QSBS strategy.** Higher fees, public records, no benefit unless taking VC or pursuing 1202.
5. **Acting as PR resident without bona fide residency.** Spending 100 days in PR and claiming Act 60 = audit + back taxes + penalties.
6. **Day counting errors for FEIE.** US territorial waters, transit days, "vacation in US" days all count or don't count by specific rules. Track daily, audit your tracking.

## Decision Matrix

```
Personal income < $200k AND no liquidity event in 5 yrs:
    -> Stay where you are. Domicile change isn't worth the friction.

Personal income $200k-$1M AND/OR major liquidity event coming in 2-5 yrs:
    -> Plan a CA exit to FL/TX/NV/TN. 12-24 month timeline.

Personal income $1M+ AND/OR $5M+ liquid wealth:
    -> Personal domicile in no-income-tax state mandatory.
    -> Trust domicile in SD mandatory.

Personal income $5M+/yr OR $25M+ wealth AND business is portable:
    -> Run PR Act 60 analysis seriously.

You hold a foreign passport AND don't need US residency:
    -> Talk to an expat tax attorney about UAE/Singapore/Portugal optimization.
```
