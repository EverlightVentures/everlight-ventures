# NEON HEIGHTS -- `NEON_HEIGHTS`
## 👑 The Crowned City (K9 Circuitry) -- CAPITAL · NE (2,0) · STONE district · 6% tax

**Canon:** glossy elite heights, the drip. Ground `midtown`. Tint `16,10,20`.
Ambient teal `#00E0C0`. Ethos: elite, arrogant, feared.
Crew aliases: Circuit Hounds, The Grid Pack, Voltage Kennel.
This is the **capital** of the Crowned city. Canon location for all skyline scenes.
Season copy names one drop here: **NeonReach** ("A Blood Moon over NeonReach").

**Must not contradict:** Crowned = cold teal `#00E0C0`, not warm. Nothing here is
weathered. This is the one district where surfaces are clean, and that cleanliness
should read as menace, not comfort. Wealth as a wall.
