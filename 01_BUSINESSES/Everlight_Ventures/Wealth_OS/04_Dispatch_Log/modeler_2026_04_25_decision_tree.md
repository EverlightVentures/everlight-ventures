## Three Branch Points Where the Plan Must Change

1. **Day 30 checkpoint: AI consulting reply rate.**
   - **Trigger:** Fewer than 3 qualified replies from 200 emails sent (1.5% reply rate).
   - **Action:** Kill the current outbound script. Rewrite the offer (tighter niche, sharper case study, video proof). Rerun for 2 weeks. If still under 1.5%, demote consulting to secondary lane and promote wholesale to primary.
   - **Why it matters:** Below 1.5% reply, the EV math inverts. Wholesale's $300/day beats a dead consulting funnel.

2. **Day 45 checkpoint: First wholesale lead quality.**
   - **Trigger:** Zero contracts under contract by day 45, OR 3+ leads worked but every buyer passed (signal that comp pricing is wrong, not that leads are bad).
   - **Action:** Kill solo wholesale. Pivot to JV-only mode (assign through partner wholesalers for half-fees). Reroute the freed time to consulting + Hive Mind productization.
   - **Why it matters:** Solo wholesale at T0 burns time fastest. JV mode preserves the lane without the cost-of-acquisition tax.

3. **Day 90 checkpoint: Cumulative revenue vs $25k floor.**
   - **Trigger:** Less than $25k cumulative cash banked by day 90.
   - **Action:** Marquise picks ONE lane and goes 90% on it for the next 90 days. The hybrid model fails when neither lane has critical mass. Default if no answer: whichever lane produced more revenue between day 0-90.
   - **Why it matters:** Two half-funded lanes lose to one fully-funded lane. The 12-month $947k math requires concentration after day 90, not diversification.

**Scenario 2: AI consulting saturation (every SMB already has someone).** Reply rates collapse below 1% by day 60. Recovery: pivot consulting to a tighter vertical (only contractors, only mobile-service businesses, only one zip code) where the case study lands hardest. Drop horizontal SMB outreach entirely. Wholesale becomes primary lane and target shifts to $300-$400k for the 12-month window. Probability: ~25%.

**Scenario 3: Wholesale market freeze (rate spike, buyer pool exits, Cleveland niche dies).** Lane A produces zero closes through day 180. Recovery: full pivot to consulting + Hive Mind productization. The remaining 6 months must produce $400k+ from the consulting + SaaS stack alone. Requires 8+ retainers at $5k MRR by day 270 and a six-figure SaaS exit or licensing deal in months 10-12. Realistic ceiling here is $300-$500k, not $947k. Probability: ~15%.

**Combined probability of at least one worst case firing: ~50%. The plan must absorb one of these scenarios and still hit $400k+. The path to $947k assumes none of the three fire and the operator hits the 90th percentile execution. Plan for $450k baseline, $947k stretch.**
