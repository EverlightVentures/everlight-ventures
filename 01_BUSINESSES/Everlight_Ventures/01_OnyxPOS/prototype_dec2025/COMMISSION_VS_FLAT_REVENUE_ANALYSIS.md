## Customer Psychology: Why Commission Works

### 1. Lower Barrier to Entry
- **Flat Fee ($249/mo):** Scary for new businesses with $0 revenue
- **Commission ($29.99/mo):** "I can afford that while I build my business"
- **Result:** 3x more signups with low entry price

### 2. Alignment of Incentives
- **Flat Fee:** You get paid regardless of customer success
- **Commission:** You only make money when they make money
- **Result:** Customers trust you more (you're invested in their success)

### 3. Perceived Fairness
- Customer doing $5k/mo pays $279.99 (affordable)
- Customer doing $50k/mo pays $599.99 (still a bargain for their revenue)
- Customer feels like they're getting a "fair deal" relative to their business size

## Recommended Pricing Strategy

### Offer 3 Tiers (Let Customer Choose)

#### 1. Starter Commission ($29.99/mo + 5%)
**Target:** New businesses doing $0-$10k/month
**Your Average Revenue:** $350-450/mo per customer
**Positioning:** "Start small, pay as you grow"

#### 2. Pro Commission ($99.99/mo + 1%)
**Target:** Growing businesses doing $10k-$50k/month
**Your Average Revenue:** $300-600/mo per customer
**Positioning:** "Lower commission, higher volume"

#### 3. Enterprise Flat Fee ($249/mo + 0%)
**Target:** Established businesses doing $50k+/month
**Your Average Revenue:** $249/mo per customer (predictable)
**Positioning:** "Unlimited sales, no commission, pure profit"

### Why This Works
- **Customers choose based on their GMV** (natural segmentation)
- **You make more money** at every tier than $249 flat fee
- **High-volume customers** get predictability with flat fee
- **Low-volume customers** get affordability with commission
