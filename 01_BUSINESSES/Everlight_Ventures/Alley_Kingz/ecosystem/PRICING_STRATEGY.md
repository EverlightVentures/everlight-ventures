## THE PRICING MODEL: VALUE BASE + (TOKEN COST x MARKUP) -- your per-token model, built in
You're right: the per-token cost belongs in the price, added on top. That's the model. Every item is priced:

```
  FINAL PRICE = VALUE BASE  +  (TOKEN COST x MARKUP)        then sanity-check vs the market ceiling
```
- **VALUE BASE** -- the rarity/market anchor for that tier (keeps it competitive -- what players expect to pay).
- **TOKEN COST** -- YOUR actual generation spend for THAT specific asset (Seedance $/token x tokens used). Tracked per project.
- **MARKUP** -- your multiple (e.g. 10x). This is your profit-per-token, added ON TOP of the base. Your call.
- **CEILING CHECK** -- if `TOKEN COST x MARKUP` would push the price above the top of its market tier, don't bury
  it in common rotation -- sell that one as a **LIMITED / founder / premium drop** where scarcity + hand-made art
  justify the premium. (This is the ONLY guardrail: a more-expensive-to-make item should be positioned as more premium.)

**This guarantees every item recovers its token cost x your markup, on top of a fair base -- exactly your model.**
And then you ALSO get a bonus on top: you pay the token cost ONCE but sell the asset to many players at ~$0
marginal cost, so after the markup is recovered on the early sales, every later sale is almost pure margin.
**Your markup is the floor; volume is the bonus. Both -- not either/or.** (That's the part I undersold before.)

**Worked example (a premium Seedance card):**
- VALUE BASE (Mythic tier) ~ 2,000 gems (~$16).
- TOKEN COST: ~100k tokens to generate ~ (say) $2.  MARKUP 10x  ->  token component = ~$20 = ~2,500 gems.
- **FINAL = 2,000 + 2,500 = ~4,500 gems (~$36)**, sold as a LIMITED Mythic (scarcity + hand-made art justify it).
- Sale #1 already recovers the token cost 10x PLUS the base value; every later sale is ~all margin.

## THE TWO-TIER COSMETIC MODEL (this is your Seedance idea, done right)
Tier cosmetics by HOW they were made -- this is exactly how you pass your higher Seedance cost to the buyer,
but through POSITIONING, not a literal per-token markup:

- **Standard tier (procedural / CSS-made, ~$0 to create):** buyable with **GOLD** (in-game grind) or cheap gems.
  These are the recolor skins, simple boards, emotes you already shipped. Keep them gold-priced -- they cost you nothing.
- **Premium tier (Seedance hand-generated art -- costs you real money to make):** **GEMS ONLY**, premium price.
  These are the marquee card skins, animated boards, hero alt-art. This is where your Seedance spend gets recovered
  -- and because gems = real money, only this tier touches revenue. Mark these "PREMIUM" / "Seedance" so the quality
  jump justifies the price.

## COVERING YOUR REAL COSTS (the part you actually asked about)
You have two cost buckets. Treat them differently:

**A) Per-asset creation cost (Seedance tokens per card/skin/map).** Amortize across that asset's sales.
- Break-even sales = (token $ cost of the asset) / (net $ per sale after ~30% store/processing).
- Rule of thumb to set: **every premium asset must clear its creation cost within ~5 sales, then it's margin.**
- If a skin costs you $3 to make and nets ~$3.50/sale, it breaks even on sale #1. Set the price so break-even is
  trivially low (1-5 sales) -- then you're safe even on low-volume items.

**B) Sunk platform/tool-build budget (the months you've invested).** This is fixed overhead -- do NOT load it onto
any single item. Amortize it across ALL revenue over a recovery window (e.g., 12 months):
- Monthly target = (total tool-build $ spent) / 12 + ongoing infra.
- Then pricing's job is just to clear that monthly target via volume. Cosmetics + pass + gem packs together.

## DON'T-UNDERCUT-YOURSELF RULES
- **Never price a premium item below its 5-sale break-even.** That's the floor.
- **Keep the $99 gem pack.** Whales are most of F2P revenue; the big pack also makes the $20 pack look reasonable (anchoring).
- **Use scarcity on Mythic/Seedance items** ("limited drop, gone in 48h") -- scarcity lets you charge top-tier and protects value.
- **Gold for cheap-to-make, Gems for costly-to-make.** Never let a real-money-cost asset be earnable for free.
- **Bundle to raise average spend**, and let the pass's "earn it back" loop pull the first purchase.

## THE CEILING
Mobile F2P cosmetics is a multi-billion market with a high ceiling; a small whale base drives most of it, and
$99 purchases are normal. You are not close to that ceiling. The risk is under-pricing premium art out of modesty,
not over-pricing. Price the premium Seedance tier confidently; keep the free/gold game generous so the audience
grows; let the whales fund it.
