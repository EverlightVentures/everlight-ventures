## 6. The Honest Call

Base case math says **$285,484 gross by month 12**. WORST case is $65,000. BEST case, with every assumption tilted favorably, comes in at **$851,532, still 10% short of the $947k target**.

Hits $947k iff ALL of the following are true simultaneously:
1. Wholesale closes 3+ deals/month at $15k+ from M6 onward (36 deals over 12 months, 0 today)
2. AI Consulting hits 4 builds/mo at $5k average AND grows retainer book to 13+ active clients by M12 (0 today)
3. SaaS books $4,800+/mo recurring by M12 (60+ clients combined, 0 today)
4. License reinstates inside 30 days, no compliance friction
5. Marquise commits 50 productive hours/week to revenue work, not infrastructure

**The honest read:** $947k in 12 months is not impossible, but it requires the BEST case to over-deliver by 11%. The realistic stretch goal at current 50hrs/wk solo capacity is $400k - $500k gross. To clear $947k, the system needs either (a) a VA hire by M3 to free 15 hours/week for sales, (b) one founding-customer SaaS contract above the modeled per-seat pricing, or (c) a wholesale deal-flow leverage event (JV or boomerang refer-in).
