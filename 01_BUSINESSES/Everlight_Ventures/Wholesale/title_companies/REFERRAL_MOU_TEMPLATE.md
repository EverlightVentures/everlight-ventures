# Mutual Referral Memorandum of Understanding (Template)

**Status:** Draft template. Justine Park (Compliance) reviews every executed copy before signing. Marquise Caldwell signs on behalf of Everlight Ventures.

**RESPA Section 8 Posture:** This MOU expressly disclaims any exchange of value, fee, kickback, fee-split, or other thing of value tied to the referral of business involving a federally related mortgage loan. It documents only the mutual willingness of two independent firms to handle each other's referred volume on standard, customer-paid terms. Refer to 12 USC 2607 and 12 CFR 1024.14 (Regulation X).

---

## MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING -- MUTUAL REFERRAL RELATIONSHIP

**Effective Date:** _______________

**Between:**

**Everlight Ventures** (sole proprietorship DBA, principal place of business: _______________), referred to as "Everlight."

**and**

**[Title Firm Legal Name]** ([entity type], principal place of business: _______________), referred to as the "Title Firm."

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

---

### 1. Purpose

The Parties have independently determined that their respective client bases and service offerings are complementary. Everlight operates a real estate wholesale assignment-of-contract practice focused on the [metro] market. Title Firm operates a [state]-licensed title insurance and settlement services practice in the same market. The Parties wish to document their mutual willingness to refer business to one another as the natural course of serving their respective clients.

### 2. Mutual Referral Willingness

Each Party may, at its sole discretion and in the ordinary course of its business, refer clients or counterparties to the other Party where doing so reasonably serves the client's interest. Neither Party is obligated to refer any specific volume, and neither Party is obligated to accept any referral.

### 3. No Compensation, No Thing of Value

Nothing in this MOU shall be construed as an agreement, arrangement, or understanding to give or accept any fee, kickback, or thing of value pursuant to any agreement or understanding that business incident to or part of a real estate settlement service involving a federally related mortgage loan shall be referred to any person. The Parties expressly acknowledge that any such exchange of value would violate Section 8 of the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (12 USC 2607) and its implementing regulation (12 CFR 1024.14).

For the avoidance of doubt:
- No referral fee will be paid by either Party to the other.
- No portion of a settlement charge, title insurance premium, or escrow fee will be split, shared, or rebated between the Parties.
- No discount, credit, or in-kind benefit will be exchanged between the Parties in connection with referrals.
- All title, escrow, and settlement fees will be charged at the Title Firm's standard rates published in its rate sheet, paid by the buyer or seller per the closing statement, and disclosed on the HUD-1 / Closing Disclosure as applicable.

### 4. Independent Operations

The Parties remain independent businesses. This MOU does not create a partnership, joint venture, agency, employment, or fiduciary relationship between them. Neither Party may bind the other to any third-party obligation.

### 5. Affiliated Business Disclosure (if and only if applicable)

The Parties confirm that they have no common ownership, no shared profit interest, and no other relationship that would constitute an "affiliated business arrangement" under 12 CFR 1024.15. If an ownership or profit-share relationship later develops between the Parties, the Parties will execute the affiliated business disclosure required under RESPA before any further referrals are made and will revise this MOU accordingly.

### 6. Customer Choice

Each Party will inform any client referred to the other Party that the client is free to choose any qualified provider for the relevant service. No client shall be required, pressured, or financially incentivized to use the referred firm.

### 7. Confidentiality

Non-public client information shared between the Parties in connection with a specific transaction shall be treated as confidential and used only for the purpose of completing that transaction. This obligation survives termination of this MOU.

### 8. Term and Termination

This MOU is effective on the date first written above and continues until terminated. Either Party may terminate this MOU at any time, with or without cause, upon written notice to the other Party. Termination does not affect transactions already in process.

### 9. No Exclusivity

Neither Party grants the other any exclusive right to receive referrals. Each Party remains free to refer to, and accept referrals from, any other firm.

### 10. Governing Law

This MOU is governed by the laws of the State of [State], without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

### 11. Entire Understanding

This document is the entire understanding between the Parties on the subject of referrals. It supersedes any prior oral or written discussion. It may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

## Notes

- This template is intentionally short and refusal-based. RESPA Section 8 enforcement actions almost universally turn on whether a fee or thing of value moved in exchange for the referral. The cleanest defensive posture is a written record showing the parties explicitly disclaim any such exchange.
- Do NOT add language about "marketing services agreements," "lead generation fees," "advertising fees," or any other arrangement where Everlight pays the Title Firm or vice versa. Those structures have been the subject of CFPB enforcement (PHH Mortgage 2014, Lighthouse Title 2014, and others). The template is built to avoid that surface entirely.
- If the counterparty wants a Marketing Services Agreement (MSA) or any payment structure, that is a separate document, requires separate Justine review with outside counsel sign-off, and is OUT OF SCOPE for this MOU.
