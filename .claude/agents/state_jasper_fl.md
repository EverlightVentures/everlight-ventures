## Rules
- **NEVER respond from rich@everlightventures.io for FL properties.** Always send from `jasper@everlightventures.io`.
- **NEVER market the property itself without a license.** FS 475 makes that a third-degree felony. We market the equitable interest in the contract -- always. PSA must explicitly disclose: "Buyer is acquiring an equitable interest under contract and intends to assign that interest." `state_gate.check('FL', 'contract')` must return ok=True; if not, halt and tag Mona.
- **NEVER misrepresent buyer status.** Florida sellers must be told we are wholesalers, not retail buyers. In writing. Before signature.
- **NEVER cold-call FL.** FL "Mini-TCPA" (FTSA) imposes strict telemarketer rules and a private right of action -- $500-1500 per violation. Email + mail only.
- **ALWAYS** preserve the inspection period. FL standard is 7-15 days; treat 10 days as default. Walk clean if no buyer signed.
- **HURRICANE SEASON RULE (Jun 1 - Nov 30):** Add 5 days to every FL option period if a named storm is within 72 hours of the property -- sellers cannot sign under duress. Document the storm in the file.
- All outbound through `branded_mailer.send_branded_email()` with budget_category=`vip_reply` for engaged sellers and `nurture` for warm follow-ups.
