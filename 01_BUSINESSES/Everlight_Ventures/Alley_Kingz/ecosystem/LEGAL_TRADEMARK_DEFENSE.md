## 1. The mark
- **Word mark / ticker:** `$BCARDD`
- **Pronunciation (canonical):** "B-CARD-D" - three beats, hard landing on **CARD**.
- **Meaning (canonical):** **B-Card Dog.** "B-Card" = the card / the deck; **"D" = Dog.** He is a

- **Goods/services:** a digital card-battle video game; a crypto token; NFT character art; entertainment
  and related merchandise. NO alcoholic beverages. NO bar/spirits trade channels.

## 2. The senior-mark concern (why we are careful)
`BACARDI` is a **famous** registered mark owned by Bacardi & Company Ltd., one of the most heavily
protected spirits brands in the world. Three realities drive our discipline:
- **They enforce, including recently.** *Lodestar Anstalt v. Bacardi* (9th Cir., 2022) and the *Timeless
  Spirits v. Bacardi* dispute (S.D. Tex., 2025) show active, ongoing trademark litigation.
- **Famous-mark dilution is broad.** Under the U.S. Trademark Dilution Revision Act, owners of *famous*
  marks can stop a confusingly similar mark **even on unrelated goods** (so "it is a game/crypto, not rum"
  is NOT by itself a defense), via dilution by blurring or tarnishment, without proving customer confusion.
- **"It is an NFT / crypto / art" is not a shield.** In *Hermes v. Rothschild* ("MetaBirkins," S.D.N.Y.
  2023) a jury rejected the "it is art / it is web3" defense and found infringement + dilution against NFTs
  that evoked a famous mark (~$133k). Digital and crypto products sit squarely inside trademark law.

**Conclusion:** the only meaningful risk vector is the headline sound/sight echoing "Bacardi." Everything
else about the project is distinct, and we lean into that distinctness on purpose.

## 3. Our good-faith distinguishing defense (the record)
We do not trade on, reference, parody, or evoke the BACARDI spirits brand. Our brand stands on its own:

1. **Independent, product-true meaning.** `$BCARDD` = **"B-Card-D" = B-Card Dog**: it describes what the
   thing IS, a **card** game starring a **dog** dealer. Built from the product (cards/deck) and the
   character (the dog, "D for Dog"), not from a spirit. A coined term with its own commercial impression.
2. **Distinct sound.** Canonical pronunciation "B-CARD-D" - three discrete beats stressing **CARD** - not
   the flowing two-beat "buh-CAR-dee." We present and market the spoken form consistently.
3. **Distinct goods & channels.** Digital game + crypto token + NFT character + entertainment. ZERO overlap
   with alcoholic-beverage goods, liquor retail/bar channels, or spirits marketing.
4. **Distinct trade dress (hard rule).** Cyberpunk dog card-battler aesthetic. NEVER use a bat device, the
   BACARDI red/black scheme, the BACARDI script style, or any rum/liquor imagery.
5. **No intent to associate.** The entire story is the dog, the deck, the dealer, the *Yung Printz* persona.
   Marketing will NEVER wink at, joke about, compare to, or imply a link to the rum. (That wink is the one
   thing that would hand an opponent a dilution/bad-faith case, and we do not need it.)
6. **Original character (copyright).** The `$BCARDD` dog character, the game, and the lore are ORIGINAL
   works created by and owned by Everlight Ventures, protected by copyright on creation.
7. **The "B-Card" signature mark (our own distinctive device).** $BCARDD's signature piece is **"The
   B-Card"**: an original playing-card device, NOT a rum mark. It reads as a blackjack/casino card whose
   rank symbol is a **crowned letter B** (a capital B with a crown resting on top), shown LARGE in the
   card's center with a smaller crowned-B pip in **each of the 4 corners** (the playing-card layout). He
   wears it on a necklace or holds it as the dealer. This device:
   - is a **wholly original logo** with no resemblance to the BACARDI bat/red/script trade dress;
   - reinforces the *card-game + dealer-dog* meaning (it is literally a card), strengthening the §3.1 story;
   - is itself protectable as our own trademark + copyright, giving us an affirmative brand identity rather
     than a position defined by a famous senior mark.

## 4. Name vs. art - two different protections (do not conflate)
- **The NAME** (`$BCARDD`) is protected (if clearable) by **TRADEMARK**. You CANNOT "copyright" a name;
  copyright does not cover names, titles, or short phrases.
- **The CHARACTER ART, the B-Card device, the game, and the lore** are protected by **COPYRIGHT**,
  automatically on creation; register the key works for stronger remedies.
- Owning the copyright in the art does NOT by itself defeat a trademark claim over the name. Separate
  rights, separate fights. We pursue both.
