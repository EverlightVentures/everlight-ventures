# Alley Kingz -- STORY MODE: "THE CROWN BLOODLINE"
*A multi-generation dog-clan saga. Warrior Cats structure x dog-lover psychology x the CROWN CLIMB spine. Built to COMPLETE THE LOOP (story <-> rank <-> 2v2 <-> district/clan).*
Research-backed 2026-06-25. Sources at bottom.

## 0. The feeling we are designing for (decided FIRST, per story-mode best practice)
"A scrappy nobody claws up from a lone stray to a legend who rules the streets -- and leaves a bloodline behind." Underdog rise + loyalty + legacy. Every art, sound, and mechanic choice serves THAT feeling.

## 1. The three research pillars
- **What makes story mode great:** fuse story INTO gameplay (missions/battles ARE the story, not cutscenes bolted on); deep character investment; real player agency/choice; pacing toward a pre-decided emotion; sound carries the emotional beats.
- **Why dog-lovers love dogs:** the oxytocin look/touch bond; the dual identity (dependent pup + loyal protector); unwavering loyalty; and the RESCUE / REDEMPTION arc above all. 5 science-backed dog personality types: Excitable/Hyperattached, Anxious/Fearful, Aloof/Predatory, Reactive/Assertive, Calm/Agreeable.
- **Warrior Cats backbone:** strict clan hierarchy (kit -> apprentice -> warrior -> deputy -> leader "-star"); ancestor spirits (StarClan) guiding via prophecy/dream; multi-generation arcs (each arc a new protagonist/generation); the former-outsider-rises-to-leader spine.

## 2. THE MAPPING that makes it click (factions = dog souls)
You START as the **Anxious/Fearful lone STRAY** -- the rescue dog the player nurtures (instant dog-lover bond + the redemption arc). Then you earn into a CLAN whose temperament IS a dog personality type:
- **Zoomie Syndicate** (Zoom) = Excitable / Hyperattached -- ride-or-die speed pack
- **Leashbreak Tactix** (Phantom) = Aloof / Predatory -- ghost ambushers
- **Boneguard Crew** (Bone) = Calm / Agreeable -- loyal, immovable grit
- **K9 Circuitry** (Crowned/Volt) = Reactive / Assertive -- sharp, dominant tech-hounds
The factions stop being color teams and become DOG SOULS -- picking one feels like picking your dog's heart.

## 3. The clan hierarchy = the rank ladder (REUSE the karma tiers, do not rebuild)
Stray -> Pup (apprentice) -> Runner -> Warrior -> Enforcer -> Right Paw (deputy) -> KING / QUEEN OF THE BLOCK ("-star"). This rides the existing karma division ladder + the CROWN CLIMB 7 stages.

## 4. The 3-generation saga (the operator's Warriors idea, made concrete)
- **GEN I -- "The Stray's Rise":** lone stray -> clan King. (= the CROWN CLIMB 7 stages: meet the Fixer, pick faction, prove yourself, crew wars, seasonal supremacy, challenge the King, reign.)
- **GEN II -- "The Bloodline":** you raise/choose a SUCCESSOR pup (from the dogs you collected/bred), play AS them, defend the throne while rivals you spared rise against you.
- **GEN III -- "The Legend Wars":** the clans your bloodline shaped collide in an era-defining city war; choices from Gen I-II change the map + who stands with you.
- **Ancestor layer ("the Old Pack" = our StarClan):** the OG fallen King + legendary dogs appear in dream-visions to deliver prophecy/objectives + foreshadow. Ties into the existing codex/lore. This is HOW story objectives are delivered (a dream from a dead legend > a quest-giver popup).

## 5. COMPLETING THE LOOP (the core ask: one world, four faces, all feeding each other)
- **STORY (PvE clan saga)** -- the spine. Missions/buildings/encounters are CLAN DUTIES. Pays out rank + territory + bloodline.
- **RANK (PvP ladder, 1v1 tower battle)** -- proving your clan's strength. Your ladder rank = your standing IN the saga; story chapters GATE behind climbing.
- **2v2 (tower battle duos)** -- clan-mate pack hunts; co-op missions live here.
- **DISTRICT / CLAN (base + territory, Clash-of-Clans economy + guarding)** -- your clan's turf. Raids = clan wars; guarding = defending the bloodline's land; seasons = which clan rules the city (the Supremacy ERA).
THE GLUE: every mode pays into ONE progression (rank + karma + territory + bloodline). Win a rank match -> clan gains territory pressure -> unlocks the next story chapter -> which sends you to guard/raid a district -> which feeds the season's clan-supremacy -> which crowns a generation. **The dog you raise in story is the dog you climb rank with is the dog that guards your district.** No mode is a side toy.

## 6. How it ships (engine-free, on what already exists)
- `window.AKStory` (the spine, already specced in CROWN CLIMB) drives the generational arc + dream-vision objective delivery.
- Rank/karma tiers = clan ranks (reuse). Districts/seasons = clan territories/eras (reuse). Missions = clan duties (reuse the mission loop already built). Tower battle = clan duels/wars (FROZEN engine untouched -- story only FRAMES the matches). Collection/breeding = the bloodline (reuse the roster).
- New work is mostly NARRATIVE DATA + the dream-vision delivery UI + the generational gates -- not new engines.

## 7. LOCKED creative direction (operator decided 2026-06-25)
1. **Generational mechanic = HYBRID:** Gen I (lone stray -> King, the full 7-stage CROWN CLIMB) is the CORE, complete-on-its-own story. Passing the torch to a successor pup (Gen II/III bloodline) is an OPTIONAL path that UNLOCKS after you take the crown. Ships sooner, grows into the full saga.
2. **Tone = GRITTY GANGLAND:** streets, turf, betrayal, hard edges -- GTA-of-dogs, NOT cuddly. The rescue-stray opening still hooks the dog-lover, but the world is hard: loyalty is earned, rivals stab backs, the crown is taken not given. Copy is street-tough.
3. **Clan naming = HYBRID:** keep the built crew names (Zoomie Syndicate, Leashbreak Tactix, Boneguard Crew, K9 Circuitry) but REFER to them as clans in the saga -- "the Boneguard clan," "Zoomie territory." No rebranding work; stronger Warriors feel.
