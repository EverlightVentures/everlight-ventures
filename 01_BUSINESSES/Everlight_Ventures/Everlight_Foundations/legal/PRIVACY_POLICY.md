## 1. Introduction

Everlight Logistics LLC ("Everlight," "we," "us," or "our") operates everlightventures.io and all associated products and services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and what rights you have regarding your data.

This policy applies to all users of our website, games, SaaS products, and digital storefronts. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use our services.

We are a California limited liability company and comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account information** -- Name, email address, display name, and date of birth (for age verification)
- **Payment information** -- Processed by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers, CVVs, or full card details on our servers. We receive your email address and a transaction reference from Stripe.
- **Contact form submissions** -- Name, email, company name, phone number, and message content when you submit a quote request or support inquiry
- **Email list signups** -- Email address when you subscribe to updates, waitlists, or newsletters
- **Leaderboard entries** -- Display name (3-character initials) and game scores

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data** -- Pages visited, features used, time spent on pages, click patterns, and navigation paths
- **Device information** -- Browser type and version, operating system, screen resolution, and device type
- **Game data** -- Game scores, session length, games played, Virtual Currency balances, purchase history within games, and gameplay statistics
- **Log data** -- IP address, access times, referring URLs, and error logs
- **Authentication tokens** -- Supabase session tokens stored in cookies and localStorage for login persistence

### 2.3 Information From Third Parties

- **Stripe** -- Transaction confirmation, payment status, and customer email
- **Google AdSense** -- Advertising cookies and identifiers (see Section 7)

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

- **Providing services** -- Processing purchases, delivering digital goods, managing subscriptions, running game sessions, and maintaining your account
- **Payment processing** -- Completing transactions and sending purchase confirmations

- **Legal compliance** -- Meeting our obligations under applicable laws and regulations

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. Specific retention periods:

- **Account data** -- Retained until you request deletion or your account is terminated
- **Purchase records** -- Retained for 7 years for tax and accounting compliance
- **Game data** -- Retained for the life of your account
- **Support correspondence** -- Retained for 3 years after resolution
- **Email list data** -- Retained until you unsubscribe
- **Server logs** -- Retained for 90 days

After the retention period, data is deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage

### 6.1 What We Use

- **Supabase authentication tokens** -- Stored in cookies and localStorage to keep you logged in. These are functional and required for the service to work.
- **Game session data** -- Stored in localStorage to track free game usage, arcade session state, and age verification status.
- **Preference cookies** -- Stored in localStorage to remember your settings (e.g., sound mute state).

### 6.2 Third-Party Cookies

- **Google AdSense** -- May place advertising cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com).
- **Stripe** -- May use cookies for fraud detection during payment processing.

### 6.3 Managing Cookies

You can clear cookies and localStorage through your browser settings. Note that clearing authentication cookies will log you out, and clearing localStorage may reset your free game credits and arcade session data.

## 7. Your Rights Under CCPA/CPRA

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right to Know

You may request that we disclose:
- The categories of personal information we have collected about you
- The specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
- The categories of sources from which we collected your information
- The business purposes for collecting your information
- The categories of third parties with whom we share your information

### 7.2 Right to Delete

You may request that we delete your personal information. We will comply unless the information is needed for completing a transaction, security purposes, legal compliance, or other purposes permitted by law.

### 7.3 Right to Correct

You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information.

### 7.4 Right to Opt Out of Sale

We do not sell personal information, so this right does not currently apply. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

### 7.5 Right to Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights. You will not receive different pricing, a different quality of service, or be denied service for making a privacy request.

### 7.6 How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit requests to: **support@everlightventures.io**

Include "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity before processing the request. We will respond within 45 days. If we need additional time, we will notify you of the extension and the reason.

You may also designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf. The agent must provide written authorization signed by you.

## 8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Everlight Arcade requires users to be 18 or older, verified through a date-of-birth entry.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately at support@everlightventures.io.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. These include:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
- Supabase row-level security policies for data access control
- Stripe PCI-DSS compliance for payment data
- Access controls limiting employee and system access to personal data
- Regular review of data handling practices

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you promptly if a breach affecting your data occurs.
