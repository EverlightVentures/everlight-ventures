Chapter 1: The Mysterious Note

🎨 Illustration — Sam & Robo find a mysterious note on the front porch

Scene: Bright sunny morning. Sam holds a mysterious note while Robo beeps beside him on the porch. B&W coloring version on left page, full color on right.

Images: 3_1_bw.jpg / 3_1_color.jpg

Sam & Robo woke up to a bright and sunny day, ready for a new adventure. They remembered their past adventures and the valuable lessons they had learned about phonics, healthy habits, and helping others. Today felt like another day to put those lessons to good use.

As they stepped outside, they noticed a strange note on their front porch. Sam picked it up and saw that it was filled with words using blends and digraphs, along with some familiar sight words they had learned before.

"Look, Robo! A mystery note!" Sam exclaimed.

Robo beeped with excitement. "Let's decode it, Sam! We'll use our new blends and digraphs skills and review what we learned before."

The note read: "Find the blue plant by the stream and check the chest for the next clue."

"Blends like 'bl' in blue and 'st' in stream, and digraphs like 'ch' in check and 'sh' in chest," Robo pointed out.

"Wow! This is going to be fun!" Sam said. They repeated the blends and digraphs together: "bl, st, ch, sh."

Using their new phonics skills, they decoded the note and set off to find the blue plant by the stream. As they walked, they practiced sight words like "what," "where," and "which," and reviewed the ones they had learned before like "the," "and," "can."

"What will we find by the stream?" Sam wondered aloud.

"Where could the chest be hidden?" Robo asked.

On their way, they bumped into their old friends, Lily and the farmer. Sam shared the mystery with them, and they all decided to help.

Lily said, "I remember when we grew that sunflower plant together. We can use our gardening skills to help find the plant."

The farmer added, "And remember, hard work pays off. Let's find that blue plant and solve this mystery together!"

✽ ✽ ✽
🎨 Illustration — Finding the blue plant and chest by the stream

Scene: Sam & Robo at a sparkling stream, discovering a glowing blue plant and a small treasure chest. Lily and the farmer watch from behind.

Images: 3_2_bw.jpg / 3_2_color.jpg

As they reached the stream, they saw the blue plant and a small chest beside it. Sam opened the chest and found another note inside.

"This is just the beginning, Robo. Let's keep going and solve the mystery!" Sam said with determination.

They felt proud of their new skills and the lessons they had learned. They knew that with teamwork, hard work, and their phonics skills, they could solve any mystery.

Chapter 2: The Missing Ring

🎨 Illustration — Sam & Robo searching behind trees

Scene: Sam & Robo look behind small trees, reading clue notes. B&W coloring version on left page, full color on right.

Images: 3_33_bw.jpg / 3_33_color.jpg

With the new clue in hand, Sam & Robo set off to solve the first mystery. The note from the chest read: "Look behind the small tree to find the missing ring."

"Look, there's another blend — 'sm' in small," Robo pointed out.

Sam nodded. "And we already know 'the' and 'find' from our sight words. Let's go!"

They walked towards the small tree, repeating the blends and digraphs as they went: "sm, bl, st, ch, sh." Along the way, they encountered various objects and scenarios that required their reading and decoding skills.

They reached the tree and looked behind it, but there was nothing there. Sam scratched his head. "Hmm, maybe we need to read the clue again."

Robo beeped and read aloud, "Look behind the small tree to find the missing ring."

They looked around and noticed several small trees nearby. "We need to check each one," Sam suggested.

As they searched, they found other notes hidden along the way, each with blends and digraphs. One note read: "The ring is near the stream by the big rock."

Chapter 3: Garden Adventure

🎨 Illustration — Planting sunflower seeds in Lily's garden

Scene: Sam, Robo, and Lily in a sunny garden, planting sunflower seeds. Soil, water, and gardening tools visible.

Images: 3_5_bw.jpg / 3_5_color.jpg

The next clue led Sam & Robo to their friend Lily's garden. "We need to help Lily with her garden to solve the mystery," Sam said.

Lily greeted them with a smile. "Thank you for helping! We need to grow a sunflower plant to find the next clue."

Robo beeped, "Let's use our knowledge of plants from our last adventure!"

They gathered seeds, soil, and water. "First, we plant the seeds in the soil," Sam explained. They patted the soil gently and made sure the seeds were well-covered.

"Next, we water the soil to help the seeds grow," Robo added. They took turns watering the soil and placed the pot in a sunny spot.

"Sunflower plants need plenty of sunlight and water to grow strong," Lily reminded them.

As they waited for the plant to grow, Robo suggested, "Let's learn some simple coding to unlock the digital clue while we wait."

Sam agreed. They used a tablet to follow simple coding instructions, which helped them unlock a digital clue: "The next clue is hidden under the big oak tree."

They all cheered when they saw the sunflower sprout. "We did it!" Sam said.

"Great teamwork!" Lily praised.

The Learning Corner

Blends and Digraphs: The Next Step in Reading

A blend is when two consonant sounds work together, and you can hear BOTH sounds:

  • bl-: blue, blend, block
  • st-: stream, strong, step
  • sm-: small, smile, smart

A digraph is when two letters make ONE brand-new sound:

  • ch-: chest, check, cheered
  • sh-: shiny, share, shell

Sight Words: what, where, which, find (plus review: the, and, can)

Activity: Blend & Digraph Treasure Hunt

Go on a hunt around your home or classroom! Find objects that start with: bl-, st-, sm-, ch-, sh-. Write down or draw each thing you find!

Bonus: Write a mystery note for a friend using blends and digraphs!
