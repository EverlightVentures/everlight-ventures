The Living Runner + The Minigame Floor

A concrete build plan for (1) the SIM-LIFE care building that finally lets the player satisfy the needs the engine already tracks, and (2) turning the MOBA / Streets / Arcade from playable-but-bare into loop-connected modes. Grounded in systems/needs.js, systems/modes.js, systems/arcade.js and bible §10.
One-line diagnosis. The metrics engine is real and guarded (needs.js), and the minigames are real playable MVPs (modes.js, arcade.js). What is missing is the place: there is no walk-in building to feed / rest / cheer the runner, the hunger and energy "ad" bubbles point at a BARN that does not exist and a Kennel that does not rest you, and the modes render on flat near-black canvas with rewards that never touch missions or story. Nothing here needs a rebuild — it needs wiring.

The 3-move minigame plan (in priority order)

Move 1 — Kill the black backdrop P1

Every mode fills a flat dark rect (modes.js:272/1089/1675). Draw the active district's hub art behind the play layer at low alpha before the mechanics draw — the same districtBg images the hub already decodes, or a single reused the_strip_bg.png. One drawImage per mode's render head.

Effort: S. Cost: free (reuse existing hub art). Instantly reads as "a place," not a test harness.

Move 2 — Connect the rewards to the loop P1

Right now MOBA/gulag/arcade pay soft currency in isolation. Route each result through the systems that already listen: fire AK_NEEDS.boostMorale('arcade'/'crewwin') on a win (arcade already does; MOBA/gulag/defense do not), report the win to missions/quests via the existing report* rail (missions.js:308), and let a mode win count toward Alley Pass XP. A win should move the runner, the pass, and a daily duty — not just a coin counter.

Effort: S-M. Cost: free.

Move 3 — Play-again + reward variety P2

Mode onClose just banners the result (modes.js:977). Add a REMATCH button on every mode's end (shares the raid play-again work, Part 3). Give arcade games a high-score that unlocks a cosmetic or a story page (feed the 106-book lake, below).

Effort: M. Cost: free.

Later — MOBA depth P2

The bible promises 5v5/draft/jungle/Lord; today's is a 1v1 duel (already marked "future research," modes.js:45-49). Not a bug — a scope note. Ship backdrop+rewards first; depth is a Wave, not a fix.

Part 3 · Cross-System Wiring Map

Live wires (working)

produce → feed() → hunger hunger → RAID dmg (RAID.ndm, index.html:1704) needs → chronicles mood ring + state-locked choices morale ← arcade/crew-win/chat missions → produce + Alley Pass XP raid → loot / retaliation / producer damage

Dead / partial wires (built, not consumed)

  • adSuggestion → BARN : phantom target, dead-end banner (fixed by Part 1).
  • adSuggestion → KENNEL "rest" : Kennel never refills stamina; refillStamina() API unused (fixed by Part 1 REST affordance).
  • needs.combatMods dmgMult in the TOWER : game.html:5656 documents it CANNOT apply — engine.js is frozen and clamps unit dmg to 1.0-1.10, so the starving debuff is redirected to a -20% match-gold penalty. The damage debuff only truly lands in the RAID loop. Working-as-designed but worth knowing: the tower and the raid punish starvation differently.
  • 106 story books → thin buff : chronicles reads them for mood + a next-battle fx (rage/tactical/easter, consumed game.html:5658). They are lore + a small buff, but they do NOT unlock modes/cards/areas. A large content lake driving one small wire — candidate to gate arcade high-scores or DEN cosmetics onto story progress.
  • MOBA/gulag/arcade rewards : isolated soft-currency islands (fixed by Part 2, Move 2).
  • THE_UNDERCITY + THE_OVERLOOK : both zones locked:true with barrier labels and no unlock path (index.html:646/674); showBarrier just says "sealed (soon)." Two districts of dead content.
