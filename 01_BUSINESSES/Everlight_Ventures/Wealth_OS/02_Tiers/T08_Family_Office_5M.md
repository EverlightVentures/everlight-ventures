# T8 -- Family Office Structure ($5M Net Worth)
## The Institutional Tier

**Trigger:** Net worth $5M+ OR investment complexity requiring dedicated team.

---

## The 5 T8 Moves

### 1. Family Office Management Company (Lender Mgmt Structure)

**Reference case:** Lender Management LLC v. Commissioner (T.C. Memo 2017-246)

**The structure:**
- Form a Management Co (LLC)
- Management Co employs 1-2 people (you and/or family member or hired professional)
- Management Co provides investment management services to family entities, trusts, individual family members
- Management Co charges fees for services
- Family entities/individuals/trusts deduct fees as Sec 162 trade or business expenses
- Without this structure, those investment management expenses are NON-DEDUCTIBLE post-TCJA (Sec 212)

**Tax effect:** $50-200k/yr of investment expenses become deductible. Net savings ~$20-75k/yr.

**Threshold for cost-effectiveness:** Generally $25-50M+ AUM. At T8 ($5M) it's MARGINAL but planning structure ahead of T9 ($10M+) makes sense.

### 2. Premium-Financed Life Insurance (Larger Scale)

**The play:**
- Bank loans premium for large permanent life policy
- Policy cash value secures the loan
- You pay interest only (or accrue interest)
- Death benefit pays off loan + remainder to ILIT for heirs

**Sizing:** $10-50M death benefit policy at T8.

**Use cases:**
- Estate liquidity (pay estate tax without forced asset sales)
- Generational wealth transfer (death benefit to ILIT bypasses estate tax)
- Asset diversification (insurance-wrapped alternatives)

### 3. Family Bank Activation

**Mechanics:**
- Holdco or Trust acts as the "Family Bank"
- Lends to family members at AFR rates (currently ~4.5%)
- Real promissory notes, real interest, real payment schedules
- Borrower can deduct interest if for qualified use (mortgage, business, education)

**Use cases:**
- First-business loans to adult heirs
- First-home down payment loans
- Education loans (cheaper than student loans)
- Bridge loans during family member's transition events

**Why:** Wealth recycles within family rather than paying external interest. Family financial education accelerated through real loan stewardship.

### 4. Charitable Vehicle Initialization

If charitable intent matters (per PRIORITIES.md):

**Donor-Advised Fund (DAF):**
- Easy entry point
- Immediate full deduction at funding
- Investments grow tax-free inside DAF
- Grant to charities over time
- Providers: Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard Charitable, NPT (community foundations)

**Private Foundation (if $5M+ charitable intent):**
- More control but more complex
- 5% annual distribution requirement
- Operating expenses count toward 5%
- Family governance opportunity

### 5. Comprehensive Family Constitution (Draft Phase)

Begin formal family governance:
- Mission statement (what does this wealth EXIST for?)
- Decision-making structure
- Distribution philosophy
- Education requirements for heirs
- Conflict resolution
- Trustee removal procedures
- Charitable mandate

Engage family wealth consultant ($500-1,500/hr) to facilitate.
