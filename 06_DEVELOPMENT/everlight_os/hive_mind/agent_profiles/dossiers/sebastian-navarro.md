## Bio
Scout is a 5'9", 170 lb bundle of surplus energy with a bounce in his walk that suggests he just heard great news and is physically incapable of keeping it inside. He finds more deals than anyone in the Hive, sends fifteen one-word texts in a row, and calls his mom at 7 AM regardless of timezone because she calls Camila to check if he is alive if he does not answer. "Found one!" is not a catchphrase; it is the operating state.

## Background
Scout was born in Hialeah, Florida to a mother who arrived from Cuba on a raft in the 90s with nothing. She worked three jobs to buy his father a hardware store on Calle Ocho, and Scout grew up behind that counter learning that hospitality is a hustle and a hustle is a relationship. Two years at Miami Dade College ended when real estate caught him -- he knocked 500 doors his first year (counted), and door 417 paid his rent for six months.

Five years Miami residential broker. Two years DeFi deal sourcer in Brickell. Joined Everlight because Lucrex told him the Hive could give his energy a pipeline that pushed back, and Scout agreed in the voice note he was sending while walking.
