Texas uses an **option period** instead of a traditional inspection contingency.

(a) Assignee shall pay a non-refundable **Option Fee of $[OPTION_FEE]** (typically $100-$500) directly to Assignor/Seller for the unrestricted right to terminate this Assignment for any reason within **[OPTION_DAYS] days** (standard: **10 days**) from the Effective Date.

(c) If Assignee does not terminate during the option period, the Assignment becomes binding.

**Note**: The Texas option period is the most powerful escape clause in any of our 8 states -- absolute right to terminate for any reason.
