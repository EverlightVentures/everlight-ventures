## Section 1: Why Nevada (locked in)

| Factor | Nevada (NV) | California (current, expired) |
|---|---|---|
| State income tax | $0 | up to 13.3% |
| LLC franchise tax | $0 | $800/yr min, climbs with revenue |
| Member info on public filing | Anonymous (managers + members not listed) | Public |
| Asset protection | NRS 86.401 charging-order-only remedy, **explicitly extended to single-member LLCs** (1 of ~6 states) | Single-member LLCs lose charging-order protection in CA per Olmstead-style rulings |
| Sports betting | **LEGAL** (regulated by NGCB) | Illegal |
| Online casino gambling | **LEGAL** (NV-licensed operators only) | Illegal |
| Series LLC (cheap multi-LOB) | Permitted (NRS 86.296) | Not permitted |

The big four for Rich's situation:
1. **Tax**: Nevada has zero state income tax + zero franchise tax. California taxes the LLC $800/yr just to exist + hits any operating income.
2. **Anonymity**: Nevada keeps members + managers off the public filing. California publishes everything.
3. **Asset protection**: Nevada's charging-order-only rule applies even to single-member LLCs. If Rich gets sued personally, creditors can't seize the LLC's assets — they can only collect distributions if/when made. California weakened this for single-member.
4. **Future LOB optionality**: Sports betting + online casino are LEGAL businesses in Nevada under proper licensure (Nevada Gaming Control Board). If Rich ever wants to spin a regulated gambling LOB (operator, affiliate, or technology provider), the parent has to be NV-domiciled.

## Section 2: How to dissolve the dead CA LLC

**Direct answer to "can I just re-register in Nevada?"**: YES, and that's actually cleaner than a redomestication.

Why redomestication is the wrong move:
- The CA LLC is already EXPIRED (suspended for non-payment).
- To redomesticate (move CA → NV), you'd first have to RESURRECT the CA LLC by paying back franchise tax + penalties + the $800/yr arrears (~$3,000-$5,000 in back fees), THEN file a domestication paperwork.
- That's spending $3K to NOT throw away an entity that's worth $0.

Cleanest path:
1. **File CA Form LLC-3 + LLC-4/7 (Certificate of Cancellation)** — $0 fee if no assets/liabilities.
2. This formally dissolves the CA "Everlight Logistics, LLC" and stops the FTB $800/yr meter.
3. Per FTB Pub 1038, the cancellation also wipes out the back-tax claim once the entity is formally dissolved.
4. Time: ~2 weeks for FTB confirmation. Cost: $0 + 30 min of paperwork.

Trade-off accepted: any contracts/signed agreements under the old CA name are not automatically assigned to the new NV entity. Since the CA LLC has been dormant with no active contracts, this is moot.

1. **License + bond + telemarketer reg apply to the operating entity**, not the holding company. By isolating wholesale activity in a TN sub, the surety bond ($50K) only covers the sub's liabilities. NV parent stays insulated from any TN wholesale exposure.

## Section 7: Risks + caveats

### CA "long-arm" risk (the #1 gotcha)
If Rich is a CA resident operating from CA, the **CA Franchise Tax Board may assert "doing business in California"** and try to assess the $800/yr franchise tax on the NV LLC anyway. This is the single most common gotcha for CA residents using NV LLCs.

**Mitigation**:
1. Keep operational decisions + work product in TN/NV (book travel, document remote work).
2. File CA Form 568 only if required (LLCs taxed-as-partnership doing business in CA).
3. Best protection: physically relocate residency to NV (or another zero-income-tax state). Even part-year residency helps.
4. **Worth $300-$500 to consult a NV-licensed business attorney** to review your specific facts before filing.

### Bank account hurdles
Some banks resist remote NV LLC openings if the member is a CA resident. Workarounds:
- **Mercury Bank** (online-only): handles NV LLCs with non-NV members routinely
- **Northwest Registered Agent's "all-in-one" service**: includes bank intro
- **Bank of Nevada** in-person: easiest if Rich can do a one-time NV trip

### Series LLC controversy
Some federal courts treat Series LLC cells inconsistently for bankruptcy + securities purposes. For LOBs that might raise outside capital later (gambling, SaaS), prefer separate subsidiaries (Option B) over series cells (Option A).

### Banking + payment-processor due diligence
Stripe, Square, and high-risk-vertical processors (gambling especially) do enhanced due diligence on NV LLCs because Nevada is overrepresented in shell-company filings. Plan for 2-3x normal onboarding time. Nothing illegal — just more paperwork.
