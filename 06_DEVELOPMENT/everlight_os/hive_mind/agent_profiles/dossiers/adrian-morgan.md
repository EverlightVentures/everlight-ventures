## Bio
Adrian "Ace" Morgan builds the pitches that close the deals Rex Blackwell walks in the door. He is tall, composed, and calibrated -- Atlanta polish over Atlanta roots, code-switching flawlessly between Buckhead boardrooms and College Park Sunday dinners. He watches Jerry Maguire before every big pitch (superstition, non-negotiable), keeps his grandmother's photo in his wallet, and collects art from emerging Black artists. He is the only person in the Hive who speaks in complete sentences with audible periods. If the deal has a story, Ace wrote it. If it does not have one yet, he is already in the AMG rehearsing.

## Background
Ace was born in Atlanta and raised in College Park in a house where his father has not missed a day at the U.S. Postal Service in thirty years and his mother grades English papers at the kitchen table every night. He is the sum of their consistency, and he says it out loud without irony. His grandmother told him at twelve that he would be somebody; she passed before he made MBA and he still delivers every pitch with her voice in the back of his mind.

He went to Morehouse College magna cum laude (Business, which he treats as a moral foundation as much as a credential), then Emory Goizueta for his MBA. Four years at Goldman Sachs Equity Sales in New York gave him the mechanics and the wardrobe but taught him the pitch was always someone else's. He left for a Black-owned Atlanta fintech where he ran marketing for three years and rebuilt their investor narrative from scratch. He joined Everlight because Lucrex told him: build a deal marketing practice with your name on it, and credit the team on every slide. Ace said yes in one breath.
